## What's in the PDF

A **23-point migration checklist** for the **November 2026 pacs.008 structured-address deadline** — distilled from EPAA working-group output, SWIFT CBPR+ guidance and the 308-bank RedCompass survey.

The checklist covers:

- **Inventory** — auditing your outbound pacs.008 traffic for unstructured-address residue
- **Mapping rules** — decomposing free-text addresses into ISO 20022 `PostalAddress` slots (TwnNm, PostCode, Ctry, BldgNb, StrtNm, Dept, SubDept)
- **Generator updates** — patching your pacs.008 templating layer so every new message emits structured fields by default
- **Receiver fallback** — keeping legacy unstructured handling alive for non-migrated counterparties
- **End-to-end test harness** — scheme-specific test cases for SWIFT, SEPA, CHAPS, T2
- **Cutover signalling** — coordinating with your scheme manager on the activation date
- **KPI reporting** — bytes-on-the-wire of structured-vs-unstructured per FI, week over week

Use it as a project tracker through cutover.
