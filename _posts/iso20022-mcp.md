## Let your AI agent make real bank payments.

Nine open MCP servers that turn plain language into validated ISO 20022 bank messages: initiate, settle, reconcile and resolve. Vendor-neutral, runs anywhere, installed in one line. Apache-2.0, live on PyPI and the official MCP registry, at 100% branch-test coverage.

## What you can do

- **Pay from plain language.** "Pay this supplier EUR 4,200" becomes a validated pain.001 or pacs.008, IBAN- and XSD-checked, ready for the rail.
- **Match a statement in seconds.** Reconcile a camt.053 statement against what you expected, with an explainable reason for every match, partial and split payment.
- **Get off SWIFT MT in time.** Convert MT103/MT101/MT94x to ISO 20022 and fix structured addresses before the 2026–2028 deadlines, one message at a time.
- **Turn a mandate into a wire.** Take a signed AP2 or x402 agent mandate and produce a wire-valid pain.001, with spending-cap and expiry guardrails.

## The nine servers

### iso20022-mcp: the gateway

One agent-friendly surface across every family. Seven meta-tools (search, list_families, list_servers, describe, validate, generate, parse) route to the dedicated servers, so an agent sees a handful of verbs instead of sixty. Light core; families install as extras. [GitHub](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/iso20022-mcp) · [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/iso20022-mcp/)

### pain001-mcp: initiate

Customer credit transfer initiation (pain.001), with schema discovery, IBAN/BIC and XSD validation, cross-version migration, and a legacy MT101 to pain.001 converter. [GitHub](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/pain001-mcp) · [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/pain001-mcp/)

### pacs008-mcp: settle

FI-to-FI credit transfers (pacs.008), payment returns (pacs.004) and status reports (pacs.002). Includes the November 2026 structured-address toolkit and a legacy MT103 to pacs.008 converter. [GitHub](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/pacs008-mcp) · [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/pacs008-mcp/)

### camt053-mcp: read statements

Bank-to-customer statements (camt.053/052): parse entries and balances into structured data, validate, and convert legacy MT94x. [GitHub](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/camt053-mcp) · [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/camt053-mcp/)

### acmt001-mcp: accounts

Account management messaging (acmt.001): open, maintain and verify bank accounts from plain data, validated against the bundled schema. [GitHub](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/acmt001-mcp) · [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/acmt001-mcp/)

### reconcile-mcp: reconcile

Match camt.053 statement entries against expected pain.001 payments: exact, short/over, one-to-many (split settlement) and many-to-one (batch credit), each with an explainable score and reasons. Ships a sandbox test-mode, so you can try it with zero real data. [GitHub](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/reconcile-mcp) · [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/reconcile-mcp/)

### camt-exceptions: resolve

Exceptions and Investigations, the least-tooled corner of ISO 20022. Generate XSD-valid camt.056 payment cancellation requests and camt.029 resolution-of-investigation messages. [GitHub](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/camt-exceptions) · [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/camt-exceptions/)

### ap2-iso20022: bridge agent payments

Bridge Google AP2 and Coinbase x402 agent-payment mandates into wire-valid pain.001 / pacs.008 records, with spending-cap, expiry and authorisation guardrails. Transforms and validates only; it never moves money. [GitHub](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/ap2-iso20022) · [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/ap2-iso20022/)

### bankstatementparser-mcp: parse statements

Read, validate and summarise bank statements (camt.053, pain.001, MT940, CSV, OFX, QFX) as MCP tools: detect the format, parse entries into structured transactions, and return a clean summary. [GitHub](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/bankstatementparser-mcp) · [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/bankstatementparser-mcp/)

## Get started in under a minute

Install the whole suite in one line:

```bash
pip install "iso20022-mcp[all]"
```

Or run the gateway with no install and no account:

```bash
uvx --from "iso20022-mcp[all]" iso20022-mcp
```

Ask it to search "cancel a payment" and it points you at camt.056; ask it to generate and you get XSD-valid XML back. Under sixty seconds from nothing to a validated message.

## Safe by design

- **Validated before return.** Every generator checks its output against the official bundled XSD before it hands it back; malformed messages never leave the tool.
- **Never moves money.** The AP2/x402 bridge only transforms and validates. Producing a message is deliberately separate from sending it, so the money-movement step stays a human-guarded action.
- **Read-only where it counts.** Reconciliation is pure matching; tools are marked read-only, idempotent and closed-world so clients can reason about safety.
- **Owned by no bank.** Apache-2.0, vendor-neutral, runs on your own infrastructure, at 100% branch-test coverage.
