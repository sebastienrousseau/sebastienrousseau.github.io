<p class="story-lede">The November 2026 CBPR+ deadline and the SEPA migration are forcing every bank, PSP, and corporate treasury onto structured ISO 20022 messages. These tools cover the full lifecycle — initiation, clearing, statements, and account management — so teams adopt the standard without ripping out core systems.</p>

<section class="story-why" aria-label="Why it matters">
<h2>Why banks and treasuries choose these.</h2>
<p><strong>Deadline-ready.</strong> Built directly against EPAA, SWIFT, and CBPR+ guidance for the structured-address and ISO 20022 cut-overs.</p>
<p><strong>Auditable by design.</strong> Schema + XSD validation, deterministic output, and PII-masking that holds up under regulated review.</p>
<p><strong>Adopt incrementally.</strong> Each tool is a library you drop into existing pipelines — no rip-and-replace — and each ships with IDE (LSP) and AI-assistant (MCP) integrations.</p>
