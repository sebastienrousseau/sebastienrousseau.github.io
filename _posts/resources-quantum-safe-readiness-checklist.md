## What's in the PDF

A **20-step first-assessment checklist** for quantum-safe readiness in payments, built on [*From Awareness to Action*](/2026-11-16-quantum-safe-readiness-epaa/index.html), the November 2026 white paper from the Emerging Payments Association Asia's Quantum Safe Cryptography Working Group.

Supervisors are moving. The Monetary Authority of Singapore is issuing formal expectations with progressive milestones, and the HKMA expects Hong Kong's banking sector, which scored 2.3 out of 10 on the Quantum Preparedness Index, to reach full readiness by 2030. The paper's answer is not to finish an inventory first. This checklist turns its approach into a tracker:

- **Ownership**: name an accountable executive and authorise a limited first assessment
- **Scope**: pick a few high-value systems and one payment flow, by risk and feasibility
- **Trace**: follow that flow from initiation through clearing and settlement, and ask whether you could rotate its certificates without calling a third party
- **Sort**: split findings into what you control, what depends on vendors or participants, and what waits on shared infrastructure
- **Vendors and contracts**: four questions for critical vendors, and what to ask for at renewal
- **Cross-border**: why agility may mean running different algorithms in different markets
- **Operators**: what scheme and infrastructure operators owe their participants

It is my own reading of the paper, not an EPAA publication. The full paper is [free from EPAA](https://emergingpaymentsasia.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/From-Awareness-to-Action-What-Quantum-Safe-Readiness-Requires-Now.pdf).
