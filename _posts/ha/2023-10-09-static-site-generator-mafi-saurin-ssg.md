## Fahimta

### Ingantaccen Static Site Generator don Yanar Gizo na Zamani

A cikin yanayin dijital da ke ci gaba da haɓaka, sauri da inganci suna da matuƙar mahimmanci. Na ƙuduri aniyar nemo hanyar da zan iya samar da gidajen yanar gizo na tsaye da sauri waɗanda ke nuna inganci mai kyau, samun dama, da abokantaka na SEO.

Gidajen yanar gizo na tsaye sune shafukan yanar gizo waɗanda ke kasancewa kafafu kuma marasa canzawa sai dai idan an sabunta su da hannu. Suna ƙunshe da fayilolin HTML, CSS, kuma wani lokacin JavaScript, suna ba da saurin lokacin lodawa da ingantaccen tsaro idan aka kwatanta da gidajen yanar gizo masu canzawa (dynamic).

Abin takaici, babu wasu samfuran da ke akwai da suka cika buƙatuna kai tsaye. Saboda haka, na ɗauki nauyin ƙirƙirar sabon mafita tun daga farko. Burina shine in samar da wani zaɓi mai sauri kuma abin dogaro ga static site generators na yanzu da ake samu a kasuwa da kuma tura iyakokin abin da zai yiwu da Rust.

## Tunanin

### Mafita na Girma Marar Iyakaci da Keɓancewa

Na ɓullo da [**Static Site Generator ⧉**][00] da nufin samar da wani static site generator (SSG) mai saurin gaske wanda aka gina da [**Rust ⧉**][03] wanda ke sauƙaƙe tsarin ƙirƙirar gidajen yanar gizo na tsaye waɗanda aka inganta don SEO da kuma daidaita su da cikakken ƙa'idodin samun dama.

Sauri da aminci sune manyan abubuwan da na fi ba da muhimmanci yayin haɓaka Static Site Generator. Rust yare ne na shirye-shiryen tsarin (systems programming language) wanda aka sani don sauri da amincinsa, wanda ke sa shi dacewa don haɓaka static site generators masu inganci.

Na kasance mai ɗokin bincika iyakokin abin da zan iya yi da Markdown. Burina shi ne in tabbatar da cewa Static Site Generator zai iya samar da gidajen yanar gizo na tsaye waɗanda ke da sauri, amintattu, kuma abin dogaro. Ina son su kasance masu sauƙin shiga ga kowa, masu sauƙin amfani da sauƙin sabuntawa, kuma su sami babban matsayi a injunan bincike.

## Tasiri

### Sabon Tsari don Static Site Generators

Samar da gidan yanar gizo na tsaye tare da Static Site Generator ba shi da wahala, yana ba da damar mayar da hankali kan abun ciki wanda ke haɓaka tasiri ga masu sauraron da aka yi niyya. Tsarin yana da sauƙi kuma yana kammaluwa cikin milliseconds.

Sakamakon haka, gidan yanar gizon yana da cikakken amsa (responsive), yana bin [**Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) ⧉**][01], [**ADA ⧉**][02], kuma an inganta shi don Search Engine Optimisation (SEO) tun daga farko.
