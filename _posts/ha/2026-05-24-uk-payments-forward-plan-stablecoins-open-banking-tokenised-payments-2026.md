## Me Ya Sa 2026 Shi Ne Shekarar Da Wannan Ya Zama Na Dabarun

Masana'antar ta wuce matakin karɓuwa. Ba ya isa kawai a haɗa hanya, a koma saƙo, a gudanar da gwajin tabbatar da AI, ko a sanar da gwajin tokenisation. A 2026, fa'idar dabarun tana zuwa daga tsara waɗannan damar a kan hanyar aiki ta gaskiya, sannan a tabbatar cewa hanyar aikin ta fi tsaro, sauri, arha, juriya, ko amfani ga abokan ciniki.

Wannan shi ne dalilin da ya sa manufofin biyan kuɗi yanzu batun matakin hukumar gudanarwa ne. Matsi iri ɗaya na ci gaba da maimaita kansa: data mai arziƙi na biyan kuɗi, sasantawa na ainihi-lokaci, kuɗin tokenised, yanke shawara na AI, Open Banking, juriya na aiki, taro a kan cloud, da ƙarin shaidar tsari. Idan an bi su dabam, waɗannan matsi suna ƙirƙirar yaɗuwar shirye-shirye. Idan an bi su a matsayin gine-gine guda ɗaya, suna ƙirƙirar ƙarfin aiki ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-fintech-backed-to-embrace-future-payments-technology "UK fintech backed to embrace future payments technology"), [Association of Corporate Treasurers](https://www.treasurers.org/hub/blog/Update-Payments-Landscape-May-2026 "Update on the Payments landscape – May 2026")).

## Tushen Gine-gine na 2026

### 1. Hanyar Aiki da Farko, Fasaha na Biyu

Banki ya kamata ya fara da matsala: ruwa-ruwa da aka kama, jinkirin sasantawa, kuɗin daidaitawa, biyan kuɗi da ya gaza, fallasawa ga zamba, raunin iya bincike, ko mummunan ƙwarewar abokin ciniki. Fasaha kawai tana da hujja inda ta cire wannan matsala ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-fintech-backed-to-embrace-future-payments-technology "UK fintech backed to embrace future payments technology")).

### 2. Data a Matsayin Filin Sarrafawa

Data mai tsari, mai gudanarwa, kuma mai gano hanya ita ce harsashin. Ba tare da data mai amfani ba, sarrafa kansa yana zama mai rauni kuma bin tsari yana zama na hannu. Tare da data mai amfani, bankuna za su iya ƙirƙirar hankali na tafiyar da, sarrafawa na ainihi-lokaci, da nazari ga abokan ciniki ([Association of Corporate Treasurers](https://www.treasurers.org/hub/blog/Update-Payments-Landscape-May-2026 "Update on the Payments landscape – May 2026")).

### 3. Tsara A Tsakanin Hanyoyi (Rails) da Dandamali

Gine-gine dole ne su tallafa hanyoyi da yawa, masu samarwa, makircin ainihi, siginar haɗari, da kadarorin sasantawa. Hukuncin tafiyar da ya kamata a yi shi ne ta hanyar farashi, sauri, ƙarewa (finality), iko, fifikon abokin ciniki, juriya, da arzikin data.

### 4. Bin Tsari da Shaida Da Aka Saka

Samfurin bin tsari dole ne ya zama na asali na hanyar aiki. Policy-as-code, audit logs masu sarrafa kansu, shaidar juriya na aiki, bayanan izini (consent records), da gudanar da samfuri suna buƙatar a ƙirƙira su a matsayin ɓangare na aiwatarwa, ba a sake ƙirƙirar su ga masu binciken ba a daga baya.

### 5. Tattalin Arziƙin Naúrar da Ƙimar Abokin Ciniki

Kowane shiri yana buƙatar shaidar ƙimar kasuwanci. Farashi-kowace-biyan, farashi-kowane-yanke-hukunci, farashi-kowane-bincike, ruwa-ruwa da aka adana, gyaran hannu da aka guje wa, asarar zamba da aka rage, da karɓuwar abokin ciniki su ne za su tantance hukuncin haɓakawa.

## Abin da Wannan ke nufi a kowace Irin Banki

### Bankunan Duniya

Bankunan duniya su ƙirƙira tsara a matakin dandamali don kowace kasuwa, hanya, token, da damar AI ba su zama samfurin aiki dabam ba.

### Bankunan Yanki

Bankunan yanki su mai da hankali kan lamuran amfani inda amana, sanin kasuwar gida, da haɗawa mai sauƙi suka kayar da girma: bayyanawar treasury, hana zamba, biyan kuɗi na Open Banking, da sabis na kuɗin dijital da aka tsara.

### Fintechs da PSPs

Fintechs su rage rikitarwa ga bankuna maimakon ƙarawa wata hanya mai rabuwa. Mafi kyawun shawarwarin za su kawo tsara, shaidar bin tsari, ko hankalin data.

### Masu Riƙe Kuɗi na Kamfani (Corporate Treasurers)

Masu riƙe kuɗi su buƙaci ingantawar da za a iya auna: ƙarancin gyaran biyan kuɗi, mafi kyawun bayyanawar ruwa-ruwa, data mai arziƙi na daidaitawa, sasantawa cikin sauri, da ƙarfafa sarrafawa kan yanke shawara da aka sarrafa kansu.

## Kammalawa

UK Payments Forward Plan a 2026 a ƙarshe tambayar gine-gine ce. Cibiyoyin da za su yi nasara ba za su zama waɗanda suka fi yawan gwaje-gwaje ko mafi yawan harshe na kirkire-kirkire ba. Za su zama cibiyoyin da ke haɗa hanyoyin aiki na abokan ciniki, ingancin data, tsara hanyoyi, bin tsari da aka saka, da tattalin arziƙin naúra cikin samfurin aiki mai daidaito.

## Tambayoyin da Ake Yawan Yi

**Me ya sa wannan batu yake da gaggawa a 2026?**

Saboda ababen more rayuwa masu dacewa, tsari, da siginar buƙatun abokin ciniki sun haɗu. Abin da ya kasance gwaji na zaɓi yanzu yana zama ɓangare na samfurin aiki na banki.

**Mene ne haɗarin aiwatarwa mafi girma?**

Haɗari mafi girma shi ne rabewa: ƙungiyoyi dabam-dabam suna gina gwaje-gwaje dabam-dabam, kowanne da data, sarrafawa, gudanarwa, da tattalin arziƙi dabam.

**Mene ne banki ya kamata ya gina da farko?**

Banki ya kamata ya fara da hanyar aikin inda akwai ƙima da za a iya auna, kamar sasantawa cikin sauri, ƙarancin kuɗin daidaitawa, ƙarancin bincike, hana zamba da aka inganta, ko mafi kyawun bayyanawar ruwa-ruwa.

**Yaya za a auna nasara?**

Ya kamata a auna nasara ta tattalin arziƙin naúra, shaidar juriya, ingancin data, karɓuwar abokin ciniki, raguwar haɗarin aiki, da ingantawar ruwa-ruwa ko jarin aiki.
