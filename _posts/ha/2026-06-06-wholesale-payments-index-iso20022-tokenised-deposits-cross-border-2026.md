# Index na Biyan Kuɗi na Wholesale a 2026: ISO 20022, Tokenised Deposits, Layukan Ainihin-Lokaci, da Settlement na Cross-Border

Biyan kuɗi na wholesale a 2026 ana sake fasalinsa ta sauye-sauye guda biyu lokaci ɗaya: bayanan biyan kuɗi da aka tsara da settlement mai shiryarwa. Matakin structured-address na Nuwamba 2026 na SWIFT yana tilasta ingancin bayanai cikin tsarin aiki, yayin da BIS Project Agorá da tokenised deposits suke gwada ko settlement na cross-border zai iya zama atomic, mai gaskiya, da kuma na koyaushe.

---

> **Taƙaitaccen Zartarwa / Mahimman Ma'auni**
>
> - **Nuwamba 2026 mataki ne mai tsanani na bayanai.** SWIFT ya ce biyan kuɗi da ke ɗauke da unstructured addresses ba za a sake tallafa wa su ba bayan canjin SR 2026.
> - **Bayanan da aka tsara sun zama kayan aikin samfur.** Gari da ƙasa dole su kasance a filayen da aka ƙaddara aƙalla, suna mai da ingancin bayanan biyan kuɗi batun abokin ciniki, aiki, da bin doka.
> - **Tokenised deposits zaɓi ne na ƙira ga wholesale.** Project Agorá yana bincika tokenised deposits na bankunan kasuwanci da tokenised reserves na babban banki a tsarin haɗaɗɗen ledger.
> - **Index ɗin ya kamata ya auna ingancin settlement, ba kawai sauri ba.** Finality, gaskiya, ƙimomin gyara, amfani da ruwa, bayanan bin doka, da gani ga abokin ciniki suna da mahimmanci kamar gudanarwa nan take.
> - **Biyan kuɗi na cross-border ya ci gaba da kasancewa wani aikin gwamnati-da-kamfani.** FSB yana ci gaba da jagorantar tsarin G20 ta hanyar aiwatarwa, tare da haɗin kai na ɓangaren gwamnati da na kamfani.
>

## Me Yasa 2026 Ita Ce Shekarar Wannan Index Ke da Muhimmanci

Stanford AI Index yana da amfani saboda yana ɗaukar fage na fasaha mai motsi cikin sauri a matsayin abin da za a iya aunawa: fitar da bincike, aikin fasaha, turawa mai alhakin, tattalin arziki, ɗaukar sashe, manufa, da ra'ayin jama'a ana kawo su cikin tsari guda ([Stanford HAI ⧉](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report "The 2026 AI Index Report")). Bankuna da cibiyoyin kuɗi yanzu suna buƙatar irin wannan tarbiyya ga kayan aiki. Agentic AI, tsaron quantum-safe, juriyar cloud native, da biyan kuɗi na wholesale ba sa sake zama tafarki na ƙirƙira daban; suna haɗuwa cikin tsarin aiki guda.

Tambaya mai amfani ga banki ba ko kowane fage yana da mahimmanci ba. Ita ce ko cibiyar za ta iya auna shiri a fadin dukansu a lokaci guda. Banki na iya turawa agentic AI kuma ya zama mai rauni idan cryptography ɗinsa ba shiri ba ne don ƙaura. Yana iya zamanantar da dandamali na cloud kuma ya gaza idan bayanan biyan kuɗi sun kasance ba a tsara su ba. Yana iya gudanar da gwajin tokenisation kuma ya samar da haɗari na tsari idan ba a tsara layukan settlement, ruwa, asalin mutum, da audit tare ba.

## Mahallin Sauyin Bayanan Biyan Kuɗi

ISO 20022 ya motsa daga aikin tsarin saƙo zuwa tsarin aiki na ingancin bayanai. Idan bayanan beneficiary, debtor, creditor, agent, gari, ƙasa, manufa, da party suna da rauni, banki zai fuskanci ƙin, gyara, gogayya na sanctions, takaicin abokin ciniki, da raunin nazari.

## Tokenised Deposits da CBDC na Wholesale

Tokenised deposits suna kiyaye samfurin kuɗin bankin kasuwanci yayin da suke ƙara shiryarwa. Kuɗin babban banki na wholesale yana kiyaye finality na settlement. Tsarin ƙira mai ban sha'awa shine haɗin: kuɗin bankin kasuwanci don dangantakar abokin ciniki da intermediation na bashi, kuɗin babban banki don settlement na ƙarshe da kwarin gwiwa na tsari.

## Sabon Samfurin Biyan Kuɗi na Wholesale

Samfurin ba shi ne kawai biyan kuɗi ba. Wani fakiti ne na gudanarwa, bayanai, ruwa, bin doka, traceability, da kula da banbance. Bankunan da za su iya fallasa waɗannan ƙarfin ta APIs da dashboards na abokin ciniki za su mai da bin doka na kayan aiki darajar abokin ciniki.

## Abin da Wannan ke Nufi Bisa Nau'in Banki

### Bankuna na Tsarin Tsari na Duniya

Bankunan duniya su kamata su ɗauki wannan index a matsayin scorecard na gine-ginen kamfani. Fifikon ba shi ne wani proof of concept ba; shaida ce cewa za a iya gudanar da hanyoyin aiki na kansu, ƙaurawar cryptographic, dogaro na cloud, da zamanantarwar biyan kuɗi a matsayin tsarin haɗari da darajar guda.

### Bankuna na Hada-hada da Bankuna na Kamfani

Bankunan hada-hada su mai da hankali kan biyan kuɗi na wholesale, bayanan da aka tsara, ruwa, tokenised deposits, da hidimomin agentic treasury. Mafi mahimmancin ba da shawara ga abokin ciniki ba shi ne kawai sauri na motsa kuɗi ba; bayyananne ne, mai iya audit, motsi na kuɗi mai shiryarwa tare da ƙarancin bincike da gani mafi kyau na working capital.

### Bankuna na Yanki

Bankuna na yanki su yi amfani da index don kauce wa ɓarna na shirin. Ba sa buƙatar shugabantar kowane iyaka, amma suna buƙatar matsayi masu gaskiya kan gudanar da AI, kasida ta post-quantum, shaidar ficewa na cloud, da shirin bayanan biyan kuɗi.

### Fintechs, PSPs, da Masu Bayar da Kayan Aiki

Fintechs da masu bayar da kayan aiki su daidaita tsarin samfurinsu zuwa shirin banki mai aunawa. Mafi kyawun shawarwari za su rage haɗarin haɗawa, ƙarfafa shaida, kuma su sa kayan aiki masu rikitarwa ya zama mai sauƙi ga bankuna su gudanar.

## Ƙarshe

Darajar rahoton index-style ita ce yana sauya tsarin fasaha da ya rabu zuwa tsarin aiki mai aunawa. A 2026, masu nasara a cikin kayan aikin kuɗi ba za su zama cibiyoyin da ke da yawan gwaje-gwaje ba. Za su zama cibiyoyin da za su iya shaida shiri a fadin 'yancin kai, tsaro, juriya, settlement, tattalin arziki, da gudanarwa a lokaci guda.

## Tambayoyin da Ake Yawan Yi

**Me yasa ISO 20022 har yanzu batu ne na 2026?**

Saboda ƙaurawar ba ta cika ba har sai bayanan biyan kuɗi sun zama masu tsari, masu gudanarwa, an kama su a tushen, kuma ana iya amfani da su a fadin tashoshi, abokan ciniki, da kayan aikin kasuwa.

**Menene tokenised deposit?**

Tokenised deposit shi ne wakilcin dijital na kuɗin bankin kasuwanci, an tsara shi don kiyaye dangantakar banki-mai-ajiya yayin da yake ba da damar settlement mai shiryarwa.

**Shin tokenised deposits suna maye gurbin stablecoins?**

Ba a ko'ina ba. Stablecoins na iya ci gaba da zama masu amfani a wasu mahallin na digital-asset da cross-border, yayin da tokenised deposits suna da kyau a tsari ga wholesale na banki da aka tsara.

**Menene bankunan ya kamata su auna?**

Auna shirin bayanan da aka tsara, ƙin biyan kuɗi, farashin gyara, lokacin settlement, amfani da ruwa, sakamakon jagorancin layi, da gani ga abokin ciniki.
