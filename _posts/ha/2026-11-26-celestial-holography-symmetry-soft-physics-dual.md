## Me ya sa holography a flat space ke buƙatar sabon nau'in dual

An fi sanin holography a matsayin bayani game da spacetimes masu cosmological constant mara kyau (negative). Celestial holography na bin wata manufa daban. Tana neman kafa holographic correspondence a matsayin sabuwar hanya zuwa quantum gravity a asymptotically flat spacetimes. Waɗannan rukunin mafita ne ga Einstein's equations masu cosmological constant na sifili da matter stress-energy da ke wuri ɗaya.

Shawarar kanta a taƙaice take. Tana ɗauka cewa akwai duality tsakanin gravitational S-matrix da correlators a cikin conformal field theory da ke kan celestial sphere. Kalmomin suna da muhimmanci: wannan duality ce da aka ba da shawara, kuma ya kamata a karanta tsarin a matsayin shirin da ake ginawa, ba daidaito da aka tabbatar ba. Wannan maƙala ta dogara ne kan "Celestial Holography" na Sabrina Pasterski, Monica Pate da Ana-Maria Raclariu (arXiv:2111.11392, 2021), white paper da aka nema don tsarin Snowmass na 2022. Tana bitar dalili daga asymptotic symmetries, ginshiƙan holographic dictionary da aka ba da shawara, yiwuwar amfani a gwaji da ka'ida, da wasu muhimman tambayoyi a buɗe. A ko'ina, ta taƙaita kanta ga massless scattering a girma 3+1, don haka kowane bayani a ƙasa yana ɗauke da wannan iyaka.

Abin da ke sa shawarar ta fi zato shi ne inda ta fito. Hanyar ta bi ta symmetry, kuma ya dace a bi ta mataki-mataki, domin kowane mataki na tilasta na gaba.

## Asymptotic symmetries: ragowar bayan gauge fixing

Asymptotic symmetries diffeomorphisms ne da ke kiyaye causal structure na boundary a cikin conformal compactification. A wata ma'ana, ana iya gane su a matsayin residual diffeomorphisms da suka rage bayan an saita gauge da boundary conditions da suka dace. Su ne abin da ya rage idan an cire redundancy amma har yanzu akwai transformations da ke aiki ba tare da sauƙi ba (non-trivially) a kan boundary.

Abin sha'awa shi ne yadda za a iya faɗaɗa boundary conditions. Nazarin da suka sassauta su sosai don ba da damar local enhancements na Lorentz subgroup, da ake kira superrotations, sun bayyana ne kwanan nan kawai. Wannan sassautawa ba ƙaramin bayani ba ne na fasaha. Ita ce maɓallin da sauran labarin ke juyawa a kai, domin local enhancement na Lorentz symmetry shi ne ainihin irin tsarin da conformal field theory mai girma biyu ta iya ɗauka.

White paper ɗin ta lura cewa symmetry generators ana wakiltar su ta halitta da currents a cikin ka'ida mai girma biyu. Matsalar gravity mai girma huɗu na ci gaba da samar da abubuwa masu girma biyu. Wannan maimaitawa ita ce alamar farko cewa dual ɗin yana kan celestial sphere.

## Tsari ɗaya, fuska uku: memory, soft theorems da Ward identities

Akwai daidaito tsakanin memory effects, soft theorems da Ward identities don asymptotic symmetries. Wannan shi ne babban abin lura na shirin soft physics, kuma an tabbatar da shi, ba zato kawai ba. Ward identities don asymptotic symmetries suna bayyana a matsayin soft theorems na S-matrix. Memory effect sakamako ne kai tsaye da ake iya gani na asymptotic symmetries; a faɗin ma'ana, memory effects alamomi ne masu ƙarancin mita (low-frequency) da ake iya gani na symmetry enhancements.

Daidaiton yana da amfani ta fuskoki biyu. Bayani a matakin amplitude game da soft particles bayani ne kuma game da symmetry, kuma bayani game da symmetry hasashe ne kuma game da abin da detector zai iya gani a ka'ida. Masanin ka'ida da ke aiki da S-matrix elements da masanin gwaji da ke tunani kan low-frequency signals, a wannan fahimta, suna magana ne kan abu ɗaya.

Labarin superrotation ya nuna daidaiton yana aiki na gaske. Hasashe game da ko superrotations na zahiri ne ya kai ga gano subleading soft graviton theorem. Wannan theorem ɗin kuma ya kafa Ward identity don Virasoro symmetry. Ra'ayin hasashe game da symmetry ya samar da theorem tabbatacce game da scattering, kuma theorem ɗin ya tabbatar da Ward identity na symmetry ɗin. Ba kasafai hasashe na ra'ayi ke biyan bashi da tsabta haka ba.

Daga nan hujjar dual ɗin ta biyo kai tsaye. Dole ne holographic dual na quantum gravity a asymptotically flat spacetimes masu girma huɗu ya ƙunshi conformal symmetry mai girma biyu. Dual theory da aka ba da shawara wadda aka gina a kan wannan symmetry ana kiranta celestial conformal field theory, ko CCFT.

## Gina ƙamus: Mellin transforms da boost eigenstates

Dual theory tana da amfani ne kawai idan akwai hanyar fassara tsakanin ɓangarorin biyu. Shigarwar farko ita ce celestial amplitude. Celestial amplitudes suna mayar da gravitational S-matrix zuwa basis na boost eigenstates, kuma suna sauyawa kamar correlation functions na conformal field theory. Wannan yana ba da muhimmiyar shigarwa a cikin holographic dictionary.

Hanyar aikin a bayyane take. Ga massless particles, ana iya samun sauyawa daga momentum zuwa boost eigenstates ta hanyar Mellin transform a cikin energy. Dalilin da ya sa sakamakon ke aiki kamar abin CFT shi ne Lorentz transformations a girma huɗu suna daidai da global conformal transformations a kan celestial sphere. Wannan yana tabbatar da cewa operators da aka gina daga Mellin-transformed wavepackets suna sauyawa a matsayin quasi-primaries masu conformal weights (h, h̄).

Sannan gravity ta ƙara wani abu. A cikin ka'idar gravity, waɗannan quasi-primaries ana ɗaga su zuwa Virasoro primaries ta hanyar haɗuwarsu (coupling) da subleading soft graviton. Subleading soft graviton mode yana haɗuwa da external particle mai helicity ℓ da energy ω ta hanyar operators ĥ da h̄ˆ. Don haka ba a ɗora Virasoro structure daga waje ba; tana fitowa ne daga yadda soft graviton ke manne wa external states na yau da kullum.

Finite-energy scattering states, waɗanda normalisable single-particle wavepackets a cikin bulk ke bayyana su, celestial operators masu spectrum ∆ = 1 + iλ, λ ∈ R ne ke kama su. Wannan bayani ne tabbatacce game da waɗanne conformal dimensions ke daidai da states na zahiri masu normalisable. Yana gaya maka wane ɓangare na complex ∆ plane ke ɗauke da scattering na yau da kullum.

## Yadda soft sector take daga ɓangaren celestial

Da zarar an fassara soft theorems zuwa boost eigenstate basis, suna samun karatu mai tsabta ta representation theory. Conformal dimensions na operators a cikin soft theorems suna ba da damar shortened representations na global conformal symmetry. Wannan yana bayyana tsarin da aka lura na soft theorems cikin multiplets. Tsarin da zai iya kama da kuskuren lissafi a ɓangaren S-matrix, a ɓangaren celestial, sakamako ne na representation theory.

Wani sakamako mai alaƙa ya shafi factorisation. Factorisation a cikin soft limit na scattering na ci gaba da kasancewa a cikin boost eigenstate basis. Canjin basis ba ya lalata wata siffa ta tsari da masana kimiyyar lissafi ke dogara da ita, kuma irin wannan gwaji ne da ƙamus da aka ba da shawara dole ya wuce.

Sannan ga poles. A matsayin function na net conformal dimension, celestial amplitudes suna da jerin simple poles. Wuraren da suke suna bambanta UV completions na quantum field theories da UV completions da ake tsammani na quantum gravity, kuma residues suna maimaita coefficients a cikin EFT expansion. Ga mai karatu na fasaha, nan ne tsarin ya fara zama kayan aiki. Analytic structure na abu guda tana raba rukunin ultraviolet behaviour biyu kuma tana dawo da effective-field-theory data.

## Celestial CFT ba CFT ta yau da kullum ba ce

Da a ce CCFT conformal field theory ce ta yau da kullum, da za a iya shigo da kayan aikin da aka saba kuma a gama. Hoton white paper ɗin ya fi ban sha'awa: wasu ɓangarori sun dace da kayan aikin da aka saba, wasu kuma ba su dace ba.

Superrotations suna kafa Virasoro symmetry wadda ake shigar da ita ta halitta cikin fasahar CFT ta yau da kullum. Wannan ɓangare sananne ne. Amma global translations, da kuma soft symmetries marasa iyaka da ke da alaƙa da (half)-integer dimension insertions, suna nuna constraints da ba na yau da kullum ba a kan CFT data. Ka'idar tana da buƙatun symmetry da suka wuce abin da CFT ta littafin karatu ke bayarwa, kuma waɗannan buƙatun suna ɗauke da bayanan bulk.

OPE data na celestial CFT suna ɗauke da halayen scattering a cikin collinear limits. Wannan yana haɗa wani ɓangare na tsarin CFT mai girma biyu da wani yanayi na zahiri da ake iya ganewa a girma huɗu. Ward identities da ke fitowa daga soft theorems kuma suna kai ga recursion relations don OPE data.

A nan bayanan takardar sun zama masu nuni, kuma ya kamata a karanta ƙarfin kowane bayani a hankali. Idan aka ɗauki wasu analyticity assumptions a cikin ∆, Ward identities sun isa su bootstrap gaba ɗaya leading kuma, a wasu lokuta, subleading collinear behaviour na scattering. Sharaɗin yana da nauyi na gaske: sakamakon ya dogara ne kan analyticity assumptions, kuma yana rufe subleading behaviour a wasu lokuta kawai.

White paper ɗin ta yi faɗaɗɗen hukunci, shi ma da taka tsantsan. Wannan siffa ta CCFT da alama (apparently) tana ba da damar kinematics mai girma huɗu ta tantance dynamics na dual mai girma biyu. Akwai kuma hujja ta kwanan nan cewa ana iya amfani da celestial symmetries don tantance wasu bulk dynamics. A ganina, wannan shi ne ra'ayi mafi tasiri a cikin tsarin, idan ya tsaya. A yawancin quantum field theories, symmetry na taƙaita dynamics amma ba ta tantance ta ba. Takardar ta kira wannan siffa ta musamman sosai ta CCFT, kuma kalmarta "apparently" ta nuna cewa bayanin da taka tsantsan yake, ba tabbatacce ba.

## Daga ka'ida zuwa detectors: memory effects

Shawarar holography da ba ta taɓa kusantar abin da ake iya gani ba za ta yi wuyar karewa a gaban masanin gwaji mai shakka. Celestial holography tana da alaƙa mafi tabbas fiye da yawancin, domin memory effects alamomi ne masu ƙarancin mita da ake iya gani na symmetry enhancements.

Shirin soft physics ya gano sababbin memory effects a cikin gravity da gauge theory: spin memory a cikin gravity, da colour memory a cikin gauge theory. Ga memories da ba a fi sani ba, akwai shawarwarin auna na proof-of-concept daban-daban da nazarin yiwuwar gwaji.

Leading gravitational memory effect ya fi ci gaba. Tuni ana gudanar da binciken gwaji a kansa. Kuma akwai darasi na aiki daga nazarin gravitational waves: zaɓin BMS frame yana da muhimmiyar rawa wajen fitar da gravitational waveform daidai. Wata siffa ta asymptotic symmetry group tana bayyana a matsayin abin la'akari na nazarin bayanai, domin frame mara daidai na nufin waveform mara daidai.

Colour memory tana da gidanta na halitta. Observable ce ta halitta a cikin yanayin zahiri da aikin electron-ion collider (EIC) da aka ƙaddamar kwanan nan zai bincika. Takardar ba ta yi alƙawarin sakamako ba, sai ta gabatar da tambayoyi biyu kai tsaye. Shin LISA za ta iya auna spin memory? Shin za a ga colour memory a EIC? Dukansu a buɗe suke. Duk wanda ya gabatar da ɗayansu a matsayin ganowa da ake tsammani zai wuce hujjar da ke akwai.

## Infrared divergences da soft hair

Soft physics ta kai kuma ga wata matsala da ta daɗe tana damun duk wanda ke lissafin scattering amplitudes: infrared divergences. Mahangar symmetry tana ba da bayani bisa ga kiyaye caji (charge conservation).

Rashin kiyaye asymptotic charge yana ba da fassara ga amplitude da ke zama sifili don fitar da adadi mai iyaka na soft particles. Idan aka haɗa adadi mara iyaka na soft emissions, ana samun sakamako mai iyaka daidai domin gudummawar soft quanta tana sa asymptotic charges su kasance a kiyaye. Zama sifili da zama mai iyaka fuskoki biyu ne na ƙa'idar lissafi ɗaya.

Sakamakon ƙa'ida ce ta gaba ɗaya. Ta hanyar gane symmetry a matsayin tushen infrared divergences, wannan mahanga tana kafa ƙa'ida ta gaba ɗaya don gina infrared safe amplitudes. Ga masu aiki a fagen, wannan yana mayar da abin da ake ɗauka a matsayin damuwar fasaha zuwa wani abu mai dalilin tsari.

Iyalin ra'ayoyin guda ɗaya ya kai ga black holes da sauran horizons, waɗanda za su iya ɗaukar soft hair. Takardar ta bayyana tantance ainihin furucin waɗannan bayanai a cikin tsarin CCFT a matsayin fannin bincike mai aiki, don haka wannan nuni ne zuwa ƙarin aiki, ba sakamako da aka kammala ba.

## Inda hujjar ke da ƙarfi da inda ba ta da shi

Yana da amfani a raba abin da aka tabbatar da abin da aka ba da shawara. Abubuwa da dama suna tsaye a kan tushe mai ƙarfi a bayanin white paper ɗin. An tabbatar da daidaito tsakanin memory effects, soft theorems da Ward identities. Haka ma hanyar daga hasashen superrotation zuwa subleading soft graviton theorem da Virasoro Ward identity. Celestial amplitudes suna sauyawa kamar CFT correlators. An bayyana simple-pole structure da karatun residues a matsayin EFT coefficients a matsayin sakamako, haka ma recursion relations don OPE data.

Wasu ɓangarori a fili sun fi rauni. Duality ɗin kanta shawara ce. Iƙirarin cewa analyticity assumptions na ba Ward identities damar bootstrap collinear behaviour na da sharaɗi. Shawarar cewa kinematics mai girma huɗu na tantance dynamics mai girma biyu tana da taka tsantsan ta "apparently", kuma hujjar cewa celestial symmetries na tantance wasu bulk dynamics an bayyana ta a matsayin hujja ta kwanan nan, ba tabbaci ba. Tambayoyin gwaji game da LISA da EIC a buɗe suke.

Ra'ayina shi ne wannan rarrabuwa ƙarfi ce. Tsarin da sakamakonsa mafi ƙarfi ke a matakin symmetry, soft theorems da analytic structure, kuma iƙirarinsa mafi ƙarfin hali an yi musu alama a matsayin mafi ƙarfin hali, tsari ne da ake iya gwadawa. Ana iya duba sakamako masu ƙarfi da sanannun soft theorems. Masu ƙarfin hali kuma suna nuna inda za a matsa.

Iyakoki biyu kuma sun shafi duk abin da ke sama. Faɗin shi ne massless scattering a girma 3+1, don haka babu abin da ke nan da ya shafi wasu girma ko massive particles. Kuma an bayyana ƙamus ɗin a matsayin shawara, inda aka ayyana celestial CFT ta hanyar tsarin symmetry ɗinta maimakon cikakken gini mai zaman kansa.

## Inda tambayoyin da ke a buɗe ke nuni

Tambayar fasaha mafi gaggawa ita ce har ina ra'ayin bootstrap zai kai. Ward identities suna kai ga recursion relations don OPE data, kuma ƙarƙashin analyticity assumptions a cikin ∆ sun isa su bootstrap gaba ɗaya leading, kuma a wasu lokuta subleading, collinear behaviour. Tambaya ta gaba ta halitta ita ce waɗanne lokuta ne har yanzu ba a iya kaiwa ba, kuma ko za a iya ba da hujjar analyticity assumptions maimakon a ɗauka kawai. Idan za a iya, shawarar cewa kinematics na tantance dynamics za ta sami nauyi na gaske. Idan ba za a iya ba, faɗin bootstrap ɗin zai ragu zuwa abin da assumptions ɗin suka ba da dama.

Ɓangaren gwaji yana ba da ɗayan gwajin. Shawarwarin auna na proof-of-concept da nazarin yiwuwa sun riga sun wanzu don spin da colour memory, kuma ana gudanar da bincike kan leading gravitational memory effect. Ko LISA za ta iya auna spin memory, da ko za a ga colour memory a EIC, za su nuna yadda tsarin symmetry da ke ƙarƙashin celestial holography ke kaiwa kai tsaye cikin na'urori. Waɗannan amsoshi ba su samu ba tukuna, kuma babu abin da ke cikin tsarin da ke tabbatar da su. Wace daga cikin tambayoyin biyu za ta warware da farko ita kanta abin kallo ce.

## Tambayoyin da Ake Yawan Yi

**Me celestial holography ke ba da shawara?**
Tana ba da shawarar duality tsakanin gravitational S-matrix da correlators a cikin conformal field theory da ke kan celestial sphere. Manufar ita ce sabuwar hanya zuwa quantum gravity a asymptotically flat spacetimes. Takardar ta gabatar da ita a matsayin shawara, ba sakamako da aka tabbatar ba.

**Me ya sa dole dual theory ta zama mai girma biyu kuma conformal?**
Asymptotic symmetries, memory effects, soft theorems da Ward identities sun zama masu daidaito, kuma symmetry generators ana wakiltar su ta halitta da currents a cikin ka'ida mai girma biyu. Subleading soft graviton theorem ta kafa Ward identity don Virasoro symmetry. Daga wannan, takardar ta kammala cewa dole ne holographic dual na quantum gravity mai girma huɗu a asymptotically flat spacetime ya ƙunshi conformal symmetry mai girma biyu.

**Mene ne celestial amplitude?**
Celestial amplitude tana mayar da gravitational S-matrix zuwa basis na boost eigenstates. Ga massless particles ana yin haka da Mellin transform a cikin energy. Sakamakon yana sauyawa kamar correlation functions na conformal field theory, wanda ke ba da muhimmiyar shigarwa a cikin holographic dictionary.

**Shin Ward identities za su iya tantance dynamics na dual theory?**
Ward identities da ke fitowa daga soft theorems suna kai ga recursion relations don OPE data. Idan aka ɗauki wasu analyticity assumptions a cikin ∆, sun isa su bootstrap leading kuma, a wasu lokuta, subleading collinear behaviour na scattering. Takardar ta ce wannan da alama yana ba kinematics mai girma huɗu damar tantance dynamics na dual mai girma biyu, bayani mai taka tsantsan ba tabbaci na gaba ɗaya ba.

**Shin ana iya gwada wani abu daga cikin wannan?**
Tuni ana gudanar da binciken gwaji kan leading gravitational memory effect. Ko LISA za ta iya auna spin memory da ko za a ga colour memory a EIC an gabatar da su a matsayin tambayoyi a buɗe. Ga waɗannan memories da ba a fi sani ba akwai shawarwarin auna na proof-of-concept da nazarin yiwuwa.
