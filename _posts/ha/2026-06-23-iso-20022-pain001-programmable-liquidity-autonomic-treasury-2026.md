> **Taƙaitaccen bayanin gudanarwa.** Watanni biyar kafin yankan SWIFT MT/MX na 22 ga Nuwamba 2026, ISO 20022 ya daina zama aikin ƙaura kuma ya zama tushen bayanai don baitulmalin Banki na Kamfanoni da Saka Hannun Jari. Kashi 44% na bankunan da binciken shirye-shiryen RedCompass Labs ya bayar da rahoton sun bata hanya ba a baya suke kan musayar tsarin waya ba; sun bata wajen wajibcin lissafin kwamiti don kawo lambobin manufa masu tsari, adireshin `<PstlAdr>` masu tsari, da bayanan aikawa masu bin CBPR+ a cikin kowane biyan kuɗi tsakanin ƙasashe da suka samo ko karɓa. Wannan labarin ya tsara pain.001 a matsayin bugun zuciya na tarin kuɗi mai shirye-shirye — abin da tsarin canonical na ISO-na-farko, shigarwar tabbatarwa-a-rarrabewa, da filin sarrafa wanda ke cinye pacs.008 kai tsaye suke kama da su a samarwa — da kuma abin da hukunci na ƙa'ida zai kasance ga bankunan da suka isa 22 ga Nuwamba har yanzu suna ɗaukar shi a matsayin matsalar fassara.

A watan Yuni 2026, ISO 20022 ya daina zama labarin ƙaura. Shi ne tushen. Kowane babban banki na kamfanoni da na saka hannun jari yanzu yana ɗaukar `pain.001`, `pacs.008`, da `camt.053` a matsayin tsarin bayanan farko na baitulmali, ba a matsayin tsarin waya da za a fassara a gefen ba. Duk da haka, da watanni biyar zuwa [yankan SWIFT MT/MX na 22 ga Nuwamba 2026](https://www.redcompasslabs.com/insights/what-now-iso-20022-deadlines-in-2026-onwards/ "RedCompass Labs: wa'adodin ISO 20022 a 2026 da gaba"), [kusan rabin bankunan duniya har yanzu ba a kan hanya ba](https://financialit.net/news/banking/nearly-half-banks-are-behind-iso-20022 "Financial IT: kusan rabin bankuna na bayan ISO 20022") don cika wajibai na bayanan tsararru, adireshin tsararru, da CBPR+ da hanyar sadarwa ke buƙata.

Wannan lambar — 44% bisa ga binciken masana'antu na baya-bayan nan — ita ce mafi mahimmancin gaskiya guda ɗaya a cikin biyan kuɗi tsakanin ƙasashe a wannan shekara. Ba labarin fasaha ba ne. Labarin lissafin kwamiti ne. Bankunan da suka isa 22 ga Nuwamba har yanzu suna fitar da saƙonnin MT103 ko pain.001 tare da tubalan adireshi marasa tsari za a yanke su daga 'yan jaridun MX kawai, za a sanya musu ƙarin kuɗi daga sauran, kuma ba za su iya ciyar da kowane injin baitulmali na wakilci wanda ya dogara da manufa, aikawa, da bayanan tsari masu karantawa ta inji ba.

Labarin 2023 a wannan rukunin yanar gizo, [Sarrafa Halittar Fayil ɗin Biyan Kuɗi Mai Bin ISO 20022 da pain.001](https://sebastienrousseau.com/2023-09-29-automating-iso-20022-compliant-payment-file-creation-with-pain001), ya tsara pain.001 a matsayin matsalar samarwa. A 2026 tsarin ya bambanta. pain.001 yanzu shi ne bugun zuciyar wani tarin kuɗi mai shirye-shirye — abin da [Ma'aunin Baitulmali Mai Aiki da Kansa 2026](https://sebastienrousseau.com/2026-06-07-autonomous-treasury-index-programmable-liquidity-tokenised-deposits-2026) ke kira tsarin jijiya mai aiki da kansa na baitulmalin CIB. Saƙonnin sune siginar. Tsarin shi ne wayoyin.

## 01. Ƙarshen tare-tare

Lokacin tare-tare na MT/MX na SWIFT ya ƙare a ranar 22 ga Nuwamba 2026. Bayan wannan ranar, nau'ikan tsakanin ƙasashe na FIN MT — MT103, MT202, MT202COV, da saƙonnin rahoton MT9xx masu alaƙa — an yi ritayar su daga amfani tsakanin ƙasashe. [Bayanin "babi na ƙarshe" na Banking Vision](https://banking.vision/en/iso-20022-the-final-chapter-begins/ "Banking Vision: ISO 20022 — babi na ƙarshe ya fara") yana bayyana shi daidai: wannan ba wani kari ba ne. Ƙofar fassarar hanyar sadarwa za ta ci gaba da aiki, amma kowane banki da ke aikawa ko karɓar saƙon da aka fassara yana biyan kuɗin gata sau biyu — sau ɗaya cikin kuɗi, sau ɗaya cikin asarar amincin bayanai.

Matsalar tsari ita ce bayanai. MT103 yana ɗauke da haruffa 35 na aikawa marar tsari a filin 70 da adireshi mai rubutu kyauta a filin 50K. pacs.008 yana ɗauke da `<RmtInf>` tare da tsararren bashin bashi, `<PstlAdr>` tare da titi, lambar gidan waya, gari, da lambar ƙasa a matsayin abubuwa daban-daban, da `<RgltryRptg>` don wajibai na musamman na ikon shari'a. Haɓakar CBPR+ na 2024 ya juya abin da ya kasance "zai iya" filaye zuwa "dole ne" filaye. Bankunan da ke fassara zuwa MT103 suna rasa bayanan da suke buƙata don gamsuwa da [FATF Recommendation 16](https://www.fatf-gafi.org/en/publications/Fatfrecommendations/Fatf-recommendations.html "Shawarwarin FATF") akan bayanan mai fara aiki da mai amfani.

Tare-tare alheri ne. Ya ƙare.

## 02. ISO a matsayin tushen bayanai don wakilai

Aikin ban sha'awa a baitulmalin 2026 yana zaune sama da tsarin. Injinan kuɗi masu shirye-shirye, masu inganta lamuni a cikin rana, da kwararar baitulmalin wakilci duk sun dogara da bayanan biyan kuɗi masu karantawa ta inji, masu inganci ta tsari. A aikace, baitulmali na wakilci yana inganta matsayin lamuni a cikin rana ta atomatik ta hanyar daidaita lambobin `<Purp>` masu tsari da bayanan aikawa da bukatun kuɗaɗen lokaci na ainihi — yana motsa kuɗi, yana zana kan layukan bashi, ko yana riƙe da aiwatarwa ba tare da ɗan adam a cikin madauki ba. MT103 ba zai iya samar da shi ba. pacs.008 zai iya.

[Rahoton BIS CPMI akan jituwa na ISO 20022 don biyan kuɗi tsakanin ƙasashe](https://www.bis.org/cpmi/publ/d230.pdf "BIS CPMI: bukatun bayanan ISO 20022 da aka daidaita") ya buga bukatun saƙo da bayanai na canonical a 2023. Ƙarin na 2026 yana yin batun ɗaya da haƙori mai kaifi: jituwa ba shawara ba ce kuma, sharadi ne na farko don manufofin [taswirar biyan kuɗi tsakanin ƙasashe ta G20](https://www.bis.org/cpmi/publ/d193.htm "BIS CPMI: taswirar biyan kuɗi tsakanin ƙasashe ta G20") akan farashi, sauri, bayyananne, da samun damar. Ba tare da lambobin `<Purp>` masu tsari ba, adireshin tsararru, da aikawa mai tsari, wakili ba shi da abin da zai yi tunani akai. Yana da rubutu.

A nan ne tunanin [Ma'aunin Baitulmali Mai Aiki da Kansa 2026](https://sebastienrousseau.com/2026-06-07-autonomous-treasury-index-programmable-liquidity-tokenised-deposits-2026) ya sauka. Kuɗi mai shirye-shirye ba sihiri ba ne. Horon ne na ciyar da wakilai saƙonnin ISO 20022 na canonical, masu inganci ta tsari, da barin manufa-a-matsayin-lambar ta jagoranci abin da wakilan za su iya motsi da ga wa. Saƙon MX shi ne motsin jijiya. Filin sarrafa baitulmali shi ne ƙashin baya. Gudanar da haɗarin samfurin SR 11-7 da lissafin kwamiti na Mataki na 5 na DORA suna zaune a sama a matsayin babban tsarin jijiya.

Cire MX kuma wakilai sun makance.

## 03. MX na asali ko ɗan ƙasa na biyu

Hakikanin gudanarwa biyu sun sake fasalta tattalin arziki a wannan kwata. Na farko, manyan bankunan 'yan jaridu sun buga jadawalin ƙarin kuɗi don 'yan adawa na MT-kawai masu aiki daga Q4 2026 — yawanci haɓaka kowane saƙo akan zirga-zirgar da aka fassara, tare da kuɗin ƙi don saƙonnin da suka kasa tabbatar da adireshin tsararru na CBPR+. Na biyu, tashar SWIFT FINplus tana ƙin pacs.008 da ba a yi shi ba kai tsaye, ba tare da koma baya na MT don sabbin kwararar tsakanin ƙasashe ba.

Wannan yana canza farashin halayyar masu jinkiri daga aikin da ya wuce zuwa jan ƙafa mai maimaitawa akan riba. Bankin ma'amala na matsakaicin matsayi yana sarrafa biyan kuɗi miliyan biyu na tsakanin ƙasashe a wata a haɓakar kowane saƙo na ko da 'yan kuɗaɗe yana duban ƙarin farashin shekara-shekara mai lambobi bakwai, kafin farashin ƙwarewar abokin ciniki na biyan kuɗi da suka kasa da farashin suna na zama mai biyan harajin fassara.

Dokokin tabbatar da CBPR+ kansu ba a iya tattaunawa. `<PstlAdr>` mai tsari tare da `<Ctry>` da aƙalla ɗaya daga cikin `<StrtNm>`/`<TwnNm>`/`<PstCd>` an cika. LEI a cikin `<OrgId>/<LEI>` inda mai fara aiki ko ƙarshen bashi shi ne mahaɗi na shari'a. Lambobin kuɗi na ISO 4217. Kwanakin ISO 8601 tare da yankin lokaci. Komai banda haka ya kasa a ƙofar hanyar sadarwa, ba a bankin manufa ba — wanda ke nufin bankin aikawa yana biyan farashin ƙin yarda kuma abokin ciniki yana ganin biyan kuɗin da ya kasa da farko.

Babu saukowa mai laushi.

## 04. Tsara APIs na baitulmali ISO-na-farko

Tsarin injiniya mai kyau na 2026 shi ne ISO-na-farko. Tsarin na ciki, kwangilar API, da saƙo-a-kan-waya duk suna raba samfurin canonical iri ɗaya: `pain.001` don farawar abokin ciniki-zuwa-banki, `pacs.008` don sasantawa banki-zuwa-banki, `camt.054` don sanarwar bashi, `camt.053` don rahoton ƙarshen rana. Ambulan JSON suna da kyau don sashin gogewar mai haɓakawa, amma sunan filaye, adireshin tsararru, lambar manufa, da tubalin rahoton tsari suna kasancewa na canonical daga ƙarshe zuwa ƙarshe.

Wani guntu na pain.001.001.09 mafi ƙanƙanta yana nuna wajibcin adireshin tsararru:

```xml
<CdtTrfTxInf>
  <PmtId>
    <EndToEndId>E2E-2026-06-23-0001</EndToEndId>
  </PmtId>
  <Amt>
    <InstdAmt Ccy="EUR">125000.00</InstdAmt>
  </Amt>
  <Cdtr>
    <Nm>Acme Manufacturing SA</Nm>
    <PstlAdr>
      <StrtNm>Rue de la Loi</StrtNm>
      <BldgNb>200</BldgNb>
      <PstCd>1049</PstCd>
      <TwnNm>Brussels</TwnNm>
      <Ctry>BE</Ctry>
    </PstlAdr>
    <Id>
      <OrgId>
        <LEI>529900T8BM49AURSDO55</LEI>
      </OrgId>
    </Id>
  </Cdtr>
  <CdtrAcct>
    <Id><IBAN>BE71096123456769</IBAN></Id>
  </CdtrAcct>
  <Purp>
    <Cd>GDDS</Cd>
  </Purp>
  <RmtInf>
    <Strd>
      <CdtrRefInf>
        <Tp><CdOrPrtry><Cd>SCOR</Cd></CdOrPrtry></Tp>
        <Ref>RF18539007547034</Ref>
      </CdtrRefInf>
```

Ƙa'idoji biyu sun fito daga wannan. Na farko, tubalin `<PstlAdr>` ba na zaɓi ba ne daga lokaci na 3 na CBPR+ zuwa gaba. Kowane API na ciki da ke karɓar layi guda na adireshi mai rubutu kyauta shi ne ƙin yarda na gaba. Na biyu, lambar `<Purp>` da tubalin `<RmtInf><Strd>` su ne abin da ke sa saƙon ya zama mai karantawa ta inji ga wakilin baitulmali. Lambar manufa ta `GDDS` tare da tsararren bashin bashi na `SCOR` za a iya daidaita su ba tare da sa hannun ɗan adam ba. Bayanin haruffa 35 na rubutu kyauta ba haka ba.

Saman API mai amfani don dandalin banki na kamfanoni na 2026 wani siririn rufin REST ne akan tsarin canonical. `POST /v1/payments/credit-transfer` yana karɓar jikin JSON wanda ke yin taswira ɗaya-zuwa-ɗaya zuwa abubuwan pain.001. Sabar tana tabbatar da CBPR+ XSD a shigarwa, tana adana XML na canonical, tana sa hannu don rashin musantawa, kuma tana fitar da abun audit na WORM. Iri ɗaya endpoint yana fitar da `camt.054` da `camt.053` callbacks akan samfurin canonical. Babu fassara. Babu juyawa.

Wannan shi ne ISO-na-farko a samarwa.

## Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi

**Me ke canzawa a 22 ga Nuwamba 2026 da bai canza ba a Nuwamba 2025?**
Nuwamba 2025 shi ne farkon raguwa na lokacin tare-tare na FIN MT/MX don nau'ikan tsakanin ƙasashe. Nuwamba 2026 shi ne ƙarshen. Bayan wannan ranar, FIN MT103, MT202, MT202COV da jerin rahoton MT9xx an yi ritayar su daga amfani tsakanin ƙasashe. Ƙofar fassarar hanyar sadarwa za ta ci gaba da aiki, amma kowane saƙo da aka fassara yana biya cikin kuɗi da kuma cikin asarar amincin bayanai. Filayen adireshi masu tsari da aikawa masu tsari na CBPR+ sun daina zama zaɓi.

**Pain.001 abu ɗaya ne da pacs.008?**
A'a. pain.001 shi ne saƙon farawar canjawar bashin abokin ciniki — ERP na kamfani zuwa banki. pacs.008 shi ne canjawar bashi tsakanin bankuna — banki zuwa banki, ta SWIFT ko hanyar daidai. Su biyun suna raba nahawu na ISO 20022 da yawancin abubuwan tsari (`<PstlAdr>`, `<RmtInf>`, `<Purp>`, `<Dbtr>` / `<Cdtr>` / `<DbtrAgt>` / `<CdtrAgt>`) amma saƙonni daban ne a kafafu daban-daban. Dandalin baitulmali na 2026 yana tabbatar da pain.001 na kamfani a shigarwa kuma yana fitar da pacs.008 a kan tsalle tsakanin bankuna ba tare da sake taswira ba.

**Me yasa tubalin `<PstlAdr>` mai tsari yake da muhimmanci sosai?**
Saboda FATF Recommendation 16 da lokaci na 3 na CBPR+ duka biyu suna buƙatar bayanan adireshi masu tsari akan filayen mai fara aiki da mai amfani tsakanin ƙasashe. Layi na adireshi mai rubutu kyauta ba za a iya tabbatar da shi, gani, ko daidaita shi a sikelin ba. Abubuwa masu tsari na `StrtNm` / `PstCd` / `TwnNm` / `Ctry` za a iya. Daga Nuwamba 2026, bankunan da ke fitar da adireshi marasa tsari ana ƙin su a kan rarrabewa ta 'yan jaridun MX-kawai kuma ana sanya musu ƙarin kuɗi daga waɗanda ke jurewa fassara.

**Menene ma'anar "ISO-na-farko" ga API na ciki?**
Yana nufin samfurin canonical a gefen banki na API shi ne bishiyar abubuwan ISO 20022, ba JSON na mallaka na banki mai laushi ba. `POST /v1/payments/credit-transfer` yana karɓar jikin buƙata wanda ke yin taswira ɗaya-zuwa-ɗaya zuwa pain.001. Sabar tana tabbatar da CBPR+ XSD a shigarwa, tana adana XML na canonical, kuma tana fitar da pacs.008 zuwa hanyar. Babu fassarar gefe, babu juyawa ta ma'ana tsakanin buƙatar kamfani da abin da ya isa ga ɗan jarida.

**Inda hakan ya bar bankin da bai fara ba?**
Watanni biyar ya isa lokaci don aikawa da bayanin saƙo mafi tsanani-da-CBPR+ da shigar ƙin-yarda-a-rarrabewa, gudanar da-biyu tabbatar da CBPR+ akan zirga-zirgar ɗan jarida mai rai, da kafar sasantawa ta-pacs.008-na asali don manyan corridors 20. Bai isa lokaci ba don sake gina dandali. Bankunan da ke wannan matsayi ya kamata su tsara: tabbatar-a-rarrabewa na farko (yana dakatar da zubar jini akan zirga-zirgar fitarwa), gyaran adireshin tsararru na biyu (yana rufe gibin ƙa'ida), cikakken sasantawa na-pacs.008-na asali na uku (yana kama fa'idar kuɗi-mai-shirye-shirye bayan wa'adin).

## Ƙarshe

Wa'adin Nuwamba 2026 shi ne sashin sauƙi. Sashin mai wuya shi ne abin da wa'adin ke tilastawa. Bankunan da suka isa cikin lokaci har yanzu suna ɗaukar pain.001 a matsayin matsalar fassara za su shafe shekaru goma masu zuwa suna sake gina samfurin bayanan baitulmalin su daga waya zuwa ciki. Bankunan da suka isa da tsarin canonical na ISO-na-farko, adireshin tsararru ta tsohuwa, da filin sarrafa kuɗi mai shirye-shirye wanda ke cinye pacs.008 kai tsaye za su gudanar da baitulmalin wakilci a ƙarƙashin lissafin kwamiti na Mataki na 5 na DORA, horon haɗarin gudanarwa na [Basel III](https://www.bis.org/bcbs/publ/d424.htm "Basel III: kammala gyare-gyaren bayan rikicin"), da gudanar da samfurin [SR 11-7](https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/sr1107.htm "SR 11-7 Jagora akan Gudanar da Haɗarin Samfurin").

Tsarin tsarin jijiya mai aiki da kansa ba ado ba ne. Baitulmali ba zai iya yin tunani game da kuɗin da ba zai iya gani ba. Wakilai ba za su iya yin aiki akan bayanan da ba za su iya rarrabewa ba. ISO 20022 shi ne wayoyin baitulmalin CIB a 2026 — saƙon tsararru shi ne yiwuwar aiki, tsarin shi ne hanyar binciken da mai gudanarwa zai bukata da safe bayan abin da ya faru na gaba.

Watanni biyar. Gina tsarin, ba hanyar magancewa ba.
