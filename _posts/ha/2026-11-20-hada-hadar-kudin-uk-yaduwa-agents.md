## Menene binciken, kuma me ba shi ba

Wallafar taƙaitaccen bayani ce na jimillar sakamakon sabon binciken AI da aka gudanar a matsayin wani ɓangare na faɗaɗɗen shirin aikin Bank of England da FCA kan AI. Wannan shirin ya ƙunshi binciken AI/ML na baya a 2019, 2022 da 2024. Binciken 2026 ya riƙe tambayoyin ginshiƙi daga zagayen baya don ci gaba, kuma ya ƙara ko ya gyara wasu don nuna ci gaban karɓuwa da ƙarfin AI tun 2024.

Kamfanoni 196 ne suka amsa, an raba su zuwa manyan kamfanoni 101 da ƙananan kamfanoni 95. Daga cikinsu, 105 sun cika cikakken binciken. Sai dai idan an faɗi akasin haka, alƙaluman sun dogara ne kan amsoshin cikakken binciken, don sauƙaƙa kwatance da binciken baya, kuma ana lissafa kaso ne daga ingantattun amsoshi na kowace tambaya kaɗai, wato kamfanonin da suka tsallake tambaya ba sa cikin denominator na wannan tambayar. Amsoshin bankunan ƙasa da ƙasa da broker/dealers suna nufin ayyukansu na UK.

Gargaɗi biyu ne ke ƙayyade iyakar da za a iya tura waɗannan alƙaluma. Ya kamata a karanta sakamakon a matsayin shaida daga kamfanonin da suka shiga, ba cikakken kimantawar karɓuwar AI a dukkan fannin kuɗi na UK ba. Kuma kwatance da 2024 yana buƙatar taka-tsantsan inda kalmomin tambayoyi, ma'anoni, iyaka ko rukunin waɗanda suka amsa suka bambanta. Zan nuna sauye-sauyen shekara zuwa shekara a ƙasa saboda su ne alƙaluma mafiya ban mamaki a rahoton, amma wannan sharaɗin yana tare da su kowane lokaci.

Don manufar binciken, tsarin AI shi ne tsarin da ke aiki da na'ura wanda, don manufofi bayyanannu ko ɓoyayyu, ke fahimta daga bayanan da ya karɓa yadda zai samar da sakamako kamar hasashe, abun ciki, shawarwari ko yanke shawara da ke iya shafar muhalli na zahiri ko na kama-da-wane. Wannan ma'ana ce mai faɗi, kuma ya dace a tuna da ita idan aka ga alƙalami kamar 86%. Tana bayyana amfani da wani faɗaɗɗen rukunin tsarin, ba lallai amfani da wata fasaha guda ɗaya ba.

## Karɓuwa ta zama ita ce al'ada

Kanun labarin a sarari yake. 86% na waɗanda suka amsa sun ce suna amfani da AI, sama da 75% a 2024. Taƙaitawar rahoton da kansa ita ce kamfanoni na ƙara ba da rahoton amfani da generative AI, agents masu amfani da AI da third-party implementations na tsarin AI. Waɗannan jigogi uku ne ke tsara yawancin abin da ke biye.

A cikin waɗanda suka cika cikakken binciken, turawa ta kusa zama cikakkiya: 94% sun ce suna da aƙalla tsarin AI guda ɗaya a production, ya kai 99% idan aka haɗa da gwaje-gwaje (pilots) da turawar da aka tsara cikin watanni 12 masu zuwa. Amfanin da aka fi ambata a yanzu shi ne enterprise productivity, kamar coding assistants ko kayan aikin taƙaitawa, wanda 86% na waɗanda suka amsa suka zaɓa.

Ƙananan kamfanoni ba su yi nisa a baya ba. A cikin waɗanda suka amsa sabon gajeren binciken (short-form survey) na kamfanonin da ke da ma'aikata ƙasa da 100, 73% sun ce suna amfani da AI, kuma daga cikinsu, 58% sun ce galibi suna amfani da manyan general-purpose large language models. Ga ƙaramin kamfani, wannan tsari ne da ya cancanci lura: fasahar da aka fi danganta da wannan guguwar karɓuwa ita ce kuma wadda yawancin ƙananan masu karɓa suka fi dogara da ita. Operations shi ne fannin da aka fi ambata don amfaninsu na AI na yanzu ko wanda aka tsara, a 64%.

Inda tsarin suke yana ba da labari makamancin haka. Dangane da tsarin da aka bayar da rahotonsu, Operations da IT sun kai kusan 31%, sama da kusan 22% a 2024. Babu wani fannin kasuwanci guda da ya kai sama da 11%. Wannan babban gibi ne. Ko mene ne tattaunawar jama'a kan AI a ayyukan da ke fuskantar abokan ciniki ko na ciniki, nauyin binciken yana cikin back office.

Fa'idojin da ake gani sun yi daidai da wannan wuri. Ingancin aiki, automation da productivity su ne fannonin fa'ida mafiya girma da ake gani daga AI a yau. Zai zama abin mamaki idan kamfanoni sun tara tsarinsu a operations da IT sannan suka ce manyan ribarsu na wani wuri daban. Ba haka suka yi ba.

## Generative AI yanzu shi ne mafi yawan tsarin

Sauyin haɗin fasaha babba ne. Generative AI ya kai 56% na dukkan tsarin AI da aka bayar da rahotonsu, idan aka kwatanta da 17% na foundation models a 2024. Zan yi taka-tsantsan kada in karanta hakan a matsayin tsalle mai kama da juna: alƙalamin 2024 ya ƙirga use cases ne maimakon tsarin, kuma rahoton ya ce ma'aunan biyu ba za a iya kwatanta su kai tsaye ba, sai dai tare suna nuna ƙaruwa mai yawa a amfanin kamfanoni da ƙarfin general-purpose. Amma ko da wannan taka-tsantsan, rukunin da ya kai fiye da rabin dukkan tsarin da aka bayar da rahotonsu ba ƙaramin gwaji ba ne kuma.

Haka kuma bai bazu daidai a tsakanin waɗanda suka amsa ba. Manyan waɗanda suka amsa goma sun kai 43% na dukkan tsarin AI da aka bayar da rahotonsu. Ƙaramin rukunin kamfanoni ne ke gudanar da babban kaso na tsarin da aka ƙirga, don haka hoton jimilla wani ɓangare hoton waɗannan goman ne. Mai karatu da ke neman abin da kamfani na yau da kullum ke yi ya kamata ya tuna da wannan taruwar.

Wani sauyi yana tafiya ta akasin abin da mutum zai yi tsammani. Kusan 7% na tsarin AI da aka bayar da rahotonsu waɗanda suka amsa sun tantance su a matsayin high materiality, idan aka kwatanta da 16% a 2024. Wannan raguwar kaso ce, ba raguwar adadi ba: rahoton ya ce jimillar adadin tsarin high-materiality da aka bayar da rahotonsu a 2026 ya fi na 2024. Binciken bai faɗi dalilin raguwar kason ba. Faɗaɗɗen tsarin da amfanin operations da IT suka mamaye zai iya, a hankali, rage kason da ya fi muhimmanci, amma wannan hasashena ne, ba abin da bayanan suka tabbatar ba. Tsarin high-materiality ma sun taru: kamfanoni goma sun kai 62% daga cikinsu.

## Agents: shawara da yawa, 'yancin kai kaɗan

Alƙaluman agents su ne misali mafi bayyana na kamfanonin da ke karɓar sabon abu yayin da suke riƙe da shi a hannu. 80% sun ce suna da AI agents a production da ke ba da decision support, ma'ana agents suna ba da shawarar matakai, mutane kuma suna yanke shawara da aiwatarwa. Tsakanin waɗannan iyakoki, 46% sun ce suna da agents masu constrained execution (aiki cikin iyakoki da ƙa'idoji da aka riga aka ƙayyade), 22% kuma sun ce suna da agents masu conditional autonomy (aiki da kansu amma suna buƙatar amincewar ɗan adam kan wasu matakai). 2% kaɗai sun ce sun tura agents masu broad autonomy.

Ina ganin 80% da 2% su ne alƙaluma biyu mafiya muhimmanci a rahoton, saboda suna nuna inda layin yake a yanzu. Agents sun zama ruwan dare; agents masu cin gashin kansu ba safai ba ne. Babban al'adar waɗanda suka amsa ita ce saka agents cikin ayyuka a matsayin masu ba da shawara, tare da taƙaita aiwatarwa cikin iyakokin da aka riga aka ƙayyade, inda aka ba da izinin aiwatarwa ma kwata-kwata.

Sadarwa da abokan ciniki tana ba da hangen nesa na musamman kan inda wannan layin zai iya motsawa. 16% sun ce suna amfani da agents a yanzu don sadarwa da abokan ciniki ta atomatik, ya kai 34% idan aka haɗa da turawar da aka tsara. Turawar da aka tsara ta ninka alƙalamin yanzu fiye da sau biyu. Tsare-tsare ba sakamako ba ne, don haka 34% bayani ne na niyya, ba karɓuwa ba. Amma yana nuna sadarwa da abokan ciniki a matsayin fanni ɗaya da kamfanoni ke sa ran ba wa agents ƙarin aiki. Shawarwari mafiya wuya ba sa cikin shirin: ko da an haɗa da turawar da aka tsara cikin watanni 12 masu zuwa, bai fi 9% na waɗanda suka amsa ba ne ke sa ran agents su amince ko su ƙi sakamakon abokan ciniki, su aiwatar da ciniki, su fara ko su toshe biyan kuɗi, ko su sauya iyakoki da matakan kariya.

Wannan yana da muhimmanci ga tattaunawar matakan kariya a ƙasa. Tsarin decision support ya dogara ne kan ɗan adam ya yanke shawara, kuma sadarwa da abokan ciniki ta atomatik ita ce irin amfanin da ɗan adam zai iya kasancewa ba kai tsaye a cikin zagayen ba. Ko matakan kariya da kamfanoni ke ba da rahotonsu a yau an gina su don wannan sauyi tambaya ce da alƙaluman binciken suka taso amma ba za su iya amsawa ba.

## Tambayar ɓangare na uku

Idan akwai wani yanayi guda da ya cancanci a duba shi sosai, to wannan ne. Third-party implementations sun kai 39% na dukkan tsarin AI da aka bayar da rahotonsu, sama da 33% a 2024. Wannan ƙaruwa ce matsakaiciya. Alƙalamin tsarin high-materiality ba matsakaici ba ne: third-party implementations sun kai 53% na tsarin AI na high-materiality, sama da 35% a 2024.

A sauƙaƙe, masu samarwa daga waje suna samar da ƙasa da huɗu cikin goma na dukkan tsarin, amma fiye da rabin waɗanda kamfanoni ke ɗauka mafiya muhimmanci. Dogaron ya taru daidai inda haɗari ya fi girma, kuma ya ƙaru da maki goma sha takwas cikin shekaru biyu. Saboda gargaɗin kan kwatanta zagaye, ba zan dogara da ainihin girman wannan tsalle ba, amma alƙiblar da matakin 53% an bayyana su a sarari.

Waɗanda suka amsa suna ganin takurawar da kansu. Damuwa kan tsaron bayanai (information security) da juriyar aiki (operational resilience) 40% na waɗanda suka amsa sun gano su a matsayin babbar takura ga karɓar AI na ɓangare na uku, sai ƙarancin bayyana gaskiya (transparency) cikin tsarin AI na ɓangare na uku a 34%. Don haka fannin da ke ƙara dogaro ga masu samarwa don tsarinsa mafiya muhimmanci shi ne kuma ke ambaton tsaron masu samarwa da rashin bayyanarsu a matsayin manyan birkunansa kan karɓar ɓangare na uku.

Ƙimar haɗari tana nuna alƙibla ɗaya. Fannonin haɗari mafiya girma da ake gani ga kamfanoni daga AI a yau su ne kariyar bayanai da tsaro, haɗarin ɓangare na uku da sarkar samarwa (supply chain), da haɗarin musamman na GenAI. Idan aka duba gaba, waɗanda suka amsa suna sa ran cyber risk amplification da critical third-party dependencies za su tsananta yayin da amfani da AI ke ƙara yaɗuwa, kuma waɗannan su ne systemic risks biyu mafiya girma a yau da kuma cikin shekaru uku masu zuwa. Ga cyber risk amplification, 57% sun ƙimanta damuwarsu a matsayin babba ko mai tsanani a yau, ya kai 80% cikin shekara ɗaya zuwa uku masu zuwa; ga critical third-party dependencies, kason ya tashi daga 40% zuwa 63%. Wannan tsammani ne, kuma binciken ya tsara shi a matsayin tsammani, amma ya yi daidai a irinsa da alƙaluman dogaro na sama.

Ga duk mai sayar da AI ga fannin hada-hadar kuɗi, abin da zan ciro na kasuwanci ne maimakon na ƙa'idoji: masu saye sun ce rashin bayyana gaskiya cikin tsarin ɓangare na uku yana takura su, kuma mai samarwa da zai iya amsa wannan damuwa kai tsaye yana magance abin da 34% na waɗanda suka amsa suka ambata. Wannan fassarata ce, ba sakamakon binciken ba.

## Gudanarwa: ƙarfi a takarda, sirara a aiki

A tsari, waɗanda suka amsa suna da tsari mai kyau. 94% sun ce suna da manufofi, ƙa'idodi ko matsayin AI na dukkan kamfani, kuma 84% sun ce suna da dandalin gudanarwar AI/model-risk na tsakiya (centralised governance fora). Daga cikin matakan kariya da ake amfani da su wajen sarrafa haɗarin bayanai a tsarin AI, amincewar ɗan adam, bita kafin turawa (pre-deployment review) da vendor due diligence kowanne fiye da 90% na waɗanda suka amsa sun ambata.

Hoton yana sauyawa da zarar ka wuce matakan kariya da ake amfani da su kafin tsari ya fara aiki zuwa waɗanda ake amfani da su bayan haka. Ongoing monitoring, gwajin zubewar bayanai (leakage testing) da bita na lokaci-lokaci ko sabunta takardar shaida (recertification) kowanne kusan rabin waɗanda suka amsa ne suka ambata. Gibin kusan maki arba'in ne ke raba kariyar farko da masu ɗorewa.

Zan ce wannan shi ne mafi raunin wuri a muhallin matakan kariya da aka bayar da rahotonsa. Bincike kafin turawa yana da sauƙin bayyanawa da sauƙin tantancewa (audit). Sa ido kan tsari a production kuɗi ne mai ci gaba, kuma alƙaluman suna nuna kusan rabin kamfanoni ne ke ba da rahotonsa. Inda tsarin ɓangare na uku suka mamaye rukunin high-materiality, gibin ya fi muhimmanci, saboda kamfanoni ba koyaushe ne za su iya duba abin da ba su gina ba. Binciken ya nuna hakan kai tsaye: ga tsarin high-materiality, 77% na waɗanda suka amsa sun ce sun dogara da zurfin sani kan tsarin da aka haɓaka a cikin gida, idan aka kwatanta da 24% ga tsarin ɓangare na uku, inda 81% suka dogara a maimakon haka da tabbacin mai samarwa (vendor assurance) da yarjejeniyoyin kwangila.

Kamfanoni sun gane wahalar. Sa ido kan AI na ɓangare na uku shi ne fannin rashin tabbas na gudanarwa ko ƙalubalen aiwatarwa da aka fi ambata: amfani da ƙa'idodin model risk management kan models na ɓangare na uku ko waɗanda masu samarwa suka bayar 54% na waɗanda suka amsa suka zaɓa a matsayin ƙalubale, kuma sa ido kan tsarin da ɓangare na uku suka samar ko suka yi tasiri sosai a kansu 49%. Due diligence a lokacin saye ya kusa zama na kowa; bin diddigi bayan haka shi ne inda kamfanoni suka ce ba su da cikakken tabbaci.

## Takurawar da ba ta ƙa'idoji ba ce

Zai zama da sauƙi a ɗauka cewa ƙa'idoji (regulation) su ne babban birki kan karɓuwa. Binciken yana ba da hoto mai gauraye. 42% na waɗanda suka amsa sun ga kariyar bayanai da sirri na UK a matsayin nauyin bin ƙa'ida mai girma, kuma 29% sun faɗi haka game da ƙa'idojin ƙasa da ƙasa. Waɗannan kaso ne na gaske, amma marasa rinjaye ne, kuma 28% na waɗanda suka amsa sun nuna babu takurawar ƙa'idoji kwata-kwata.

Takurawar aiki tana bayyana a ƙananan kaso. Takurawa mafiya ambato da ba na ƙa'idoji ba, wato wahalar haɗa AI cikin hanyoyin kasuwanci da yanke shawara da ake da su, da takurawar tsofaffin tsarin (legacy systems) ko ababen more rayuwa, 23% da 22% na waɗanda suka amsa suka gano su a matsayin manyan takurawa bi da bi. A cikin ƙananan kamfanoni, 30% sun gano kariyar bayanai, sirri da batutuwan IT a matsayin takurawa.

Idan aka karanta su tare, alƙaluman suna nuna cewa cikas mafiya ambato su ne kariyar bayanai da tsaron masu samarwa, tare da haɗawa da legacy infrastructure a baya. Ba zan wuce gona da iri wajen fassara jerin ba, tunda ba a yi tambayoyin iri ɗaya ba (wasu suna tambaya kan nauyin bin ƙa'ida, wasu kan manyan takurawa), amma rashin wani cikas guda mai rinjaye ya yi daidai da karɓuwar 86%.

## Tsammani: ƙarin fa'ida, ƙarin haɗari

Waɗanda suka amsa suna sa ran fa'idoji su ƙaru a dukkan fannoni cikin shekaru uku masu zuwa, inda ƙaruwa mafiya girma da ake tsammani ke cikin cybersecurity da operational resilience, da samar da kuɗaɗen shiga da matsayin gasa. Waɗannan tsammani ne, kuma binciken ya ɗauke su haka; suna nuna inda kamfanoni ke fatan AI zai je, ba inda ya riga ya je ba.

Tsammanin haɗari yana tafiya tare. Cikin shekara ɗaya zuwa uku masu zuwa, waɗanda suka amsa suna sa ran matakan haɗari su ƙaru a dukkan rukunoni. Tushen ya ce ci gaban AI zai iya kawo fa'idoji kamar ƙarin inganci, ingantaccen tallafin yanke shawara da ƙarfafa sarrafa haɗari, kuma zai iya haifar ko ƙara haɗari da suka shafi gudanarwa, operational resilience, kariyar bayanai, sakamakon masu amfani, dogaro ga ɓangare na uku da daidaiton harkokin kuɗi. An sassauta rabi biyun wannan jumla, kuma hakan daidai ne.

Akwai abin lura a wannan haɗuwa. Cybersecurity da operational resilience suna cikin ƙaruwar fa'ida mafiya girma da ake tsammani, kuma cyber risk amplification ya bayyana a matsayin systemic risk mafi girma. Kamfanoni suna sa ran AI ya taimaka da cyber resilience kuma ya ƙara tsananta haɗarin cyber a lokaci guda. Binciken bai daidaita waɗannan tsammani ba, kuma ba zan yi kamar na yi ba. Suna zaune gefe da gefe.

## Frontier AI da cyber

Binciken ya tambaya musamman kan ƙarfin cyber na frontier AI. 88% na waɗanda suka amsa sun tantance ƙarfin cyber na frontier AI da ke canzawa cikin shekaru biyu masu zuwa a matsayin mai tasiri matsakaici ko babba kan bayanin haɗarin kamfaninsu (risk profile). Wannan kimantawa ce ta gaba, ba sakamakon da aka auna ba, kuma ya kamata a karanta ta a matsayin tsammanin waɗanda suka amsa.

Duk da haka, ta riga ta canza wasu halaye. 64% sun zaɓi vulnerability management da saurin patching a matsayin fannin cyber resilience da ya samu ƙaruwar hankalin shugabanni mafi girma sakamakon sanin frontier AI. Don haka damuwar ta haifar da sauyi na zahiri a hankalin shugabanni zuwa patching, a cewar kusan kashi biyu cikin uku na waɗanda aka tambaya.

Wani ƙaramin sakamako daban yana zaune kusa: 57% na waɗanda suka amsa sun ce suna la'akari da tasirin dorewa (sustainability) na amfani da AI. Rinjaye ne, amma da ƙyar, kuma kaɗan ne ke faɗin haka a bainar jama'a: 82% ba sa ba da rahoton tasirin dorewa na AI a waje, ko da yake 36% daga cikinsu suna shirin yin haka cikin shekaru biyu.

## Abin da za a sa ido a kai

Alƙaluman suna bayyana masana'antar da ta karɓi AI ko'ina, ta bar mutane ke iko da yanke shawara, kuma ta miƙa ƙaruwar kaso na tsarinta mafiya muhimmanci ga masu samarwa daga waje. Tashin hankalin yana cikin wannan batu na ƙarshe. Matakan kariya da kamfanoni suka fi ambata, kamar manufofi, amincewa da due diligence, suna faruwa da wuri. Waɗanda za su sa ido kan tsarin mai samarwa a tsawon lokaci kusan rabin kamfanoni ne ke ambatonsu.

Alƙaluma biyu za su cancanci a duba su a zagaye na gaba, idan kalmomin sun yi daidai. Na farko shi ne alƙalamin sadarwa da abokan ciniki: 16% a yau, 34% idan aka haɗa da turawar da aka tsara. Idan tsare-tsaren suka zama amfani a production, kason 2% na broad autonomy da kason 80% na decision support za su gaya mana ko tsarin "ɗan adam ne ke yanke shawara" ya tsira bayan ya haɗu da yawan aiki. Na biyu shi ne 53%. Idan third-party implementations suka ci gaba da ɗaukar babban kaso na tsarin high-materiality yayin da ongoing monitoring ke tsaye kusa da rabi, waɗanda suka amsa binciken da kansu, waɗanda tuni suke ɗaukar critical third-party dependencies a matsayin babban systemic risk, za su bayyana matsalar kafin ta iso. Tambayar da ke buɗe ita ce ko al'adar monitoring za ta rufe gibin kafin dogaron ya ƙara faɗaɗa.

## Tambayoyin da Ake Yawan Yi

**Kamfanoni nawa ne ke amfani da AI a cewar binciken Bank of England da FCA na 2026?**
86% na waɗanda suka amsa sun ce suna amfani da AI a yanzu, sama da 75% a 2024 da 58% a 2022. Binciken ya samu amsoshi 196, 101 daga manyan kamfanoni da 95 daga ƙananan kamfanoni. Rahoton ya yi gargaɗin cewa bambance-bambancen haɗin waɗanda suka amsa da iyakar tambayoyi na nufin kason 2026 da na 2024 ba sa nufin rukuni ɗaya, don haka a kwatanta su da taka-tsantsan.

**Wane irin 'yancin kai kamfanonin kuɗi na UK ke ba wa AI agents?**
Kaɗan ƙwarai. 80% na waɗanda suka cika cikakken binciken sun ce sun tura agents don decision support, inda agents ke ba da shawarar matakai, mutane kuma ke yanke shawara da aiwatarwa. 46% sun ce suna da agents masu constrained execution, 22% masu conditional autonomy, kuma 2% kaɗai masu broad autonomy.

**Nawa ne na AI na kamfanoni ke zuwa daga ɓangare na uku?**
Third-party implementations sun kai 39% na dukkan tsarin AI da aka bayar da rahotonsu, sama da 33% a 2024 da 17% a 2022. Sun kuma kai 53% na tsarin AI na high-materiality, sama da 35% a 2024.

**Me kamfanoni ke gani a matsayin manyan haɗarin AI?**
A matakin kamfani, haɗari mafiya girma a yanzu su ne kariyar bayanai da tsaro, haɗarin ɓangare na uku da supply chain, da haɗarin musamman na GenAI kamar hallucinations da prompt injection. A matakin tsarin baki ɗaya, cyber risk amplification da critical third-party dependencies su ne haɗari biyu mafiya girma, a yau da kuma cikin shekaru uku masu zuwa.

**Ina matakan kariya na gudanarwar AI suka fi rauni?**
Amincewar ɗan adam, bita kafin turawa da vendor due diligence kowanne fiye da 90% na waɗanda suka amsa sun ambata, yayin da ongoing monitoring, leakage testing da bita na lokaci-lokaci ko recertification kowanne kusan rabin waɗanda suka amsa ne suka ambata. Sa ido kan AI na ɓangare na uku shi ne ƙalubalen gudanarwa da aka fi ambata.
