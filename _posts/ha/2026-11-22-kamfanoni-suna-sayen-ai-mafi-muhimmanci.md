## Binciken da ke bayyanawa, ba ya umarni

Bank of England da Financial Conduct Authority sun gudanar da bincike na huɗu kan artificial intelligence da machine learning a fannin hada-hadar kuɗi na UK, kuma wallafar ta fito fili kan iyakokinta. Tana ba da taƙaitaccen bayani na jimillar sakamakon binciken, kuma "ya kamata a karanta" sakamakonta "a matsayin shaida daga kamfanonin da suka shiga, ba a matsayin cikakken kimantawar karɓuwar AI a dukkan fannin kuɗi na UK ba".

Tushen yana da muhimmanci. Binciken ya samu amsoshi 196, daga manyan kamfanoni 101 da ƙananan kamfanoni 95. Kamfanonin da ke da ma'aikata ƙasa da 100 za su iya cika gajeriyar takardar tambayoyi, kuma waɗanda suka amsa 105 sun cika cikakken binciken. Sai dai idan an faɗi akasin haka, alƙaluman sun dogara ne kan cikakken binciken. Marubutan sun kuma yi gargaɗin cewa kwatance da 2024 "ya kamata a fassara su da taka-tsantsan inda kalmomin tambaya, ma'anoni, iyaka ko rukunin waɗanda suka amsa suka bambanta". Idan wani alƙalami ya canza tsakanin 2024 da 2026, ina ajiye alƙaluman biyu gefe da gefe kuma in riƙe wannan gargaɗi a zuciya.

Karatuna, wanda tsari ne ba gaskiya ba, shi ne cewa zaren da ya fi muhimmanci a sakamakon shi ne dogaro ga ɓangare na uku. Gabatarwar ta ce kamfanoni "suna ƙara ba da rahoton amfani da generative AI, agents masu amfani da AI da third-party implementations na tsarin AI". Daga cikin waɗannan uku, na ukun ne ya fi haɗuwa kai tsaye da gudanarwa, da juriya (resilience) da abin da waɗanda suka amsa suka ce yana damun su. Sassan da ke ƙasa suna bin wannan zaren.

## Ina tsarin suke, kuma wa ke da mafi yawansu

Gaba ɗaya, 86% na waɗanda suka amsa sun ce suna amfani da AI, sama da 75% a 2024, ko da yake marubutan sun lura cewa kason biyu ba sa nufin rukunin kamfanoni ɗaya. Generative AI ya kai 56% na dukkan tsarin AI da aka bayar da rahotonsu a 2026. A 2024, foundation models sun kai 17% na use cases na AI da aka bayar da rahotonsu. Binciken ya ce waɗannan ma'aunai "suna kama fannoni daban-daban na karɓuwar AI don haka ba za a iya kwatanta su kai tsaye ba", yayin da idan aka haɗa su suke nuna ƙaruwa mai yawa a amfanin ƙarfin general-purpose da aka bayar da rahotonsa.

A fannin kasuwanci, Operations da IT sun kai kusan 31% na dukkan tsarin AI da aka bayar da rahotonsu, sama da kusan 22% a 2024. Babu wani fanni guda da ya wuce 11%, don haka fanni mafi girma ya kusa ninka kowane fanni sau uku aƙalla. A ɓangaren domain, enterprise productivity, kamar coding assistants ko kayan aikin taƙaitawa, shi ne amfanin yanzu da aka fi ambata, wanda 86% na waɗanda suka amsa suka zaɓa.

Rarrabuwa tsakanin kamfanoni ba ta yi daidai ba. Kusan rabin kamfanoni sun ce suna da tsarin AI goma ko ƙasa da haka, yayin da kamfanoni goma da suka fi yawan tsarin suka kai 43% na dukkan tsarin AI da aka bayar da rahotonsu. Kusan 10% na kamfanoni sun ce suna da fiye da 100. Don haka jimillar adadin tsarin tana nuna ƙananan adadin manyan kadarori tare da ƙanana masu yawa.

Materiality na ba da labari mai zurfi. Kusan 7% na tsarin AI da aka bayar da rahotonsu waɗanda suka amsa suka tantance a matsayin high materiality, idan aka kwatanta da 16% a 2024. Idan aka karanta shi shi kaɗai, yana kama da faɗuwar muhimmancin AI ga kamfanoni. Binciken ya kawar da wannan karatu: jimillar adadin tsarin AI na high-materiality da aka bayar da rahotonsu a 2026 ya fi na 2024. Kason ya faɗi ne saboda jimillar ta girma da sauri. Tsarin high-materiality ma sun taru wuri ɗaya, inda kamfanoni goma suka kai 62% nasu.

## Agents sun yaɗu, kuma galibi shawara suke bayarwa

Agents ne ke jawo sharhi mafi zafi, don haka ya dace a faɗi alƙaluman a fili. 80% na waɗanda suka amsa sun ce suna da AI agents da aka tura a yanzu, kuma ƙarin 14% sun ce suna shirin tura su cikin watanni 12 masu zuwa.

Matakin 'yancin kai (autonomy) shi ne abin da ke da muhimmanci. 80% na waɗanda suka amsa sun ce sun tura agents don decision support, inda agents ke ba da shawarar matakai, kuma mutane ne ke yanke shawara da aiwatarwa. 46% sun ce suna da agents masu constrained execution, 22% masu conditional autonomy, kuma 2% kaɗai masu broad autonomy. Taƙaitawar binciken da kansa ita ce "turawa ta yaɗu, amma autonomy yana da iyaka".

Amfanin da ke fuskantar abokan ciniki shi ne inda ake gwada iyakar da ke tsakanin shawara da aiki. 16% na waɗanda suka amsa sun ce suna amfani da agents yanzu don sadarwa ta atomatik da abokan ciniki, wanda ya tashi zuwa 34% idan aka haɗa da turawar da aka tsara. Ko da an haɗa turawar da aka tsara cikin watanni 12 masu zuwa, ba fiye da 9% ne suka sa ran agents za su amince ko su ƙi sakamakon abokan ciniki, su aiwatar da cinikayya ko su sanya oda, su fara, su aiwatar ko su toshe biyan kuɗi, ko su canza iyakoki, maƙasudai da matakan kariya.

## Sauyin zuwa ɓangare na uku

Third-party implementations sun kai 39% na tsarin AI da aka bayar da rahotonsu a 2026, idan aka kwatanta da 33% a 2024 da 17% a 2022. Kason tsarin high-materiality ya ƙara matsawa: third-party implementations sun kai 53% na tsarin AI na high-materiality, sama da 35% a 2024. Wannan ya mayar da ɓangare na uku daga 'yan tsiraru zuwa masu rinjaye a cikin tsarin da kamfanonin da kansu suke ɗauka a matsayin high materiality, kuma ya faru ne yayin da jimillar adadin waɗannan tsarin ke ƙaruwa.

Masu samarwa sun taru wuri ɗaya. Manyan masu samar da cloud da compute uku sun kai 79% na dukkan dangantakar cloud/compute da aka bayar da rahotonsu, kuma 98% na waɗanda suka amsa sun ce suna amfani da ɗaya daga cikinsu. Manyan masu samar da model API/hosting uku sun kai 54% na dangantakar da aka bayar da rahotonsu. Daga cikin dangantakar cloud da compute daban-daban 169 da aka bayar da rahotonsu, 37% an ɗauke su a matsayin masu high criticality kuma masu wuyar maye gurbinsu, kuma 35% kaɗai na wannan rukunin ke da tsarin ficewa da aka gwada.

Waɗanda suka amsa suna sane da wannan tashin hankali. Damuwa kan information security da/ko operational resilience ita ce babbar takura kan karɓar AI na ɓangare na uku da aka fi ambata, wadda 40% na waɗanda suka amsa suka zaɓa, sai ƙarancin bayyana gaskiya (transparency) cikin tsarin AI na ɓangare na uku a 34%. Idan aka haɗa manyan takurawa da matsakaita, wahalar tattaunawa kan kariyar kwangila kamar raba alhaki (liability allocation), haƙƙin bincike (audit rights) da sanarwar gaba kan sauye-sauye ita ce ƙalubale na biyu da aka fi ambata.

## Ba a ɗaukar saye kamar kowane saye

Iƙirarin kanun labarin yana buƙatar taka-tsantsan a nan, domin binciken bai nuna kamfanoni suna ɗaukar AI da aka saya kamar sun gina shi da kansu ba. Yawancin waɗanda suka amsa sun yi amfani da ƙarin hanyoyin kimantawa da haɗawa kan tsarin AI na ɓangare na uku, maimakon dogaro da hanyoyin saye iri ɗaya da na sauran ayyukan ɓangare na uku. Gano da sarrafa haɗarin da ya keɓanta da AI, da ƙarin matakan tsaro da kariyar bayanai, su ne matakan da aka fi ambata.

Layin da ya fi sirara shi ne juriya. Ƙarin buƙatun operational resilience, kamar joint incident testing ko exit planning, kamfanoni 27% ne suka ambata. Kan sabuntawar masu sayarwa, 48% na waɗanda suka amsa sun ce koyaushe suna yin regression testing kan sabuntawar high-materiality ga models da masu sayarwa suka samar ko guardrails ɗinsu, inda ƙarin 25% ke yi galibi. 73% sun ce koyaushe ko galibi suna da tsayayyun tsare-tsaren rollback, kuma 25% sun ce koyaushe ko galibi suna barin sabuntawar high-materiality na masu sayarwa su shiga production kai tsaye ta atomatik. Kamfanoni 48 kaɗai suka amsa wannan tambayar, kuma binciken ya ce a ɗauki sakamakon da taka-tsantsan.

## Hanyoyi biyu na fahimtar tsari

Sakamakon gudanarwa mafi kaifi kwatance ne tsakanin tsarin high-materiality na cikin gida da na waje. 77% na waɗanda suka amsa sun ce sun dogara da zurfin ilimin tsarin da aka gina a cikin gida, idan aka kwatanta da 24% kan tsarin ɓangare na uku. Kan tsarin ɓangare na uku, 81% sun dogara ne maimakon haka kan vendor assurance da tsare-tsaren kwangila. Tabbatar da ɗabi'a da sakamako (assurance of behaviour and outcomes), da constrained autonomy, an ambace su sosai kan nau'ikan tsarin biyu.

Fassarata ita ce kamfanoni suna fahimtar abin da suke ginawa ta hanyar sanin sa, kuma abin da suke saye ta hanyar mai samarwa da kwangila. Duka biyun kayan aiki ne masu ma'ana, kuma binciken bai yi hukunci kan ɗayansu ba. Ba ɗaya suke da ilimin kai tsaye da fiye da kashi uku cikin huɗu na waɗanda suka amsa ke dogara da shi kan tsarin high-materiality na cikin gida ba.

A halin da ake ciki, kason tsarin high-materiality da ke zuwa ta hanyar ɓangare na uku ya girma daga 35% zuwa 53%. Waɗanda suka amsa suna nuna wuri ɗaya. Sa ido kan tsarin AI na ɓangare na uku shi ne fannin rashin tabbas na gudanarwa ko ƙalubalen aiwatarwa da aka fi ambata. Amfani da ƙa'idojin model risk management kan models na ɓangare na uku ko na masu sayarwa 54% na waɗanda suka amsa ne suka zaɓa, kuma sa ido kan tsarin da ɓangare na uku suka samar ko suka yi tasiri sosai a kansu 49%.

Waɗannan zaɓuɓɓuka ne daga jerin ƙalubale masu yiwuwa, don haka ba ma'aunin gazawa ba ne. Amma suna nuna inda kamfanonin da kansu suke ganin wahalar take. Idan an rubuta ƙa'idojin model risk management ne da models na cikin gida a zuciya, kamar yadda zan iya cewa galibi haka aka yi, alƙalamin 54% shi ne abin da za a sa ran gani idan yawancin tsarin high-materiality suna zuwa daga waje. Takurar transparency, a 34%, tana nuna alƙibla ɗaya daga wata tambaya daban.

## Yadda gudanarwa take a takarda

Yawancin kamfanoni sun gina tsarin. 94% na waɗanda suka amsa sun ce suna da manufofi, ƙa'idoji ko ma'aunan AI na dukkan kamfani. Tsarukan third-party risk management, tsare-tsaren gudanar da bayanai (data governance) da sa ido na manyan jami'ai kowannensu aƙalla kashi uku cikin huɗu ne suka ambata, kuma 84% sun ce suna da dandalin gudanarwa na AI/model-risk na tsakiya. Ƙasa da rabi ne suka ambaci littattafan jagora na incident management da martani.

Matakan kariya na haɗarin bayanai sun kasu zuwa matakai biyu. Bita kafin turawa (pre-deployment review), amincewar ɗan adam da vendor due diligence kowannensu fiye da 90% na waɗanda suka amsa ne suka ambata. Sa ido mai ɗorewa (ongoing monitoring), gwajin zubewa (leakage testing) da bita na lokaci-lokaci ko sake tabbatarwa kowannensu kusan rabi ne suka ambata. Kusan kashi biyu cikin biyar na waɗanda suka amsa sun kuma ambaci independent validation, ongoing monitoring da drift detection, ko change control da versioning a matsayin ƙalubalen gudanarwa.

Siffar ta fi nauyi a farko: tace tsari a ƙofar shiga ya kusa zama na kowa, yayin da binciken da ke nuna ko har yanzu yana aiki yadda ake tsammani daga baya kusan rabi ne ke ambatonsu. Binciken bai faɗi dalili ba, kuma ba zan yi hasashe ba. Kan tsarin da aka saya waɗanda ke canzawa ta sabuntawar masu sayarwa, wannan mataki na baya shi ne inda assurance na mai sayarwa ke haɗuwa da shaidar kamfanin da kansa.

## Abin da kamfanoni ke sa rai a gaba

Ingancin aiki, automation da productivity su ne fannonin fa'ida mafiya girma da ake gani daga AI a yau, ko da yake ba a ɗauki ɗaya daga cikin fa'idojin da aka lissafa a matsayin wanda ya wuce matsakaici sosai ba. Waɗanda suka amsa sun sa ran fa'idoji su ƙaru a dukkan fannoni cikin shekaru uku masu zuwa, inda ake sa ran ƙaruwa mafi girma a cybersecurity da operational resilience, da kuma samar da kuɗaɗen shiga da matsayin gasa.

Fannonin haɗari mafiya girma da ake gani ga kamfanoni daga AI a yau su ne kariyar bayanai da tsaro, haɗarin ɓangare na uku da sarkar samarwa (supply chain), da haɗarin da ya keɓanta da GenAI. Waɗanda suka amsa sun sa ran matakan haɗari su ƙaru a dukkan rukunoni cikin shekara ɗaya zuwa uku masu zuwa, ko da yake a matsakaici sun ɗauki haɗarin a matsayin marasa tsanani a 2026 fiye da 2024.

Ra'ayin tsarin baki ɗaya (systemic) shi ne inda ɓangare na uku suka fito fili. Cyber risk amplification ya haifar da damuwa mafi girma a yanzu, inda 57% na waɗanda suka amsa suka ɗauke shi a matsayin high ko severe, wanda ya tashi zuwa 80% kan shekara ɗaya zuwa uku masu zuwa. Critical third-party dependencies ne suka biyo baya, suna tashi daga 40% zuwa 63%. Waɗannan su ne manyan haɗarin systemic guda biyu da aka fi ɗauka da muhimmanci a yau da kuma cikin shekaru uku masu zuwa.

Marubutan sun tsara daidaiton cikin kalmomi masu taka-tsantsan. Ci gaban AI "na iya kawo fa'idoji" ciki har da inganci mafi girma, ingantaccen decision support da sarrafa haɗari mai ƙarfi, kuma "na iya haifar ko ƙara haɗari" ciki har da dogaro ga ɓangare na uku da kwanciyar hankalin kuɗi (financial stability). Kalmar "na iya" tana cikin jimloli biyun, kuma ya kamata ta ci gaba da kasancewa a can a kowane sake bayarwa.

## Ƙarfin cyber na frontier da sarkar samarwa

88% na waɗanda suka amsa sun tantance ƙarfin cyber na frontier AI da ke canzawa cikin shekaru biyu masu zuwa a matsayin mai tasiri matsakaici ko babba kan bayanin haɗarin kamfaninsu. Wannan ya haɗa matsakaici da babba tare, don haka bai gaya mana nawa ne ke sa ran tasiri mai tsanani ba.

Lokacin da aka tambaye su ta wace hanya frontier AI ke haifar da haɗari mafi girma a yanzu, 44% sun zaɓi saurin amfani da raunin software da ke akwai (faster exploitation of existing software vulnerabilities), sai 19% da suka zaɓi ƙaruwar tasirin hare-haren supply-chain ko na ɓangare na uku. 64% sun zaɓi vulnerability management da saurin patching a matsayin fannin cyber resilience da ya samu ƙaruwar hankalin shugabanci mafi girma. Idan aka duba gaba, 45% sun sa ran amfani da kayan aikin kariya masu amfani da AI wajen sarrafa haɗarin ɓangare na uku cikin shekaru biyu masu zuwa.

## Abin da ke rage saurin kamfanoni

Takurawa marasa alaƙa da ƙa'idoji da aka fi ambata su ne wahalar haɗa AI cikin hanyoyin kasuwanci da yanke shawara da ake da su, da waɗanda tsofaffin tsarin (legacy systems) ko kayan more rayuwa suka haifar, waɗanda 23% da 22% na waɗanda suka amsa suka ɗauka manyan takurawa bi da bi. Takurawan ƙa'idoji da aka fi ambata sun shafi kariyar bayanai da sirri na UK, da ƙa'idojin ƙasa da ƙasa, waɗanda 42% da 29% na waɗanda suka amsa suka ga suna ɗora nauyin bin ƙa'ida mai girma bi da bi. Binciken yana ba da rahoton ra'ayoyi kan nauyi, don haka bai tabbatar da yadda ƙa'idojin suke da nauyi a zahiri ba.

Ƙananan kamfanoni sun amsa gajeriyar takardar tambayoyi. A cikin waɗanda suka amsa gajeren binciken, 73% sun ce suna amfani da AI, kuma daga cikinsu, 58% sun ce galibi suna amfani da general-purpose large language models. Operations shi ne domain da aka fi ambata don amfani na yanzu ko wanda aka tsara, a 64%, kuma 30% sun ambaci kariyar bayanai, sirri da batutuwan IT a matsayin takurawa.

## Abin da bincike na biyar zai iya warwarewa

Binciken ya bar tambaya bayyananna ga na gaba. Third-party implementations sun tashi daga 35% zuwa 53% na tsarin AI na high-materiality. Masu samar da cloud da compute uku sun mamaye 79% na dangantakar cloud/compute da aka bayar da rahotonsu. Waɗanda suka amsa sun sanya critical third-party dependencies cikin manyan haɗarin systemic guda biyu na shekaru uku masu zuwa, kuma ƙalubalen gudanarwa da aka fi ambata shi ne sa ido kan waɗannan tsarin daidai.

Abin da bayanan bayani ba za su iya gaya mana ba shi ne ko vendor assurance da tsare-tsaren kwangila, waɗanda 81% na waɗanda suka amsa ke dogara da su kan tsarin high-materiality na ɓangare na uku, sun isa ɗaukar wannan nauyi. Shaida kan yadda tsarin da aka saya ke aiki a production za ta taimaka wajen amsa ta, kuma ongoing monitoring, wanda kusan rabin waɗanda suka amsa suka ambata a matsayin matakin kariya na haɗarin bayanai, ɗaya ne daga cikin matakan da za su iya samar da ita. Idan bincike na gaba ya nuna monitoring da exit plans da aka gwada suna tashi tare da kason ɓangare na uku, kamfanoni sun fara rufe gibin da wannan ya rubuta. Idan kason ya ci gaba da hawa yayin da su suka tsaya a inda suke, gibin zai ƙara faɗi.

## Tambayoyin da Ake Yawan Yi

**Menene binciken Bank of England da FCA kan AI a fannin hada-hadar kuɗi na UK?**
Shi ne bincike na huɗu na haɗin gwiwa kan artificial intelligence da machine learning a fannin hada-hadar kuɗi na UK, bayan binciken 2019, 2022 da 2024. Wallafar 2026 taƙaitaccen bayani ne na jimillar sakamako daga waɗanda suka amsa 196, inda 105 suka cika cikakken binciken. Marubutan sun ce a karanta sakamakon a matsayin shaida daga kamfanonin da suka shiga, ba cikakken kimantawar dukkan fannin ba.

**Nawa ne na AI na kamfanoni ke zuwa daga ɓangare na uku?**
Third-party implementations sun kai 39% na tsarin AI da aka bayar da rahotonsu a 2026, idan aka kwatanta da 33% a 2024 da 17% a 2022. Sun kai 53% na tsarin AI na high-materiality, sama da 35% a 2024.

**Ta yaya kamfanoni ke fahimtar tsarin AI na ɓangare na uku?**
Kan tsarin high-materiality, 77% na waɗanda suka amsa sun ce sun dogara da zurfin ilimin tsarin da aka gina a cikin gida, idan aka kwatanta da 24% kan tsarin ɓangare na uku. Kan tsarin ɓangare na uku, 81% sun dogara ne maimakon haka kan vendor assurance da tsare-tsaren kwangila. An kuma ambaci assurance of behaviour and outcomes da constrained autonomy sosai.

**Shin kamfanoni suna kimanta AI na ɓangare na uku daban da sauran saye?**
Yawancin waɗanda suka amsa sun yi amfani da ƙarin hanyoyin kimantawa da haɗawa kan tsarin AI na ɓangare na uku maimakon dogaro da hanyoyin saye iri ɗaya da na sauran ayyukan ɓangare na uku. Gano da sarrafa haɗarin da ya keɓanta da AI, da ƙarin matakan tsaro da kariyar bayanai, su ne aka fi ambata. Ƙarin buƙatun operational resilience kamar joint incident testing ko exit planning 27% ne suka ambata.

**Waɗanne ƙalubalen gudanarwa kamfanoni ke ba da rahotonsu?**
Sa ido kan tsarin AI na ɓangare na uku shi ne fannin rashin tabbas ko ƙalubalen aiwatarwa da aka fi ambata. Amfani da ƙa'idojin model risk management kan models na ɓangare na uku ko na masu sayarwa 54% na waɗanda suka amsa ne suka zaɓa, kuma sa ido kan tsarin da ɓangare na uku suka samar ko suka yi tasiri sosai a kansu 49%.
