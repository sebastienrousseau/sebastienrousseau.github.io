## Barazanar Quantum ga Tsarin Biyan Kuɗi

Kayayyakin more rayuwa na biyan kuɗi na zamani sun dogara ne akan cryptography na maɓallin jama'a (public-key cryptography). RSA, ECC, da Diffie-Hellman. Don tantance ma'amaloli, kare bayanan mai katin, da kuma samar da tsaro ga saƙonni tsakanin cibiyoyin kuɗi. Waɗannan algorithms ɗin suna goyan bayan SWIFT, SEPA, tsarin sasanta ma'amala na ainihi (real-time gross settlement systems), da kusan kowane tsarin katin da ke aiki a yau.

Kwamfutocin quantum da ke amfani da algorithm na Shor za su iya karya waɗannan hanyoyin cryptographic na asali (cryptographic primitives). Kodayake har yanzu babu ingantattun injunan quantum masu jure kuskure (fault-tolerant quantum machines) a sikelin da ake buƙata, yanayin haɓaka kayan aiki. Kamar yadda IBM, Google, da sauran su suka nuna. Ya sa wannan ya zama batun lokaci na aikin injiniya maimakon na tunani kawai. Cibiyar Kula da Ma'aunai da Fasaha ta Amurka (NIST) ta riga ta kammala tsarin farko na ƙa'idodin cryptography bayan-quantum (post-quantum cryptographic standards) (FIPS 203, 204, da 205) a matsayin martani.

## Haɗarin Harvest-Now Decrypt-Later

Barazanar ba ta iyakance ga wani kwanan wata na gaba lokacin da kwamfutocin quantum za su sami isasshen ƙarfi ba. Masu gudanarwa a matakin ƙasa (state-level actors) da kuma ƙwararrun abokan gaba (sophisticated adversaries) sun riga sun fara tsarewa da adana bayanan da aka ɓoye a yau, da nufin warware ɓoyayyen bayanin da zarar albarkatun quantum sun kasance. Wannan tsari na harvest-now decrypt-later (HNDL) yana nufin cewa kowane bayanan biyan kuɗi mai mahimmanci na dogon lokaci. Kamar bayanan doka, taskokin bin ƙa'idoji, da wajibai na yarjejeniya. Sun riga sun kasance cikin haɗari.

Hukumomin kula da harkokin kuɗi sun fara mayar da martani. Hukumar Kula da Kuɗi ta Singapore (MAS) ta fitar da jagora kan shirye-shiryen quantum. Hukumar Kula da Prudent na Ostiraliya (APRA) ta nuna haɗarin cryptographic a cikin tsarin juriya na fasaha. Dokar Juriya ta Ayyukan Dijital ta Tarayyar Turai (DORA) ta ba da umarnin sarrafa haɗarin ICT wanda dole ne ya yi la'akari da barazanar da ke tasowa, ciki har da kwamfutar quantum.

## Tasiri a Faɗin Hanyoyin Biyan Kuɗi

Sakamakon ya shafi dukkan sassan kayayyakin more rayuwa na biyan kuɗi:

**Saƙonnin SWIFT:** Tsarin saƙon MT da MX sun dogara ne akan TLS da sa hannu na dijital (digital signatures) don tabbatar da aminci da tantancewa. Karyewar tsarin mabuɗin (key infrastructure) zai raunana tsarin amincin da ke haɗa cibiyoyi fiye da 11,000 a duk duniya.

**SEPA da biyan kuɗi na take-take:** Tsarin Tura Kuɗi na Take na SEPA (SEPA Instant Credit Transfer) na Hukumar Biyan Kuɗi ta Turai yana aiwatar da ma'amalolin da ba za a iya soke su ba a cikin ƙasa da daƙiƙa goma. Karyewar cryptographic a wannan saurin ba ya barin wata dama don sa hannun ɗan adam ko tantancewa da hannu.

**Tsarin biyan kuɗi na ainihi:** Faster Payments (UK), FedNow (US), da NPP (Ostiraliya) duk suna da dogaro iri ɗaya kan classical cryptographic primitives don tantance saƙonni da kuma tabbatar da mahalarta.

**Bin doka da bayanai na dogon lokaci:** Bayanan biyan kuɗi da aka adana don dalilai na ƙa'ida. Galibi ana buƙatar adana su na tsawon shekaru biyar zuwa goma ko fiye da haka. Za su fi tsawon lokacin tsaro na cryptography da ya kare su a lokacin da aka ƙirƙira su. Shirye-shiryen ƙaura na [ISO 20022](/2023-09-29-automating-iso-20022-compliant-payment-file-creation-with-pain001/index.html) dole ne su yi la'akari da tsawon rayuwar cryptographic na bayanan da suke samarwa.

**Blockchain da distributed ledger technology:** Dandalin kadarorin dijital da kayan aikin biyan kuɗi da aka yi wa alama (tokenised payment instruments) waɗanda suka dogara da elliptic curve cryptography suna fuskantar barazana ta kai tsaye da kuma sananniya daga algorithms na quantum.

## Abin da Ya Kamata Ƙungiyoyi Su Yi Yanzu

Canji zuwa cryptography mai aminci na quantum ba haɓakawa ɗaya ba ne kawai, amma shiri ne na shekaru da yawa wanda ke buƙatar shiri na musamman:

**Kayan aikin cryptographic:** Dole ne ƙungiyoyi su tsara kowane tsarin, tsari (protocol), da ma'ajiyar bayanai da suka dogara da classical public-key cryptography. Wannan ya haɗa da takaddun shaida na TLS, tantancewar API, tsarin HSM (HSM configurations), tsarin sarrafa mabuɗi (key management systems), da ɓoye bayanan da ke adane (data-at-rest encryption).

**Amfani da algorithm na post-quantum:** NIST ta daidaita ML-KEM (FIPS 203) don encapsulation na mabuɗi da ML-DSA (FIPS 204) don sa hannun dijital. Ya kamata ƙungiyoyi su fara gwada waɗannan algorithms ɗin a wuraren da ba na kasuwanci ba (non-production environments) kuma su haɓaka taswirar ƙaura don mahimman tsarin aiki.

**Sauƙin cryptographic:** Dole ne a tsara tsarin. Ko a sake gina su (refactored). Domin a sami damar musanya algorithms na cryptographic ba zare da buƙatar sake tsara dukkan aikace-aikacen ba. Wannan ƙa'idar tana aiki ga hanyoyin biyan kuɗi (payment gateways), tsarin saƙon tsakiya (messaging middleware), da APIs na abokan ciniki baki ɗaya.

**Hanyoyi na haɗin gwiwa:** A lokacin lokacin miƙa mulki, tsarin cryptographic na haɗin gwiwa wanda ke haɗa classical da algorithms na post-quantum suna ba da kariya mai zurfi (defence-in-depth). Wannan tsarin yana kiyaye dacewa da tsarin baya (backward compatibility) yayin da yake samar da juriya ga quantum (quantum resistance).

## Ƙungiyar Aiki ta EPAA da Haɗin Gwiwar Masana'antar

Hukumar Emerging Payments Association Asia (EPAA) ta kafa Ƙungiyar Aiki ta Cryptography ta Quantum Safe don magance waɗannan ƙalubale ta hanyar daidaita ayyukan masana'antu. Ƙungiyar aikin tana haɗa mahalarta daga ko'ina cikin tsarin biyan kuɗi, gami da IBM, HSBC, KPMG, JPMorgan Chase, da PayPal, da sauran su.

Ta hanyar tarukan bita da aka gudanar a Sydney, Hong Kong, da Singapore, ƙungiyar aikin ta ɓullo da tsari na bai ɗaya don tantance haɗarin quantum a tsarin biyan kuɗi da kuma gano hanyoyin ƙaura masu dacewa. Sakamakon takardar. [Quantum-Safe Payments: Me yasa Ya Zama Dole Masana'antar Biyan Kuɗi Ta Hanzarta Aiki Yanzu][epaa]. Yana wakiltar matsayi na yarjejeniya kan gaggawa da faɗin ƙalubalen.

Binciken ƙungiyar aikin ya kammala da cewa shirye-shiryen quantum-safe yanke shawara ce ta yanzu game da kayayyakin more rayuwa, ba ta gaba ba. Ƙungiyoyin da suka jinkirta suna cikin haɗarin samun kansu sun kasa cika tsammanin ƙa'ida, kare bayanai na dogon lokaci, ko kuma kula da haɗin gwiwa tare da abokan hulɗa waɗanda riga sun ƙaura.
