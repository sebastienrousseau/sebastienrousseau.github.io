# Wa'adinku na High-Risk Ya Matsa Zuwa Disamba 2027. Na Chatbot ɗinku Bai Matsa Ba.

**Kwana tara da suka wuce AI omnibus ya fara aiki kuma ya mayar wa bankuna wata goma sha shida.** Wajiban high-risk na tsarin Annex III — waɗanda suka shafi tantance cancantar bashi da tsara farashin inshora, tanade-tanaden da aka gina kowane shirin AI na banki a kansu — sun matsa daga yau zuwa 2 Disamba 2027. Tsarin da aka saka a cikin kayayyakin da ake sarrafawa a ƙarƙashin Annex I sun koma 2 Agusta 2028. Wannan sauƙi ne na gaske a mafi tsadar sashen fayil ɗin. Haka kuma shi ne mafi haɗarin nau'in labari mai daɗi, domin jinkirin da aka sanar a sakin layi ɗaya ana karanta shi a matsayin jinkirta komai. Article 50 bai matsa ba. Wajibansa na transparency suna aiki tun yau, kuma ba sa sauka a kan credit engine. Suna sauka a kan chatbot ɗin manhajar wayar hannu, a kan wasiƙar da AI ta rubuta, da kuma muryar roba ta cibiyar tuntuɓa.

> **Takaitawa ga Shugabanni**
>
> - **Sauƙin gaskiya ne kuma kunkuntar.** Wata goma sha shida a Annex III yana canza tattalin arzikin shirin sosai. Yana shafar aikin rarraba high-risk, ba transparency ba.
> - **Yau rana ce ta bin ka'ida mai aiki.** Article 50 yana aiki tun 2 Agusta 2026. Rubutun bai tanadi lokacin jinkiri ga tsarin da aka kawo kasuwa bayan yarjejeniyar omnibus ba.
> - **Tanadin canji ya fi kunci fiye da yadda yake ji.** Tsarin generative da ke kasuwa kafin yarjejeniyar omnibus suna da lokaci har 2 Disamba 2026 don wajibcin machine-readable marking. Sabbin turawa ba sa gadar wannan.
> - **Matsalar tashar ce sanye da alamar model risk.** Kusan kowace fuska da abin ya shafa mallakar dijital ce, tallace-tallace ko cibiyar tuntuɓa — sassan da ba su da kujera a kwamitin jagorancin AI Act.

## Me ya matsa kuma me bai matsa ba

Daidaito yana da muhimmanci a nan fiye da yadda aka saba, saboda kanun labarin da rubutun da ke aiki sun rabu.

AI Act kansa shi ne Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. Kunshin sauƙaƙawa da aka sani da AI omnibus an gabatar da shi a Nuwamba 2025, ya kai yarjejeniyar siyasa a Mayu 2026, kuma ya fara aiki a 27 Yuli 2026 — kwana tara kafin ranar da wannan labarin ke ɗauke da ita.

Abin da ya jinkirta:

- **Wajiban high-risk na Annex III** sun koma **2 Disamba 2027**. Wannan shi ne ɓangaren da ya ƙunshi tantance cancantar bashi da credit scoring na mutane, da kuma tantance haɗari da tsara farashi a inshorar rai da lafiya. Ga yawancin bankuna, wannan shi ne dukan shirin.
- **High-risk na Annex I** — tsarin da aka saka a matsayin abin tsaro cikin kayayyakin da dokar kayayyaki ta EU ta riga ta rufe — ya koma **2 Agusta 2028**.

Abin da bai jinkirta ba:

- **Wajiban transparency na Article 50** suna aiki tun **2 Agusta 2026**.

Tazarar da ke tsakanin waɗannan gaskiya biyu ita ce dukan maudu'in wannan rubutu. Bankin da ya kashe wata goma sha takwas yana gina conformity assessment, takaddun fasaha da ƙarfin yin rajista don wani samfurin scoring ya samu yanzu wata goma sha shida na sarari kan wannan aikin. Wannan bankin da alama yana da assistant mai fuskantar abokin ciniki a manhajar wayarsa wanda ya samu wajibci na doka yau — a ƙarƙashin wani daraktan daban, ana ba shi kuɗi daga wani kasafi daban, kuma babu shi kwata-kwata a shirin shirin AI Act.

Yanayin gazawar ba ƙin bi ba ne. Tsarin ƙungiya ne.

## Wajibai huɗu da ke aiki yau

Article 50 gajere ne, kuma ya cancanci a fayyace abin da yake nema da gaske maimakon a sake fasalta shi har ya zama mara tabbas.

**Hulɗa kai tsaye.** Dole ne providers su tabbatar an sanar da mutane cewa suna hulɗa da AI system ba da mutum ba, sai dai idan wannan a bayyane yake ga mutum mai isasshen sani a wannan yanayin. Ga banki, wannan shi ne assistant ɗin manhaja, na'urar web chat, da voice bot ɗin da ke ɗaukar waya kafin mutum.

**Abun ciki da AI ta ƙirƙira.** Dole ne providers na tsarin da ke ƙirƙirar sauti, hoto, bidiyo ko rubutu na roba su yi wa fitarwa alama a tsarin **machine-readable** kuma su sa a iya gano ta a matsayin abin da aka ƙirƙira ko aka sarrafa ta hanyar wucin gadi. Fasahohin da ake tunani su ne watermarks, metadata, alamun provenance na cryptography da fingerprints.

**Emotion recognition da biometric categorisation.** Dole ne deployers su sanar da mutanen da tsarin ya shafa. Sentiment analytics a cibiyar tuntuɓa shi ne ɗan takarar da ya fi bayyana, kuma sau da yawa ana sayen sa a matsayin kayan aikin tabbatar da inganci maimakon AI system.

**Deepfakes da rubutun maslahar jama'a.** Deployers da ke ƙirƙira ko sarrafa hoto, sauti ko bidiyo da ke zama deepfake dole ne su bayyana hakan. Rubutun da aka wallafa domin sanar da jama'a kan al'amuran maslahar jama'a shi ma yana ɗauke da wajibcin bayyanawa — kuma yana kaiwa cikin sadarwar kamfani da wallafa bincike zurfi fiye da yadda yawancin bankuna suka ƙiyasta.

## «Machine-readable» ne ke ɗaukar dukan nauyin

Daga cikin wajibai huɗu, buƙatar yin alama ita ce kaɗai da ke zama shirin injiniya ba canjin rubutu ba.

Rubutun da ake gani «an ƙirƙira da AI» mataki ne mai ma'ana kuma bai cika wajibcin da aka gina kan iya karantawar na'ura ba. Manufar buƙatar ita ce tsarin da ke ƙasa — dandali, mai bincike, kayan tantancewa — ya iya tantance asali ba tare da wani mutum ya karanta shafin ba. Watermarking, metadata da aka saka, provenance na cryptography da fingerprinting duk fasahohi ne da aka ambata, kuma suna nuna hali daban a cikin yanayin da abun ciki ke fuskanta da gaske: sake rikodi, yanke, ɗaukar hoton allo, canza tsari, da wucewa ta dandalin wasu ɓangarori waɗanda ke cire metadata a matsayin ƙa'ida.

Wannan yana haifar da tambayoyi uku ga banki, kuma babu ɗayansu na doka.

Waɗanne sarƙoƙin abun ciki ne ke samar da fitarwa ta roba kwata-kwata — har da waɗanda suka bayyana a waje da tafiyar da harkoki, kamar wasiƙun abokan ciniki da AI ta rubuta da ƙirƙirar hotunan tallace-tallace. Wace fasahar yin alama ce ke tsira daga hanyar rarrabawa da kowace sarƙa ke amfani da ita. Kuma ko kayan aikin da ke cikin tarin suna goyon bayanta, domin wajibcin yin alama yana da ƙarfi kamar tsarin da ya fi rauni a sarƙar da ke taɓa kadara bayan an ƙirƙira ta.

Akwai tanadin canji da ya cancanci a karanta a hankali maimakon da fata. Tsarin generative da ke kasuwa kafin yarjejeniyar omnibus ta Mayu 2026 suna da lokaci har **2 Disamba 2026** don cika wajibcin machine-readable marking na Article 50(2). Ga turawa da ke wanzuwa, wannan hutu ne na gaske. Ba lokacin jinkiri na gama-gari ba ne, kuma bai kai ga abin da banki zai kafa wata mai zuwa ba.

## Provider ko deployer, kuma me ya sa ba matsalar mai sayarwa ba ce

Wajiban sun rabu bisa matsayi, kuma wannan rabuwar ita ce inda tunanin sayayya kan gaza.

A taƙaice, yin alama ga fitarwa ta roba a wurin ƙirƙirar ta yana kan **provider** — ɓangaren da ke haɓaka tsarin kuma ya kawo shi kasuwa. Bayyanawa ga mutanen da abin ya shafa galibi yana kan **deployer** — bankin da ke amfani da tsarin a ƙarƙashin ikonsa. Bankin da ya ɗauki lasisin conversational assistant shi ne deployer. Zai iya zama provider kuma idan ya sanya sunansa a kan tsarin ko ya canza shi sosai, kuma daidai abin da ke faruwa ke nan lokacin da wata cibiya ta lulluɓe foundation model cikin assistant mai tambarinta.

Sakamako biyu suna biyowa.

Takardar shaidar mai sayarwa ba ta kawar da wajibcin deployer ba. «Dandalinmu ya bi AI Act» magana ce game da wajiban mai sayarwa, ba game da bayanin da banki ke yi wa abokan cinikinsa a fuskarsa ba.

Kuma white-labelling na iya ƙetare banki wancan layin. Yayin da cibiya ke ƙara mai da tsarin wani ɓangare na uku nata — suna, salo, ƙananan gyare-gyare — haka ya ƙara yiwuwa tana ɗauke da wajiban provider da ba ta taɓa yin kwangila a kansu ba.

## Lambar da ke tsara fifiko

Rashin bin Article 50 yana cikin matakin da ke ɗauke da tarar gudanarwa har zuwa **Yuro miliyan 15 ko kashi 3% na jimlar kuɗin shiga na shekara a duniya**, duk wanda ya fi girma.

Wannan lambar ta cancanci a karanta ta gefen ƙoƙarin da ake buƙata. Bayyana cewa chatbot chatbot ne ƙaramin aiki ne. Yin alama ga abun ciki na roba a hanyar machine-readable shiri ne na injiniya na gaske amma mai iyaka. Babu ɗayansu da ya kai tsadar tsarin conformity assessment da ya matsa yanzu zuwa Disamba 2027. Rashin daidaito tsakanin haɗari da farashin gyara shi ne dalilin yin wannan yanzu, kuma hujja ce mai sauƙi ba kasafai ba don kai wa hukumar gudanarwa.

## Littafin Aiki

Matakai biyar, kuma na farko ba nazarin doka ba ne.

1. **Ku lissafa fuskoki, ba samfura ba.** Ku tambayi kowane sashe mai fuskantar abokin ciniki wace AI ce ke taɓa abokin ciniki. Rijistar AI Act da model risk ta gina ba za ta ƙunshi mai ƙirƙirar hotunan tallace-tallace ba, ba kuma assistant ɗin rubutu na cibiyar tuntuɓa ba.
2. **Ku rarrabe provider da deployer kowane tsari.** Ku yi hakan kowane tsari ba kowane mai sayarwa ba, domin mai bayarwa guda zai iya barinku a matsayai daban-daban gwargwadon yadda kuka yi tambari da gyara abin da ya sayar muku.
3. **Ku gwada ko alamarku tana tsira.** Ku wuce da kadara mai alama ta hanyar rarrabawa ta gaske — CMS, dandalin imel, tashar sada zumunta — ku duba ko siginar provenance tana nan a ƙarshe. Yawancin metadata ba sa tsira daga wannan tafiyar.
4. **Ku sanya mai alhakin ƙofar fitarwa.** Bin Article 50 halayya ce ta kowace fitowa ta tashar, ba takardar shaida ta lokaci ɗaya ba. Idan babu mai amincewa mai suna, zai koma baya a karo na farko da wata ƙungiya za ta yi turawa a ƙarƙashin matsin wa'adi.
5. **Ku ci gaba da dumama shirin da aka jinkirta.** Wata goma sha shida ba soke ba ne. Ƙungiyoyin da aka wargaza a Agusta 2026 ana sake gina su da tsada mafi girma a 2027, kuma aikin rarraba da ke tantance ko tsari ya faɗa cikin Annex III ko a'a bai zama mafi sauƙi ba ko kaɗan.

Sauƙin ka'idoji da wuya ya zama daidai ga kowa, kuma kusan bai taɓa zama daidai ta hanyar da kanun labari ke nunawa ba. Cibiyoyin da za su ji kunya bana ba su ne suka karanta doka ba daidai ba. Su ne suka ji «an jinkirta», suka wargaza shirin, kuma ba su taɓa duba wane wajibcinsu ne wannan kalmar ta rufe ba.

## Tambayoyin da Ake Yawan Yi

**To an jinkirta AI Act ko a'a?**  
A wani ɓangare. AI omnibus, mai aiki tun 27 Yuli 2026, ya jinkirta wajiban high-risk na Annex III zuwa 2 Disamba 2027 da na Annex I zuwa 2 Agusta 2028. Bai jinkirta Article 50 ba, wanda wajibansa na transparency ke aiki tun 2 Agusta 2026.

**Muna amfani da chatbot na wani ɓangare na uku kawai. Muna cikin iyaka?**  
Kusan tabbas eh, a matsayin deployer. Wajiban deployer — har da sanar da mutane cewa suna hulɗa da AI system — suna kan bankin da ke gudanar da tsarin a ƙarƙashin ikonsa, kuma ba sa ƙarewa da sanarwar bin ka'ida ta mai sayarwa. Idan kun sanya tambarinku a kan assistant ko kuka canza shi sosai, kuna iya ɗaukar wajiban provider kuma.

**Lakabin da ake gani «AI-generated» ya isa?**  
Ga wajibcin yin alama, a'a. Article 50(2) an gina shi kan machine-readable marking domin tsarin da ke ƙasa su gano asalin roba ba tare da karatun mutum ba — watermarks, metadata, alamun provenance na cryptography, fingerprints. Rubutun da ake gani kyakkyawan aiki ne a kai, ba maye gurbi ba.

**Menene tanadin canji don yin alama ga abun ciki?**  
Tsarin generative da ke kasuwa kafin yarjejeniyar omnibus ta Mayu 2026 suna da lokaci har 2 Disamba 2026 don cika buƙatar machine-readable marking. Yana da alaƙa da wannan rukunin kaɗai; tsarin da aka tura bayan haka ba ya gadar ƙarin lokacin.

**Ya kamata mu wargaza shirin high-risk tun da ya matsa?**  
A'a. Wata goma sha shida na sauƙi dama ce ta yin aikin rarraba yadda ya kamata, ba dalilin tsayawa ba. Tantance ko wani tsari ya faɗa cikin Annex III kwata-kwata shi ne ɓangaren da cibiyoyi ke ci gaba da raina, kuma ba a jinkirta shi a wahala ba — a wa'adi kaɗai.

**Nawa ne kuɗin idan mun yi kuskure?**  
Karya Article 50 yana cikin matakin da ke jawo tarar gudanarwa har zuwa Yuro miliyan 15 ko kashi 3% na jimlar kuɗin shiga na shekara a duniya, duk wanda ya fi girma. Idan aka auna da ƙoƙarin gyara — rubutun bayyanawa da sarƙar yin alama ga abun ciki — wannan rabon shi ne dalilin motsawa a wannan kwata.
