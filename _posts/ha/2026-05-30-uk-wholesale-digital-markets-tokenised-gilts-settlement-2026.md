# Kasuwannin Dijital na Yawa a Birtaniya: Gilts Tokenised, Sasantawa, da Sabon Matsayin Zakara

<p class="post-lead-tldr"><strong>Taƙaitawa.</strong> Birtaniya ta ɗauki matsayin Wholesale Digital Markets Champion don daidaita gilts da aka mai da su zuwa tokens, sasantawa ta DLT, akwatin gwaji na DSS na Bank of England, da kuma babbar manufar kuɗin yawa na dijital. Alamar 2026 ita ce cewa tokenisation na yawa ya wuce daga muhawarar manufofin zuwa ƙarfin gasa na ƙasa — kuma tambayar dabaru ga bankunan City of London ta canza daga "shin za mu gwada kadarorin tokenised" zuwa "wane gine-ginen dandali ne ke kare matsayin yawa naka akan Singapore Project Guardian, Swiss SDX, Frankfurt, da Project Agorá". Bankuna da ke mu'amala da gilts tokenised + repo + sasantawar takardun shaida a matsayin matsalar tsari guda ɗaya suna kiyaye haƙƙin yawa; bankuna da ke gudanar da gwaje-gwaje biyar a layi ɗaya suna rasa ginshiƙin bayanai.</p>

  <li><strong>Matsayin Zakara yana mai da shi aikin ƙasa.</strong> Kuɗin yawa tokenised yanzu yana zaune cikin dabarar ƙarfin gasa na Birtaniya, ba kawai shirye-shiryen akwatin gwaji na FCA / BoE ba.</li>
  <li><strong>Gilts tokenised su ne ƙofar shiga.</strong> Gilts a matsayin garantii + akwatin gwaji na DSS + sasantawa ta DLT tare suna gwada cikakken tsarin yawa — repo, sasantawar takardun shaida, biya, da kuɗi na cikin rana.</li>

  <li><strong>Rarrabuwa ita ce maƙiyi.</strong> Gwaje-gwaje na kowace kadara / kowace dandali suna gina haɗarin aiki na shekaru goma masu zuwa — kawai tsarin dandali tare da tattalin arziki masu aunawa ne ke kare haƙƙin yawa.</li>

Matsayin Wholesale Digital Markets Champion na Birtaniya yana mai da kuɗin yawa tokenised zuwa aikin ƙarfin gasa na ƙasa. Alamar 2026 ita ce cewa tokenisation na yawa ya ƙaura daga wasan kwaikwayo na ƙirƙira zuwa cikin tsarin aiki na banki, inda tambayar yanke shawara ita ce horon zane: wane bayanai, hanyoyi, sarrafawa, alhakai, da kwararar aikin abokin ciniki ke da alaƙa ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-fintech-backed-to-embrace-future-payments-technology "UK fintech backed to embrace future payments technology")).

## Me ya sa 2026 ita ce Shekarar da Wannan ya Zama Mai Dabaru

Masana'antar ta wuce matakin karɓuwa. Ba ya isa a haɗa hanya, a ƙaura saƙo, a gudanar da hujjar ra'ayi na AI, ko a sanar da gwajin tokenisation. A 2026, fa'idar dabaru tana fitowa daga tsara waɗannan iyawa akan ainihin kwararar aiki, sannan a tabbatar da cewa kwararar aikin ta fi aminci, sauri, arha, juriya, ko amfani ga abokan ciniki.

Wannan shi ne dalilin da ya sa tokenisation na yawa yanzu maganar matakin kwamiti ne. Matsi guda suna sake komawa: bayanan biya masu wadata, sasantawa ta lokaci-zahiri, kuɗi tokenised, yanke shawara ta AI, Open Banking, juriyar aiki, taro na cloud, da shaidar tsari mafi ƙarfi. Idan aka kula da su daban-daban, waɗannan matsi suna ƙirƙirar yaduwar shirye-shirye. Idan aka kula da su a matsayin gine-gine ɗaya, suna ƙirƙirar ƙarfin aiki ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-fintech-backed-to-embrace-future-payments-technology "UK fintech backed to embrace future payments technology"), [Association of Corporate Treasurers](https://www.treasurers.org/hub/blog/Update-Payments-Landscape-May-2026 "Update on the Payments landscape – May 2026")).

## Tushen Gine-gine na 2026

### 1. Kwararar Aiki Tukuna, Fasaha ta Biyu

Banki ya kamata ya fara da gogayya: kuɗi a tarko, jinkirin sasantawa, kuɗin daidaitawa, biya da suka kasa, fuskantar zamba, rauni na iya dubawa, ko ƙwarewar abokin ciniki marar kyau. Fasahar ana ba ta hujja ne kawai inda take cire wannan gogayya ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-fintech-backed-to-embrace-future-payments-technology "UK fintech backed to embrace future payments technology")).

### 2. Bayanai a Matsayin Filin Sarrafawa

Bayanai masu tsari, masu sarrafawa, da masu iya bibiya su ne tushe. Ba tare da bayanai masu amfani ba, aikin atomatik ya zama mai rauni kuma biyayya ta zama ta hannu. Tare da bayanai masu amfani, bankuna na iya ƙirƙirar hankalin ja-gora, sarrafawa ta lokaci-zahiri, da nazari ga abokan ciniki ([Association of Corporate Treasurers](https://www.treasurers.org/hub/blog/Update-Payments-Landscape-May-2026 "Update on the Payments landscape – May 2026")).

### 3. Tsari a Hanyoyi da Dandalai

Gine-ginen dole ya tallafa wa hanyoyi da yawa, masu samar da kaya, tsare-tsaren asali, alamomin haɗari, da kadarorin sasantawa. Ya kamata yanke shawarar ja-gora a yi shi ta hanyar farashi, sauri, ƙarewa, hurumi, zaɓin abokin ciniki, juriya, da wadatar bayanai.

### 4. Biyayya da Shaida Ginannu

Tsarin biyayya dole ya zama na asali ga kwararar aiki. Manufa-a-matsayin-lambar, bayanan duba ta atomatik, shaidar juriyar aiki, bayanan yarda, da gudanarwar samfuri suna buƙatar a samar da su a matsayin wani ɓangare na aiwatarwa, ba a sake ƙirƙira su ga masu duba daga baya ba.

### 5. Tattalin Arziki na Naúrar da Ƙimar Abokin Ciniki

Kowane shiri yana buƙatar shaidar ƙimar kasuwanci. Farashi-da-biya, farashi-da-yanke-shawara, farashi-da-bincike, kuɗin da aka adana, gyare-gyare na hannu da aka guji, asarar zamba da aka rage, da karɓar abokin ciniki ya kamata su yanke shawarar yin girma.

## Abin da Wannan ke Nufi ta Nau'in Banki

### Bankunan Duniya

Bankunan duniya su ƙirƙiri tsarin matakin-dandali ta yadda kowace kasuwa, hanya, token, da iyawar AI ba ya zama tsarin aiki daban.

### Bankunan Yanki

Bankunan yanki su mai da hankali kan amfani inda amana, ilimin kasuwa na gida, da haɗawa mai sauƙi suka fi girma: ganuwar baitulmali, hana zamba, biyan kuɗi na Open Banking, da sabis na kuɗi dijital da aka tsara.

### Fintechs da PSPs

Fintechs su rage rikitarwa ga bankuna maimakon ƙara wata hanya keɓe. Kyawawan shawarwari za su kawo tsari, shaidar biyayya, ko hankali na bayanai.

### Masu Kula da Kuɗin Kamfanoni

Masu kula da kuɗi su buƙaci ingantattun ci gaba masu aunawa: ƙarancin gyare-gyaren biya, ganuwar kuɗi mafi kyau, bayanan daidaitawa masu wadata, sasantawa cikin sauri, da ikon ƙarfi akan yanke shawara ta atomatik.

## Kammalawa

Kasuwannin Dijital na Yawa a Birtaniya a ƙarshe tambaya ce ta gine-gine. Cibiyoyin da za su yi nasara ba za su zama waɗanda ke da gwaje-gwaje mafi yawa ko harshen ƙirƙira mafi ƙarfi ba. Za su zama cibiyoyin da ke haɗa kwararar aikin abokan ciniki, ingancin bayanai, tsarin hanya, biyayya ginanniya, da tattalin arziki na naúra zuwa tsarin aiki mai daidaito.

## Tambayoyin da Aka Saba Yi

**Me ya sa wannan magana ke gaggawa a 2026?**

Saboda kayan more rayuwa, tsari, da alamomin buƙatar abokin ciniki masu dacewa sun haɗu. Abin da ya kasance gwajin zaɓi yanzu yana zama ɓangare na tsarin aiki na banki.

**Menene mafi girman haɗarin aiwatarwa?**

Mafi girman haɗari shi ne rarrabuwa: ƙungiyoyi daban-daban suna gina gwaje-gwaje daban-daban, kowanne da bayanai daban-daban, sarrafawa, gudanarwa, da tattalin arziki.

**Menene banki ya kamata ya fara ginawa?**

Banki ya kamata ya fara da kwararar aiki inda akwai ƙimar da za a iya aunawa, kamar sasantawa cikin sauri, ƙarancin farashin daidaitawa, ƙarancin bincike, ingantacciyar hana zamba, ko ganuwar kuɗi mafi kyau.

**Yaya za a auna nasara?**

Ya kamata a auna nasara ta tattalin arziki na naúra, shaidar juriya, ingancin bayanai, karɓar abokin ciniki, rage haɗarin aiki, da ingantawar kuɗi ko jarin aiki.
