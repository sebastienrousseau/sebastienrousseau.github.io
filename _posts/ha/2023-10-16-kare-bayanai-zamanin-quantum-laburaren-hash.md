## Insight

### Barazanar Kwamfutar Quantum da Ke Fitowa

Yayin da yanayin dijital ke haɓaka, ƙungiyoyin sabis na kuɗi dole ne su karɓi sabbin fasahohi don su kasance masu gaba da fafatawa. Rashin yin hakan na iya haifar da barinsu a baya, domin sauyin dijital yana shafar kowane masana'antu.

Kwamfutar quantum tana shelanta wani gagarumin sauyi na musamman, tana ba da ikon haifar da gagarumin ci gaba a sassa daban-daban, gami da Banki da Sabis na Kuɗi. Duk da haka, tana zuwa da babban haɗari ga tsaron dijital, la'akari da ikonta na iya ɓangaren bayanan sirri har ma da lambobin sirri mafi sarƙaƙiya.

Kwamfutar quantum tana sa wasu fasahohin ɓoye bayanan sirri na gargajiya su zama tsofaffi, saboda tana iya magance matsalolin lissafi waɗanda kwamfutoci na yau da kullun ba za su iya ba.

A cikin yanayin yau, Alice da Bob za su iya sadarwa cikin aminci ta amfani da maɓallan cryptographic, suna hana Eve ɓata saƙonnin. Amma, cikakken tsaron rarraba maɓalli da adana su ba za a iya tabbatar da shi gabaɗaya ba. Sakamakon haka, kwamfutocin quantum suna zama babban barazana ga ɓoye bayanai da tsaron dijital.

#### Tsaro Amma da Rauni: Kewaya Ƙalubalen Cryptographic a Zamanin Quantum

##### Bayani na Alamomi

* *Alice zuwa Eve - Alice ta aika saƙon da aka ɓoye*
* *Eve ta katse saƙo - Eve ta katse saƙon Alice*
* *Eve ta yi ƙoƙarin ɓangaren bayanan sirri - Eve ta yi ƙoƙari amma ta kasa ɓata saƙon*
* *Eve zuwa Bob - Eve ta aika saƙon da aka ɓoye zuwa ga Bob*
* *Bob zuwa Eve - Bob ya aika da amsa da aka ɓoye zuwa ga Eve*
* *Eve ta katse saƙo - Eve ta katse amsar Bob*
* *Eve ta yi ƙoƙarin ɓangaren bayanan sirri - Eve ta sake kasawa wajen ɓata saƙon*
* *Eve zuwa Alice - Eve ta aika saƙon da aka ɓoye zuwa ga Alice*

##### Bayani mai zurfi

###### Ɓoye bayanai na yanzu

Algoritmomin ɓoye bayanai na yanzu da Alice da Bob ke amfani da su suna da inganci wajen hana Eve ɓata saƙonninsu. Koyaya, kwamfutar quantum tana haifar da barazana mai yuwuwa ga tsaron wawanan algoritmomin.

###### Haɗarin quantum mai yuwuwa

Kwamfutocin quantum suna da sauri nesa ba kusa ba fiye da kwamfutoci na gargajiya wajen yin wasu nau'ikan lissafi, gami da lissafin da ake amfani da su don ɓata some encryption algorithms. Idan Eve tana da damar yin amfani da kwamfutar quantum, za ta iya ɓata ɓoyayyen bayanan kuma ta karanta saƙonnin Alice da Bob.

###### Haɗarin rarraba maɓalli da adana su

Ko da Alice da Bob suna amfani da ɓoye bayanai mai ƙarfi, saƙonninsu na iya kasancewa cikin haɗari idan maɓallan da ake amfani da su wajen ɓoye da kuma ɓata saƙonnin sun lalace. Ana iya lalata maɓallai ta hanyoyi da yawa, kamar ta hanyar sata, kutse (hacking), ko hare-haren injiniyan zamantakewa (social engineering).

###### Bukatar post-quantum cryptography

Post-quantum cryptography wani sabon fanni ne na cryptography wanda aka tsara shi don ya zama mai tsayayya ga hare-haren quantum. Algoritmomin ɓoye bayanai na post-quantum har yanzu suna kan haɓakawa, amma suna da damar kare bayanai daga hare-haren quantum.

### Gabatar da Cryptography mai Tsayayya da Quantum

Cryptography mai tsayayya da quantum, wanda kuma aka sani da post-quantum cryptography (PQC) ko cryptography mai tsaron quantum, yana nufin algoritmomin cryptographic da ake amanna suna da tsaro a kan hare-haren kwamfutar quantum.

Dole ne ƙungiyoyi su ɗauki matakan kariya da suka dace don kare bayanan su daga haɗarin kwamfutar quantum. Aiwatar da ɓoye bayanai mai tsayayya da quantum da dabarun quantum entanglement na iya ba kamfanonin sabis na kuɗi ƙarin tsaro.

* **Cryptography mai tsayayya da quantum** wani sabon nau'in ɓoye bayanai ne wanda zai iya jure hare-hare daga kwamfutocin quantum. Algoritmomin ɓoye bayanai masu tsayayya da quantum na iya hanzarta sarrafa bayanai da daidaito, wanda ke sa su zama zaɓi mafi inganci.

* **Quantum entanglement** za a iya amfani da shi wajen ƙirƙirar tsarin [quantum key distribution](/2023-12-11-quantum-key-distribution-revolutionising-security-in-banking/index.html) ([QKD](/2023-12-11-quantum-key-distribution-revolutionising-security-in-banking/index.html)), waɗanda za su iya samarwa da rarraba maɓallan cryptographic amintattu a kan dogon nisa. Tsarin [QKD](/2023-12-11-quantum-key-distribution-revolutionising-security-in-banking/index.html) suna da kariya daga hare-haren kwamfutocin quantum, wanda hakan ya sa su zama mafi kyau don kare bayanan kuɗi masu mahimmanci.

## Idea

### Ɗakin Karatu na Hash (HSH): Jagorancin Interoperability a Cryptography mai Tsayayya da Quantum

Ɗakin Karatu na Hash (HSH) yana ba da mafita mai sauƙi, inganci, da sauƙin amfani don kare bayanai tare da cryptography mai tsayayya da quantum. Yana ba masu haɓakawa damar amfani da algoritmomi masu tsayayya da quantum a cikin aikace-aikacen su ba tare da buƙatar zurfin fahimtar algoritmomin cryptographic na asali ba.

An gina ɗakin karatun akan yaren shirye-shirye na Rust, wanda aka sani da sauri da inganci, kuma ya fi dacewa don cryptography, da amintaccen dogon lokaci.

## Impact

### Amfanin Ɗakin Karatu na Hash mai Tsayayya da Cryptographic Quantum

[Ɗakin Karatu na Hash (HSH) ⧉][00] yana ba da wadatattun kayan aikin cryptographic na zamani, yana samar da gagarumin shinge ga ƙalubalen zamanin quantum. Muhimmancinsa yana cikin kare bayanai masu mahimmanci a zamanin da kwamfutar quantum ke zama babban haɗari ga tsaron dijital.

Ɗakin karatun yana ba ƙungiyoyi da cibiyoyin kuɗi mafi girman matakin kariya da ake samu a kan layi tare da zaɓin algoritmomi, gami da Argon2i, BScrypt, da Scrypt. Waɗannan ayyuka ne amintattu na samar da maɓallai ta hanyar kalmar sirri (PBKDFs). Ana amfani da PBKDFs don canza kalmomin sirri zuwa maɓallan cryptographic. An tsara su don su kasance a hankali kuma suna cin ƙwaƙwalwar ajiya (memory-intensive), wanda ke sa su da wuya a fashe ta hanyar amfani da hare-haren ƙarfi (brute-force).

Bugu da ƙari, ɗakin karatun yana ba da tabbacin cewa ba wai kawai sakamakon yana da tsaro da inganci ba, har ma sun dace sosai don aikace-aikacen matakin kasuwanci, masu sauƙin haɓakawa, da sauƙin amfani.

## Incentives

### Kewaya Yanayin Kwamfutar Quantum Cikin Aminci

* **Tabbacin Tsaro**: Yin amfani da Ɗakin Karatu na Hash (HSH), yana ba ƙungiyoyi tabbacin cewa bayanan su sun kasance a cikin aminci.

* **Tabbatar da Gaba (Future-Proofing)**: Karɓar algoritmomi masu tsayayya da quantum yanzu zai kare ƙungiyoyi daga matsaloli masu yuwuwa a nan gaba.

* **Ingancin Kuɗi**: Ɗakin Karatu na Hash (HSH) kyauta ne na buɗaɗɗen tushe (open source) kuma ana iya amfani da shi ba tare da buƙatar lasisi masu tsada ko kuɗin rajista ba. Wannan ya sa ya zama zaɓi mai ban sha'awa ga ƙungiyoyin da ke neman rage kuɗaɗen su yayin da suke samun damar shiga kwamfutar quantum mai aminci.

### Kiyaye Amon Abokan Ciniki

* **Kare Bayanan Abokan Ciniki**: Kare bayanan abokan ciniki daga hare-haren kwamfutar quantum yana haɓaka amana ga iyawar ƙungiyoyi don kare bayanai.

* **Bin Doka da Dokoki**: Aiwatar da manyan hanyoyin cryptographic yana taimakawa wajen bin ƙaƙƙarfan dokokin kariya da ƙa'idodin bayanai, ta yadda za a guje wa sakamakon shari'a da tara.

## Conclusion

[Ɗakin Karatu na Hash (HSH) ⧉][00] yana ba da mafita mai sauƙi, inganci, da sauƙin amfani don kare bayanai tare da cryptography mai tsayayya da quantum. Yana bawa masu haɓakawa damar haɓaka tsarin cryptographic ɗinsu cikin sauƙi don zama mai tsayayya da quantum ba tare da zurfin fahimtar algoritmomin ba.

Cryptography mai tsayayya da quantum wani fanni ne da ke haɓaka cikin sauri, kuma ɗakin karatu na HSH ya jajirce wajen ci gaba da kasancewa a gaba. Ana sabunta ɗakin karatun akai-akai tare da sabbin algoritmomi da fasalulluka don karewa daga barazanar da ke fitowa.

[Hukumar Kula da Ma'auni da Fasaha ta Kasa (NIST) ⧉][02] a halin yanzu tana fayyace ƙa'idodin algoritmomin post-quantum cryptographic, ta hanyar aikinta na [Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) ⧉][03].

Kare bayanan ku daga hare-haren kwamfutar quantum yana da mahimmanci ga kowace ƙungiya da ke sarrafa bayanai masu mahimmanci. [Ɗakin Karatu na Hash (HSH) ⧉][00] kayan aiki ne mai ƙarfi wanda zai iya taimaka muku kare bayananku daga wannan barazana mai fitowa.
