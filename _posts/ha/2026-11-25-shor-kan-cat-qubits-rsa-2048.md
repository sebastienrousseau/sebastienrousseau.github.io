## Me ya sa Shor's algorithm har yanzu ke kafa ma'auni

Takardar ita ce "Shor's algorithm compiled on cat qubits: 22 593 qubits for RSA-2048 in 3.14 days" ta Axel Pappalardo, Dahmun Goudarzi da wasu marubuta takwas, galibinsu a Alice & Bob a Paris, wadda aka wallafa a 2026 a matsayin takarda 2026/2665 a IACR Cryptology ePrint Archive.

A shekarar 1994, Peter Shor ya gabatar da quantum algorithm don rarrabe babbar lamba (factoring) ko lissafin discrete logarithm a cikin polynomial time. Fiye da shekaru talatin bayan haka, ita ce har yanzu aikin ma'auni ga fault-tolerant quantum hardware, kuma dalilan na aiki ne, ba na shauƙi ba.

Na farko, matsalar mai sauƙin bayyanawa ce kuma sakamakon mai sauƙin tabbatarwa ne. Na biyu, babu sanannen classical algorithm da zai iya rarrabe lambobi masu girman cryptography, misali RSA keys na fiye da bits 2048, cikin lokaci mai ma'ana. Haɗa biyun kuma za ka sami gwaji mai tsafta: nasarar gudanar da Shor's algorithm a irin waɗannan girma hujja ce mai ƙarfi cewa an gudanar da lissafin a kan kwamfutar quantum. Babu wurin jayayya sosai kan ko amsar daidai ce, kuma babu wurin cewa classical machine ce ta yi aikin.

Siffar da ke sa algorithm ɗin ya zama gwaji mai kyau ita ce ke sa wucewarsa ta yi wuya. Saboda zurfin da yake buƙata, lissafin ba zai yi nasara ba sai da babban quantum error correction. Dole ne a aiwatar da biliyoyin logical operations ba tare da kuskure ba, matakai da dama sama da dubban ayyukan da na'ura marar gyaran kurakurai za ta iya aiwatarwa. Wannan giɓin shi ne dukkan labarin fannin. Babu wanda zai rufe shi ta gina physical qubits masu ɗan kyau kaɗan; dole ne a rufe shi ta encoding, kuma tambaya mai ban sha'awa ita ce a wane farashi.

Nau'o'in discrete logarithm ma suna da muhimmanci. Ga ECDLP, discrete logarithm a kan elliptic curves, mafi kyawun sanannen classical attack exponential ne. Haɓaka ECDLP algorithm ya ga hanyoyi uku daban-daban na ingantawa sun bayyana a wallafe-wallafen kwanan nan. A ɓangaren factoring, aikin da Chevignard et al. da Gidney suka yi a baya ya rage adadin logical qubits daga ninki da dama na girman key zuwa rabinsa. Algorithm ɗin bai tsaya cak ba, kuma dole ne a karanta duk wata ƙididdigar hardware a kan manufar algorithm da ke motsi.

## Kwatanta na'urori na buƙatar yarjejeniya kan ma'auni

Ƙididdigar albarkatu tana da amfani ne kawai idan za a iya kwatanta biyu daga cikinsu, kuma hakan na buƙatar yarjejeniya kan abin da za a ƙirga. Takardar ta lissafa aikace-aikacen da aka yi la'akari da shi, adadin logical qubits, adadin physical qubits, jimillar lokacin aiwatarwa da adadin shots. Kowanne yana da ma'anar da ya dace a kiyaye.

Logical qubits su ne qubits masu ƙarancin kuskure, waɗanda ke fitowa daga quantum error correction da aka yi a kan rukunin physical qubits da ake iya ƙerawa masu yawan kuskure. Adadin shots shi ne matsakaicin adadin aiwatar da algorithm kafin nasara. Musanya tsakanin adadin physical qubits da lokaci gabaɗaya mai yiwuwa ne, shi ya sa alƙalami ɗaya na kanun labarai zai iya ɓatarwa; algorithm ɗaya a kan hardware ɗaya za a iya ambato shi a maki da dama a kan lanƙwasa.

A cikin wallafe-wallafe, manyan ma'auni biyu da ake amfani da su don kwatanta gine-gine su ne adadin physical qubits da jimillar lokacin aiwatarwa. Waɗannan su ne lambobi biyu da wannan rubutu ke ci gaba da komawa gare su, kuma su ne biyun da aka bayyana sakamakon takardar da su.

Takardar ta kuma bayyana dalilin da ya sa waɗannan ma'auni suka inganta da sauri haka. Levers uku masu cin gashin kansu ne ke ba da damar inganta manyan ma'auni biyun kuma suna bayyana saurin haɓakar Shor's algorithm daga 1994 zuwa 2026. Ɗaya daga cikinsu, high-level algorithm, ya ƙunshi rage adadin logical qubits, adadin gates masu tsada, ko inganta yiwuwar nasara. Kowace wallafa da ke inganta aikin Shor's algorithm tana aiki a kan lever ɗaya zuwa uku, tana ayyana triplet: takamaiman algorithm, takamaiman hardware platform da takamaiman aiwatar da QEC. Triplet na wannan takarda shi ne Shor's algorithm tare da adders da aka daidaita, superconducting cat qubits, da gine-ginen cat-code guda biyu.

## Me ke sa cat qubit ya bambanta

Fasahar qubit da aka yi la'akari da ita ita ce dissipative cat qubit, wadda ke cikin dangin superconducting qubits. Wani nau'i, Kerr-cat qubit, ana daidaita shi ta two-photon drive maimakon haka. Transmons su ne nau'in superconducting da aka ɗauka a Gidney–Ekerå, don haka wurin kwatancen a fili yake.

Encoding ɗin ya bambanta a asali. Transmons suna encoding state a matakan makamashi biyu na anharmonic oscillator. Cat qubit kuwa yana encoding state a superpositions na coherent states biyu masu saɓanin phase na electromagnetic mode, waɗanda ake kira Schrödinger cat states.

Sakamakon da ke da muhimmanci ga ƙidayar albarkatu shi ne rashin daidaito a hayaniya (noise). Ga cat qubits, bit-flip rate yana raguwa exponentially tare da wani parameter da ake iya daidaitawa, adadin photons a cikin cat state, yayin da phase-flip rate ke ƙaruwa linearly kawai tare da wannan parameter. Za ka iya sayen babban kariya daga bit-flip ka biya ɗan ƙaramin hukuncin phase-flip kawai. Wannan karkata tsakanin nau'o'in hayaniya biyu tana ba da damar tsarin gyaran kurakurai mafi sauƙi, kamar repetition code architecture, tare da ƙarancin rabo sosai tsakanin logical qubits da physical qubits, domin phase-flips kaɗai ne ake buƙatar gyarawa. Error correction codes masu ci gaba, kamar LDPC codes, suna ƙara rage rabon.

Wannan ita ce hujjar dukkan aikin. Idan hardware ta ba ka noise channel da ya riga ya karkata, code da aka tsara a kan nau'in kuskure ɗaya kaɗai na buƙatar physical qubits ƙalilan sosai ga kowane logical qubit fiye da code da aka gina don hayaniya mai daidaito. Ƙididdigar wannan takarda gwaji ne na nisan da wannan hujja ke iya kaiwa ta cikin cikakken aiki da aka tattara.

An tsara noise model ɗin ya zama a taƙaice da gangan. An taƙaita shi da parameter ɗaya, pZ, phase-flip probability, wanda galibi rabo tsakanin κ2 (two-photon dissipation, tasirin da ke daidaita cat state) da κ1 (one-photon loss, tasirin da ke lalata cat state) ke tafiyar da shi. Marubutan sun kira wannan muhimmin siffar hardware. An ɗauka cewa bit flips ba su da tasiri. Riƙe wannan jimla ta ƙarshe; tana ɗauke da nauyi fiye da yadda take bayyana da farko.

## Daga algorithm zuwa physical qubits

An gina ƙididdigar ta matakan abstraction. Mataki mafi girma shi ne Algorithm layer, wanda a wannan aikin bayanin Shor's algorithm ne da subroutines ɗinsa. Ana bayyana algorithms a wannan mataki da block diagrams, pseudo-code da wakilcin lissafi na sauye-sauyen da ake yi a kan registers.

A ƙasansa akwai Logical layer, mataki mafi ƙasƙanci na abstraction da har yanzu bai dogara da fasahar quantum da aka yi la'akari da ita ba, don haka yana ba da damar kwatanta algorithms ba tare da la'akari da hardware ba. A wannan layer, ana bayyana kowane subroutine daidai a cikin logical qubits da logical gates ko logical operations. Wannan rabuwa tana da amfani ga duk mai kwatanta algorithms: ana iya auna shawarwari biyu a kan logical cost kafin kowa ya fara jayayya kan wace hardware ce za ta gudanar da su.

Takardar sannan ta ƙara sauka. An tattara manyan subroutines zuwa ayyukan physical qubits a kan gine-gine biyu, ɗaya a kan repetition cat codes, ɗaya kuma a kan LDPC cat codes. Wannan tattarawa ce ke bambanta wannan aiki da lissafin kimantawa a bayan takarda. Lambobin ba a fitar da su daga ƙidayar logical tare da tsayayyen ninkin overhead ba; sun fito ne daga ayyukan da aka tattara.

An buƙaci sababbin abubuwa da dama don isa can. Marubutan sun tsara parity-Cnot gate don cat qubits, sun faɗaɗa multi-target Cnot gate zuwa gine-ginen LDPC-cat, kuma sun daidaita hanyar active injection zuwa gine-ginen cat-qubit. Sun kuma daidaita zuwa gine-ginen cat-qubit adder daga ƙididdigar albarkatun Gidney ta 2025 don rarrabe RSA-2048. A ƙarshe, sun haɗa tattarawar adder ɗinsu da ƙididdigar albarkatu mafi ƙaranci ga phaseup tare da ƙirar factories da tattarawar lookup daga ƙididdigar albarkatun FeMoco ta Kroell et al.

Lura da sake amfani. Adder ɗin ya fito ne daga ƙididdigar factoring; factories da tattarawar lookup sun fito ne daga ƙididdigar chemistry. Aron abubuwa tsakanin aikace-aikace alama ce cewa ginshiƙan fault-tolerant computation suna zama ababen more rayuwa na tarayya maimakon ƙira ta musamman. Hakanan tunatarwa ce cewa an yi wa phaseup ƙididdiga mafi ƙaranci fiye da adder, don haka ba kowane ɓangare ke da daidaito iri ɗaya ba.

## Sakamakon: maki uku na bayanai

RSA-2048 ita ce tsarin da marubutan suka tattara gaba ɗaya, kuma a wannan tsari tana buƙatar kimanin cat qubits 23,000 da kwanaki 3.14. Idan aka musanya sarari da lokaci, tana gudana cikin awa 1.25 a kan kimanin cat qubits 611,000, musanyar da takardar ta fitar daga tsarin da aka tattara. Wannan alƙalami yana gefen mai kyau na lanƙwasar sarari da lokaci ta fuskar sauri: ɗan fiye da awa ɗaya ga girman key da babu sanannen classical algorithm da zai iya rarrabewa cikin lokaci mai ma'ana.

Maki na biyu shawara ce. An ayyana FFDLP-512 a matsayin misalin matsalar FFDLP ga prime group na bits 512 da exponent na bits 80. Marubutan sun gabatar da shi a matsayin ma'aunin tsaka-tsaki, kuma yana buƙatar kimanin cat qubits 6,700 da mintuna 21. Shor's algorithm ga FFDLP yana da tattalin sarari, wanda ya dace da adadin qubits da ke ƙasa da alƙalamin RSA-2048 da kimanin matakai biyu na girma (orders of magnitude).

Ina ganin ma'aunin tsaka-tsakin shi ne ra'ayi mafi amfani a takardar ga duk mai gina hardware. Manufar qubits 611,000 wurin isowa ne; ba ta gaya wa ƙungiya abin da za ta nuna a shekara mai zuwa ba. Ma'auni a kimanin cat qubits 6,700 da mintuna 21 yana ba da tasha mafi kusa da har yanzu ke gwada dangin algorithm ɗaya. Hujjar takardar ita ce FFDLP-512 tana amfani da dukkan blocks da ake buƙata ga RSA-2048, don haka za ta iya nuna ainihin aiwatar da Shor's algorithm daga farko zuwa ƙarshe ba tare da gajerun hanyoyi ba. Takardar ta kuma faɗi gaskiya cewa ana iya yin cryptanalysis na wannan matsala ta hanyar classical, don haka darajar ma'aunin ita ce tasha ga na'urar, ba karya tsaro a kansa ba.

## Hasashen shekaru 100

Yanzu zuwa jimlar da ya dace a riƙe. An ɗauka cewa bit flips ba su da tasiri, kuma takardar ta faɗi abin da hakan ke nufi a hardware: buƙatar rashin bit-flips tana daidai da bit-flip time na kimanin shekaru 100.

Wannan lamba ce mai tsanani, kuma marubutan ba su bar ta haka ba. Ana iya sassauta buƙatar zuwa bit-flip time na kimanin awa ɗaya ta amfani da ƙarin concatenation layer cikin distance-3 bit-flip error-correcting code. An riga an gabatar da gine-gine kamar Elevator code don samar da gyaran bit-flip, don haka ra'ayin ƙara layer da ke sarrafa ragowar bit-flip channel yana da tarihi.

An ƙiyasta farashin, kuma kalmomin takardar sun yi taka-tsantsan daidai: marubutan sun ƙiyasta cewa kuɗin RSA-2048 zai ƙaru zuwa kimanin cat qubits 45,000. Wannan ƙiyasi ne kuma bai kamata a karanta shi a matsayin sakamakon da aka tattara kamar alƙalamin 23,000 ba. Duk da haka, ya dace a faɗi siffar musanyar a fili. Outer code ɗin yana sassauta buƙatar bit-flip time da ninki fiye da miliyan ɗaya, a kuɗin kusan ninki biyu na physical qubits: daga kimanin 23,000 zuwa kimanin 45,000, tare da lokacin gudanarwa na aƙalla kwanaki 3.14.

Bai kamata a sanya alƙalamin 45,000 a gaban na 611,000 ba. Babbar lambar ita ce ƙarshen lanƙwasar sarari da lokaci mai tattalin lokaci; ƙaramar ita ce tsarin da aka tattara tare da ƙarin layer na kariyar bit-flip, don haka ƙari ne a kan 23,000, ba ragi daga 611,000 ba. Abin da za a iya faɗa shi ne buƙatar shekaru 100 hasashen ƙira ne ba dokar halitta ba, kuma marubutan sun gano hanyar concatenation da ke kewaye da ita.

## Karanta sakamakon a matsayin wanda ya kafa kamfani ko injiniya

Ga masu karatu na fasaha, darasin aiki shi ne cewa ƙididdigar tana saka wa duk wanda ya duba wane hasashe ne ke ɗauke da nauyi. Uku sun yi fice.

Na farko shi ne karkatar hayaniya. Komai game da ƙarancin rabo tsakanin logical da physical ya dogara ne kan cewa ana danne bit-flips exponentially a adadin photons yayin da phase-flips ke girma linearly kawai. Idan platform ba zai iya isar da wannan rashin daidaito ba, fa'idodin repetition-code da LDPC-code da aka bayyana a nan ba za su biyo baya ba.

Na biyu shi ne rabon κ2 zuwa κ1. Takardar ta kira shi muhimmin siffar hardware, kuma tun da noise model ɗin ya ragu zuwa parameter ɗaya pZ wanda galibi wannan rabo ke tafiyar da shi, ita ce lambar da za a sa ido a kanta a kowane taswirar hardware ta cat-qubit. Ƙungiyar da za ta iya ba da rahoton pZ a ƙarƙashin yanayin aiki na zahiri tana ba ka bayanin da wannan ƙididdiga ta fi damuwa da shi, aƙalla bisa noise model na takardar kanta.

Na uku shi ne bit-flip time, wanda muka riga muka tattauna. Tare, waɗannan hasashe uku suna ayyana yankin da manyan lambobin ke tabbata a cikinsa.

Ga waɗanda suka kafa kamfanoni, karatu mai amfani ya fi hasashe ƙunci. Takardar ba ta ba da ranar da za a gina kowane ɗayan wannan ba, kuma babu abin da ke cikin takardar da ya kamata a ɗauka a matsayin haka. Abin da take bayarwa shi ne taswira da aka daidaita daga siffofin hardware zuwa ƙidayar albarkatu, tare da kwatancen gine-gine biyu da aka bayyana da kuma ma'aunin tsaka-tsaki. Irin wannan aiki ne ke bai wa mai zuba jari ko ƙungiyar hardware damar yin tambaya madaidaiciya: shin qubit ɗinku ya cika buƙatun pZ da bit-flip da ƙididdigar ta ɗauka?

Akwai kuma batu game da hanya. Marubutan sun tattara manyan subroutines maimakon faɗaɗawa daga ƙidayar logical, sun sake amfani da ɓangarori daga wasu ƙididdigar da aka wallafa, kuma sun faɗi inda suka samar da alƙalami mafi ƙaranci kawai, kamar a phaseup. Wannan bayyana gaskiya game da waɗanne ɓangarori ne dalla-dalla da waɗanne ne a kimanta shi ke sa a iya duba ƙididdigar, kuma iya dubawa ita ce siffar da ta fi muhimmanci a fannin da manyan lambobi ke motsawa da matakan girma tsakanin takardu.

## Inda ƙididdigar ta bar fannin

Saurin haɓakar Shor's algorithm daga 1994 zuwa 2026 ya fito ne daga levers uku masu cin gashin kansu, kuma wannan takarda ta ja dukkansu ta hanyarta: daidaita adder ta fuskar algorithm, takamaiman hardware platform a superconducting cat qubits, da aiwatar da QEC biyu da aka tattara zuwa ayyukan physical. Sakamakon shi ne jerin alƙaluma bayyanannu ga RSA-2048: kimanin cat qubits 23,000 da kwanaki 3.14 a tsarin da aka tattara, awa 1.25 a kan kimanin cat qubits 611,000 idan aka musanya sarari da lokaci, da ƙiyasin cat qubits 45,000 idan bit-flip time kimanin awa ɗaya ne kawai kuma ƙarin concatenation layer ke sarrafa bit-flips.

Tambayar da takardar ta tayar ita ce wadda ke tsakanin alƙaluman 23,000 da 45,000. Bit-flip time na kimanin shekaru 100 manufa ce mai wuya; bit-flip time na kimanin awa ɗaya ta fi sauƙi sosai, a kuɗin distance-3 code layer wanda marubutan suka ƙiyasta overhead ɗinsa maimakon tattarawa. Ko ƙungiyoyin hardware za su ga ya fi sauƙi su tura bit-flip times zuwa alƙalami mafi tsanani, ko su karɓi gajeren lokacin su biya ƙarin layer, shi ne shawarar da wannan ƙididdiga ta ajiye a gabansu. Kuma hanya mafi tsafta ta ganowa fiye da jayayya kan RSA-2048 tana nan a kan tebur: a gina zuwa FFDLP-512, a kimanin cat qubits 6,700 da mintuna 21, a ga wace hanya cikin biyun za ta fara isa can.

## Tambayoyin da Ake Yawan Yi

**Cat qubits nawa takardar ta ƙiyasta don rarrabe RSA-2048?**
A tsarin da aka tattara gaba ɗaya, kimanin cat qubits 23,000 (22,593) da kwanaki 3.14. Idan aka musanya sarari da lokaci, RSA-2048 tana gudana cikin awa 1.25 a kan kimanin cat qubits 611,000. Waɗannan alƙaluma sun ɗauka error-correction cycle na 1 µs, reaction time na 10 µs, phase-flip noise mai pZ = 5 × 10⁻⁴ da rashin bit-flips.

**Me alƙalamin qubits 45,000 ke nufi?**
Ƙiyasin marubutan ne ga RSA-2048 idan bit-flip time kimanin awa ɗaya ne kawai, don haka ana buƙatar ƙarin distance-3 bit-flip code layer. Ƙari ne a kan tsarin kimanin qubits 23,000, tare da lokacin gudanarwa na aƙalla kwanaki 3.14, ba ragi daga alƙalamin qubits 611,000 ba.

**Menene FFDLP-512 kuma me ya sa aka gabatar da shi?**
FFDLP-512 misali ne na finite-field discrete logarithm problem ga prime group na bits 512 da exponent na bits 80. Takardar ta gabatar da shi a matsayin ma'aunin tsaka-tsaki da ke amfani da dukkan blocks da ake buƙata ga RSA-2048, a kimanin cat qubits 6,700 da mintuna 21. Ana iya yin cryptanalysis ɗinsa ta hanyar classical, don haka tasha ce ta hardware, ba karya tsaro a kansa ba.

**Me ya sa cat qubits ke buƙatar ƙarancin physical qubits ga kowane logical qubit?**
Ga cat qubits, bit-flip rate yana raguwa exponentially tare da adadin photons a cikin cat state, yayin da phase-flip rate ke ƙaruwa linearly kawai. Wannan karkata na nufin phase-flips kaɗai ne ake buƙatar gyarawa, wanda ke ba da damar repetition code architecture tare da ƙarancin rabo sosai tsakanin logical qubits da physical qubits. LDPC cat codes suna ƙara rage rabon.

**Wane parameter na hardware takardar ta kira muhimmi?**
An taƙaita noise model da parameter ɗaya, pZ, phase-flip probability, wanda galibi rabo tsakanin two-photon dissipation (κ2) da one-photon loss (κ1) ke tafiyar da shi. Takardar ta kira wannan muhimmin siffar hardware. Bit-flip time shi ne ɗayan hasashen da ke ɗauke da manyan lambobin.
