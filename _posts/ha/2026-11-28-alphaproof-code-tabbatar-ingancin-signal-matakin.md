## Me ya sa wannan sakamako ke da muhimmanci ga masu fitar da cryptographic software

Ana bayyana Signal a matsayin ma'auni na duniya a aikace don saƙonni masu end-to-end encryption, kuma an tura nau'ukan protocol ɗin a WhatsApp, manhajar Messenger ta Meta, da Google Messages. Idan protocol yana ƙarƙashin tattaunawa masu yawa haka, gibin da ke tsakanin zane da aka tabbatar yana da tsaro a takarda da compiled code da ke aiki a zahiri ya daina zama batun ilimi kawai. Proof a takarda na iya zama babu aibi yayin da implementation ke yin wani abu daban gaba ɗaya.

Wannan gibin ne aikin ke kai hari. Marubutan sun bayar da proofs na farko na halayen tsaro a matakin protocol don wani implementation na cryptographic protocol da aka tura sosai kuma ba a canza shi ba. Ka sake karanta wannan jumlar, domin kowane ƙayyadewa na da aiki. Matakin protocol na nufin proofs ɗin sun shafi halaye kamar forward secrecy, ba kawai rashin kurakuran memory ba. Aka tura sosai na nufin libsignal, ba samfurin bincike ba. Ba a canza ba na nufin babu wanda ya sake rubuta code ɗin zuwa siffa mafi sauƙi ga prover.

Ga waɗanda suka kafa kamfanoni, karatun aikace shi ne kan farashi. Marubutan sun gabatar da formal verification a matsayin madadin neman bugs bayan sun bayyana: mutum yana ayyana halayen daidaito da tsaro da dole tsarin manhaja ya aiwatar, sannan ya tabbatar ta hanyar lissafi cewa tsarin ya cika su. Sun bayyana cewa a tarihi ta yi tsada ga yawancin manhajoji. Sakamakon marubutan ya nuna cewa AI na iya sa ta zama mai yiwuwa ga tsare-tsare na zahiri. Wannan iƙirari ne mai sharaɗi, kuma zan ɗauke shi a haka: shawara ce da babban case study ɗaya ke goyon baya, ba sakamakon tattalin arziki da aka tabbatar ba. Duk da haka, case study na wannan girma shaida ce mafi nauyi fiye da yawancin iƙirarin da ake samu a wannan fanni.

## Matsalar verification ta fi yadda take gani wahala

Kayan tushe na tsarin sun nuna wani sanannen ma'auni na yadda verification ke da wahala: CompCert, C compiler da aka tabbatar, wanda ya ɗauki shekaru shida na aikin mutum ɗaya kafin a tabbatar da shi, bisa ga adadin da aka ruwaito a nan. Ka yi hankali da wannan adadi, domin bayanin da ke cikin baka game da shekarun aiki ginin tushen ne, ba ainihin magana da aka ɗauko ba. Batun yana nan duk da haka: tabbatar da manhaja mai nauyi alƙawari ne na shekaru da dama.

An ce tabbatar da code mai ƙarfin masana'antu da ke akwai ya fi wahala, domin prover ba zai iya canza code ɗin don sauƙaƙa proof ba. Laburaren production da optimising compiler ya haɗa shi ne ainihin wannan yanayi. Idan kana da 'yancin sake tsara shirin, za ka iya siffanta shi har proof ta fito cikin sauƙi. Idan dole ne ka ɗauki code ɗin yadda yake, za ka gaji kowane salon rubutu mai wahala da kowace shawarar compiler.

AlphaProof Code ya zaɓi hanya mafi wahala da gangan. Ana haɗa Rust code ɗin da ba a canza ba da compiler na yau da kullum, sannan a loda executable ɗin cikin Lean tare da semantics na AArch64. A zahiri, marubutan sun haɗa libsignal cikin ƙaramin command-line harness kuma suka haɗa shi da rustc compiler na yau da kullum zuwa AArch64 Linux machine code. Abin da ake tabbatarwa shi ne machine code. Wannan manufa ce mafi ƙarfi fiye da tunani a matakin source, domin ba sai ka amince da compiler cewa zai kiyaye halayen da ka nuna ba.

## Yadda tsarin ke raba aiki

AlphaProof Code tsari ne na haɗin gwiwa tsakanin ɗan Adam da AI kan formal specification da verification na manhajoji na zahiri. Ya dogara kan ƙa'ida da na ga tana da gamsarwa: yawancin ƙoƙarin ɗan Adam a software verification yanzu yana cikin gina specification. Rubuta proof, ɓangaren da a da ke cinye shekarun ƙwararru, yana ƙara zama abin da na'urori za su iya yi.

Marubutan sun yi hujja, daga sakamakonsu, cewa ko manyan proofs game da manhaja ƙungiyoyin AI agents za su iya rubuta su da zarar an tsayar da specification, kuma proof agents na iya aiki da kansu saboda kernel na Lean na duba kowace proof a ƙarshe. Tsarinsu ya raba aikin gida biyu. Mai amfani ɗan Adam da AI agents suna rubuta Lean specifications na code na zahiri tare, kuma da zarar mai amfani ya tantance specification, ƙungiyar autonomous agents tana tabbatar da cewa code ɗin ya cika ta. Marubutan sun bayyana a fili cewa, ga manyan tsare-tsare masu sarƙaƙiya, taimakon AI na iya rage farashin rubuta specification da assurance amma ba zai maye gurbin hukunci da bita na ɗan Adam gaba ɗaya ba.

Matakin specification ba sa hannu kawai ba ne. Ƙungiyar reviewer agents tana duba ko draft yana bin tushensa da implementation da aminci. A tsarin marubutan, mai amfani yana bita, gyara da amincewa da specification kafin a rubuta kowace proof, kuma git commit ɗinta ya zama baseline da gatekeeper zai duba proofs da shi daga baya. Sun ruwaito cewa yana iya ɗaukar zagaye da yawa na iteration tsakanin mai amfani da agents kafin a sami specification mai kyau, ko da akwai samfuri a cikin littattafan bincike; ga Signal, haɗa samfuran handshake da ratchet na baya da rufe sabon post-quantum ratchet ya ɗauki zagaye da dama na drafts da bita. Marubutan sun ba da shawarar raba alhaki: marubuta 'yan Adam suna zaɓar abin da za a tabbatar, warware rashin bayyana, auna security trade-offs da yanke dukkan shawarwarin ƙarshe, yayin da AI agents ke rubuta da gwada specifications ƙarƙashin jagorancin ɗan Adam. Na ɗauki wannan matsayi a matsayin daidai. Yana ba da hukunci ga ɓangaren da za a iya ɗora wa alhakinsa.

Dukkan agents a AlphaProof Code, har da mai rubuta specification, coding agents ne na gama-gari waɗanda skills ke ayyana halayensu, wato takardu da aka rubuta da harshen ɗan Adam. Babu wani tsarin prover na musamman da aka bayyana a nan. Takardun rubutu ne ke jagorantar halayen, abin da ke sa tsarin ya zama mai sauƙin dubawa da gyarawa idan aka kwatanta.

## Inda amincewa take a zahiri

Muhimmiyar shawarar zane a tsarin ita ce abin da ya ƙi amincewa da shi. Ba a amince da proofs masu layuka miliyoyi da AI agents suka samar ba, domin kernel na Lean na ƙin kowane kuskuren hankali a cikin proof. A wata ma'ana, saboda kernel na Lean ne ke duba proof a ƙarshe, amincin sakamakon bai dogara kan amincin proving agents ba.

Wannan yana da muhimmanci domin, kamar yadda marubutan suka faɗa, amincin sakamakon bai dogara kan amincin proving agents ba: kernel na Lean ne ke duba proof a ƙarshe. Bisa ga takardar, gatekeeper agent yana karɓar proof ne kawai idan kernel na Lean ya duba ta kuma Lean comparator ya tabbatar cewa tana tabbatar da ainihin kwafin specification da aka ajiye a gefe. Sharuɗɗa biyu ke nan. Dole proof ta zama ingantacciya, kuma dole ta zama proof ta bayanin da ya dace. An tsara sharaɗi na biyu don kare kai daga wata gazawa mai wayo: agent da ke canza bayanin kuma ya tabbatar da wani abu mafi sauƙi fiye da abin da aka nema. Comparator yana duba cewa bayanin da kowane definition da ya dogara da shi iri ɗaya ne da kwafin da aka ajiye, kuma proof tana amfani da standard axioms na Lean kawai.

Saboda haka trusted base ƙarami ne idan aka kwatanta da proof ɗin kanta. Kusan layuka 27,000 na specifications da ake amincewa da su da AArch64 semantics ke riƙe da kusan layuka miliyan 6.3 na proof. Hankalin mai bita yana da iyaka, kuma wannan rabo na nuna maka inda za ka kashe shi.

Amma specification ita ce wurin rauni, kuma marubutan sun faɗi haka a fili. Specification a AlphaProof Code ta ƙunshi saitin Lean theorem statements tare da kowane definition da suka dogara da shi. Dole a amince da ita kuma ba za a iya duba ta kai tsaye ta na'ura ba, kuma kuskure na iya raunana tabbacin da theorem ke bayarwa. Theorem da ke yin assumptions masu tsauri fiye da kima kan inputs, ke bayyana halaye masu rauni kawai na output, ko ke sanya ƙayyadaddun sharuɗɗa kaɗan kan existentially quantified witnesses na iya tabbata ko da code yana da bugs. Idan kuskure a specification ya raunana ƙarfin attacker, tabbacin tsaro ma ya raunana.

Definitions na iya ƙunsar extern contracts da ke ayyana assumptions game da sassan tsarin da ke wajen iyakar verification campaign. Iyakoki ne na gaskiya, amma iyakoki ne: duk abin da ke bayan extern contract ana ɗauka ne, ba a tabbatar ba.

Don haka babban adadin, layuka miliyan 6.3, shi ne mafi ƙarancin muhimmanci wajen auna ko ya kamata ka amince da sakamakon. Layuka 27,000 ne ya kamata a karanta.

## Abin da aka tabbatar game da Signal

Proofs ɗin sun shafi PQXDH handshake na post-quantum da Triple Ratchet na Signal, kuma sun tabbatar da forward secrecy, authentication, post-compromise security da handshake secrecy. An tsara PQXDH handshake da Triple Ratchet don tabbatar da tsaron saƙonni ko da an tona wasu daga cikin keys, kuma har ma kan abokan gaba na nan gaba da ke da damar amfani da quantum computer.

Al'adun modelling guda biyu na gargajiya ne ke tsara ma'anar "tsaro" a nan. Symbolic model, wanda kuma ake kira Dolev–Yao model, yana ɗaukar cryptographic primitives a matsayin "black boxes" marasa siffa da ke cika daidaitattun halayen tsaro. Computational model yana iyakance damar nasarar attacker mai inganci bisa wahalar primitives da ke ƙasa. Computational security proof na bayyana cewa akwai reduction daga hari kan protocol zuwa keta wani assumption da ke ƙasa.

Samfuran biyu suna ba da tabbaci daban-daban, kuma an raba sakamakon bisa haka. Proofs da agents suka rubuta sun tabbatar da forward secrecy, post-compromise security, da sirri da sahihancin saƙo kai tsaye don compiled binary kan classical attackers. A symbolic model, sun tabbatar da handshake secrecy kan quantum attacker na nan gaba. Mai karatu mai hankali ya kamata ya riƙe wannan bambanci. Sakamakon kan quantum adversary an bayyana shi a symbolic model, kuma sakamakon kan classical attackers su ne aka bayyana kai tsaye don compiled binary.

Akwai wata matsala ta biyu da ke bayyana dalilin da ya sa aikin specification ya yi wahala. Specifications da ke akwai suna nazarin handshake da ratchet daban-daban kuma sun riga post-quantum ratchet na Signal; formal models na Signal na baya sun yi nazarin handshake ɗinsa na farko da ratchet ɗinsa daban-daban. Proof game da protocol yadda aka tura shi tana buƙatar su biyun tare, da kuma post-quantum ratchet. Da jagorancin ɗan Adam, agents sun faɗaɗa specifications da ke akwai zuwa protocol yadda aka tura shi. Wannan misali ne mai kyau na collaboration model a aikace: littattafan bincike suna ba da mafari, 'yan Adam suna jagoranci, agents kuma suna yin faɗaɗawa.

Marubutan sun kuma ce aikinsu ya nuna cewa AI na iya tabbatar da machine code kan specifications masu zurfi daga littattafan bincike, har da halayen mu'amala tsakanin stateful program da adaptive adversary. Wannan rukuni ne na halaye da ya cancanci lura. Stateful program da ke fuskantar adaptive adversary shi ne ainihin yanayin da messaging client ke ciki.

Marubutan sun yi iƙirarin kasancewa na farko: a kalmominsu, waɗannan su ne proofs na farko da suka haɗa tabbacin tsaro a matakin protocol da code ɗin da ba a canza ba na wani implementation na cryptographic protocol da ake amfani da shi sosai. Iƙirarinsu ne, ba bincike mai zaman kansa ba, kuma na ruwaito shi a haka. Sun bayyana sakamakon a matsayin babban mataki zuwa ga wani babban ƙalubale da aka ƙaddamar a watan Afrilu 2026.

## Shin specifications na kama kurakurai na gaske?

Specification da ba za ta iya bambanta code mai kyau da code mai lalacewa ba ba ta da amfani, kuma proof kan ta ba ta da daraja ma fiye da haka. Marubutan sun gwada wannan kai tsaye. Sun sake shigar da bugs biyu na libsignal da aka gyara a baya kuma suka gina rukunin synthetic mutants 17 don duba ko specifications na kama kurakurai na gaske.

Sakamakon, kamar yadda marubutan suka ruwaito: ga bugs biyu na tarihi da kuma, zuwa yanzu, 16 cikin 17 na synthetic mutants, Lean na tabbatar da cewa trace da aka naɗa (witness trace ɗaya kowanne) ba za a iya bayyana shi da symbolic specification ba ƙarƙashin wata fassarar primitives ta zahiri da ta dace da run ɗin da aka naɗa. Cikakkun binary refutations na bugs biyu na tarihi kernel ya duba su ne kawai kan wani tsohon (36-hook) untrusted placed variant na bayanin; mayar da su zuwa trusted statement na babban theorem yana nan a buɗe.

Ina so in yi daidai kan abin da wannan ke faɗi da wanda ba ya faɗi. Goma sha shida cikin goma sha bakwai ba goma sha bakwai cikin goma sha bakwai ba ne, kuma case study ɗin bai yi bayani kan mutant ɗin da ya rage ba, don haka ba zan yi hasashe a kansa ba. Ka kuma lura da sharaɗin kan bugs na tarihi: an duba cikakkun binary refutations kan wani tsohon untrusted placed variant na bayanin mai 36 hooks, ba lallai na ƙarshe ba. Wannan dalla-dalla ne da ya kamata a sani kafin wani ya ambaci sakamakon a matsayin nasara cikakkiya.

Duk da haka, wannan nau'in gwaji ne da ya dace. Yana kai hari kan specification, inda tsarin ya ce haɗarin da ya rage yake, maimakon nuna yawan proof. Mutation testing na specification yana da arha idan aka kwatanta da verification campaign, kuma yana amsa tambayar da babu yawan proof da kernel ya duba da zai iya amsawa: shin wannan bayanin zai lura da bug na gaske?

## Abin da wannan ke nufi a aikace

Iyakokin sun cancanci kulawa daidai da matakin da aka cimma. Ga manyan tsare-tsare masu sarƙaƙiya, taimakon AI na iya rage farashin rubuta specification da assurance amma ba zai maye gurbin hukunci da bita na ɗan Adam gaba ɗaya ba. Marubutan ba su ce akasin haka ba. Tattalin arziki yana canzawa, amma ba ya faɗuwa zuwa sifili, kuma ɓangaren da ya rage na ɗan Adam shi ne ke tantance ko tabbacin yana da ma'ana.

Ina ganin wannan rabo daidai ne, kuma yana nuna hanya mai hankali da ƙungiya za ta karanta wannan aiki. Kada ka tambayi ko agents za su iya rubuta proofs; sakamakon libsignal, mai kusan layuka miliyan 6.3, ya amsa wannan. Ka tambayi wa a ƙungiyarka zai iya rubuta da tantance specification irin wadda aka bayyana a nan: Lean theorem statements tare da kowane definition da suka dogara da shi, da extern contracts da ke nuna abin da ake ɗauka. Wannan mutumin, da tsarin bita da ke kewaye da aikinsa, shi ne albarkatun da ba su da yawa.

Akwai kuma darasi na zane a yadda tsarin ke mu'amala da sassa marasa aminci. Ba ya ƙoƙarin sa agents su zama amintattu. Yana sa amincinsu ya zama ba shi da tasiri kan sakamakon ta hanyar wucewa da komai ta ƙaramin checker tsayayye. Ƙungiyoyin da ke gina agentic systems a wasu fannoni na iya yin tambaya iri ɗaya kan pipelines ɗinsu: mene ne kwatankwacin kernel na Lean, kuma shin gatekeeper yana duba bayanin da ya dace?

Ga masu karatu da ke wajen cryptography, abin da za a iya amfani da shi a wasu wurare shi ne siffar workflow, ba protocol ɗin musamman ba. A zanen marubutan, 'yan Adam da agents suna rubuta specification tare, reviewer agents suna duba amincinta, ɗan Adam yana amincewa da ita, commit yana daskare ta a matsayin baseline, autonomous agents suna tabbatar da ita, kuma gatekeeper yana duba cewa kowace proof ingantacciya ce kuma tana tabbatar da ainihin bayanin da aka amince da shi. Kowane mataki yana kai hari kan nau'in gazawa daban.

Bai kamata a zuzuta kowane ɗayan waɗannan ba. Shawarar cewa AI na sa verification ta zama mai yiwuwa ga tsare-tsare na zahiri tana da sharaɗi a tushen, kuma babban misali ɗaya, ko yaya yake da ban sha'awa, misali ɗaya ne. Specification ta kasance abu da ake amincewa da shi wanda babu kayan aiki da ke duba maka shi. Kuma tabbacin kan quantum adversary sakamako ne na symbolic model don handshake secrecy, ba na computational ba.

Tambayar da ke buɗe da case study ɗin ya taso ita ce wadda marubutan da kansu suka nuna ta hanyar kiran wannan babban mataki zuwa ga babban ƙalubale da aka ƙaddamar a watan Afrilu 2026: idan rubuta proof yanzu yana da arha da zarar specification ta wanzu, shin specification engineering zai zama matsewar hanya ta gaba da za a kai wa hari? Sakamakon mutation ya nuna cewa za a iya gwada specification, kuma binciken "zagaye da yawa na iteration" ya nuna cewa har yanzu tana da jinkiri. Nawa daga cikin wannan jinkirin agents za su iya ɗauka, yayin da 'yan Adam ke riƙe da shawarwarin ƙarshe? Wannan shi ne adadin da ya kamata a sa ido a kansa a case study na gaba.

## Tambayoyin da Ake Yawan Yi

**Mene ne AlphaProof Code?**
Tsari ne daga Google DeepMind da Google don haɗin gwiwa tsakanin ɗan Adam da AI kan formal specification da verification na manhajoji na zahiri. Mai amfani ɗan Adam da AI agents suna rubuta Lean specifications tare; da zarar mai amfani ya tantance specification, ƙungiyar autonomous agents tana tabbatar da cewa code ɗin ya cika ta.

**Me aka tabbatar daidai a libsignal?**
Rust code na libsignal ba tare da an canza shi ba, an haɗa shi da rustc compiler na yau da kullum zuwa AArch64 Linux machine code. Proofs ɗin sun shafi forward secrecy, authentication, post-compromise security da handshake secrecy don PQXDH handshake na post-quantum da Triple Ratchet na Signal.

**Me ya sa ba sai an amince da AI agents ba?**
Kernel na Lean na duba kowace proof, kuma gatekeeper yana karɓar proof ne kawai idan Lean comparator ya tabbatar cewa tana tabbatar da ainihin kwafin specification da aka amince da ita da aka ajiye a gefe. Ana ƙin kuskuren hankali, haka ma proof ta bayani mafi sauƙi.

**Me har yanzu dole a amince da shi?**
Kusan layuka 27,000 na specifications da AArch64 semantics, da duk wani extern contract da ke bayyana sassan tsarin da ke wajen campaign ɗin. Ba za a iya duba specification kai tsaye ta na'ura ba, kuma kuskure a cikinta na raunana tabbacin da theorem ke bayarwa.

**Shin aikin ya tabbatar da tsaro kan quantum attackers?**
Wani ɓangare. An tabbatar da handshake secrecy kan quantum attacker na nan gaba a symbolic model. An tabbatar da forward secrecy, post-compromise security, da sirri da sahihancin saƙo kai tsaye don compiled binary kan classical attackers.
