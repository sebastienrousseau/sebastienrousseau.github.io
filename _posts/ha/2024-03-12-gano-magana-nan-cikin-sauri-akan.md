Wannan labarin yana gabatar da taƙaitaccen bayani kan [**takardar bincike**][00] wacce ke binciko haɗin OpenAI Whisper tare da Metal Performance Shaders (MPS) akan macOS, tana ba da sabuwar hanya don gane magana a lokaci na gaske. OpenAI Whisper babban samfurin automatic speech recognition (ASR) ne na zamani wanda aka horar da shi akan babban dataset na sautuka daban-daban kuma yana iya fassara magana a cikin harsuna da yawa. Haɗin babban tsarin neural network na Whisper da hanzarin GPU na MPS yana ba da damar inganta gudu da daidaito don sarrafa magana akan na'ura (on-device speech processing), yana haɓaka sirrin mai amfani da sauƙi yayin da yake buɗe sabbin damammaki ga masu haɓaka application don haɗa damar speech-to-text na lokaci na gaske kai tsaye cikin applications na macOS.

## Gabatarwa

Fasahar gane magana tana taka muhimmiyar rawa wajen sauƙaƙe aikace-aikace masu yawa, tun daga haɓaka damar shiga (accessibility) zuwa sauƙaƙe hulɗar mai amfani. Neman samun babban aminci (high-fidelity) da ƙarancin jinkiri (low-latency) ASR ya kasance mafi yawancin lokuta a cikin ikon manyan cloud servers ne, wanda ke haifar da ƙalubale dangane da damar shiga, sirri, da jinkiri. Koyaya, binciken kwanan nan ya gabatar da wani canji mai sauyi: haɗin OpenAI Whisper tare da hanzarin GPU da Metal Performance Shaders (MPS) ke bayarwa akan macOS. Wannan haɗin gwiwa yana wakiltar babban ci gaba a cikin damar gane magana akan na'ura (on-device speech recognition) kuma ya yi daidai da ƙarin fifikon da ake bayarwa akan sirrin mai amfani da tsaron bayanai.

[**Metal Performance Shaders (MPS)**][01] fasaha ce da Apple ya haɓaka wacce ke ba da damar babban aikin GPU (high-performance GPU computation) akan na'urorin macOS. Yana ba masu haɓakawa damar amfani da ikon GPU don sarrafa abubuwa a lokaci guda (parallel processing), wanda ke haifar da ingantaccen gudu a cikin ayyukan lissafi daban-daban, gami da machine learning da computer vision.

![divider][divider].class=\"m-10 w-100\"

### 1. Juyin Halitta na Gane Magana a macOS

Juyin halitta na fasahar gane magana akan na'urorin macOS ya samo asali ne daga ci gaban samfuran neural network da fasahohin hardware acceleration. Tsofaffin tsarin gane magana galibi suna fuskantar ƙalubale wajen daidaito, jinkiri, da ingancin kwamfuta, musamman lokacin da ake hulɗa da lafuzza daban-daban, surutun baya, da yanayin rikodi daban-daban. Gabatar da OpenAI Whisper ya kafa wani sabon ma'auni don ingantaccen kuma madaidaicin gane magana a cikin harsuna da lafuzza daban-daban, yana ba da mafita mai dacewa don aikace-aikacen lokaci na gaske.

![divider][divider].class=\"m-10 w-100\"

### 2. Amfani da OpenAI Whisper da Metal Performance Shaders

Takardar binciken ta bayyana wata sabuwar hanya ta hanyar haɗa manyan abubuwan da OpenAI Whisper ke da su tare da babban aikin kwamfuta (high-performance computation) na MPS akan macOS. An sami wannan haɗin kai ta hanyar inganta samfurin Whisper don yin aiki akan GPU ta amfani da tsarin MPS framework, wanda ke ba da damar ingantaccen parallel processing. Masu binciken sun aiwatar da fasahohi kamar model quantization da pruning don rage girman samfurin da buƙatun kwamfuta yayin da suke riƙe babban daidaito. Ta hanyar amfani da damar parallel processing na GPU, tsarin yana samun ingantaccen gudu mai ban mamaki, tare da saurin fassara wanda ya kai sau 8 zuwa 12 fiye da lokaci na gaske don maganganu na yau da kullum. Wannan yana haɓaka ƙwarewar mai amfani ta hanyar rage lokutan jira kuma yana ba da damar aikace-aikacen lokaci na gaske mafi faɗi, daga live captioning zuwa tsarin sarrafa murya mai ma'amala (interactive voice-controlled systems).

![divider][divider].class=\"m-10 w-100\"

### 3. Tasiri ga Masu Amfani da Masu Haɓakawa

Haɗin Whisper da MPS akan macOS yana da tasiri mai mahimmanci ga duka end-users da masu haɓaka application. Ga masu amfani, yana ba da ingantacciyar ƙwarewa a cikin gane magana na lokaci na gaske, yana ba da fassarar kusan nan da nan tare da babban daidaito yayin kiyaye sirri da tsaro na sarrafawa akan na'ura (on-device processing). Ana iya amfani da wannan fasaha a cikin yanayi daban-daban na zahiri, kamar aikace-aikacen sarrafa murya don sarrafa gida ta atomatik (home automation), sabis na fassarar lokaci na gaske don tarurruka da laccoci, da kuma siffofin accessibility ga masu amfani da ke da matsalar ji. Masu haɓakawa suna samun damar shiga akwatin kayan aiki (toolkit) don haɗa aikin speech-to-text cikin applications ɗinsu, tare da ƙarin fa'idodi na ingancin amfani da makamashi da haɗin gwiwar Python mara matsala.

![divider][divider].class=\"m-10 w-100\"

### 4. Tura Karɓa da Ƙirƙira

Tsarin modular architecture da aiwatar da Python na wannan tsarin yana sauƙaƙe haɗin kai cikin applications da ake da su kuma yana rage shingen shiga ga masu haɓaka da ke neman haɗa damar gane magana (speech recognition). Koyaya, masu haɓakawa na iya fuskantar ƙalubale dangane da daidaita samfurin (model customization) da dacewa da takamaiman lokutan amfani, gami da inganta aiki don tsarin hardware daban-daban. Takardar binciken tana ba da jagora kan magance waɗannan ƙalubale, kamar fine-tuning na samfurin akan bayanan takamaiman yanki (domain-specific data) da aiwatar da dabarun rarraba albarkatu masu ƙarfi (dynamic resource allocation). Bugu da ƙari, tsarin voice activity detection mai adana makamashi, wanda ke samun 94% precision da 96% recall, yana tabbatar da cewa applications sun kasance masu saurin amsawa da daidaito ba tare da cinye albarkatun na'ura ba. Wannan haɗin fasalulluka yana da damar tura karɓa tsakanin masu haɓakawa da haɓaka ƙarin ƙirƙira a fagen gane magana na lokaci na gaske.

![divider][divider].class=\"m-10 w-100\"

## Kammalawa

Haɗin OpenAI Whisper da Metal Performance Shaders akan macOS yana wakiltar babban ci gaba a fasahar gane magana na lokaci na gaske. Ta hanyar ba da ingantaccen gudu, daidaito, da inganci, wannan ƙirƙira tana haɓaka ƙwarewar mai amfani kuma tana buɗe sabbin damammaki don haɓaka application. Wannan binciken yana ba da gudummawa ga ci gaba da ci gaban fasahohin AI kuma yana da yuwuwar ƙarfafa ƙarin ci gaba a cikin sarrafa magana akan na'ura (on-device speech processing) a duk faɗin dandamali daban-daban. Yayin da wannan fasaha ke ci gaba da haɓaka, tana da yuwuwar kawo sauyi kan yadda masu amfani ke mu'amala da na'urorinsu, yana sa sadarwar dijital ta kasance mai sauƙi da sauƙin shiga.
