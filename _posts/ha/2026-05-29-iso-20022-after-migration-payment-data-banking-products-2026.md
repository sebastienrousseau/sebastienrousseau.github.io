# ISO 20022 Bayan Ƙaura: Juyar da Bayanan Biyan Kuɗi zuwa Kayayyakin Banki a 2026

ISO 20022 bayan ƙaura damar samfurin bayanai ce. Adireshi mai tsari, lambobin manufa, bayanan daftari, saƙonnin bincike, da abubuwan da suka faru na biyan kuɗi masu wadata za su iya zama kayayyakin daidaitawa, zamba, kuɗi, bin doka, da nazari. Alamar 2026 ita ce kayayyakin bayanan biyan kuɗi sun ƙaura daga gidan wasan kwaikwayo na sabuntawa zuwa cikin tsarin aiki na banki, inda tambayar yanke hukunci ita ce horon ƙira: wane bayanai, hanyoyin, kulawa, alhakai, da ayyukan abokin ciniki suka kasance tare ([Kyriba](https://www.kyriba.com/blog/iso-20022-corporate-treasury-2026/ "ISO 20022 migration")).

>
> - **Kayayyakin Bayanan Biyan Kuɗi yanzu dabaru ne.** An ɗaure batun da tsarin aiki, juriya, darajar abokin ciniki, da shaidar tsari maimakon ƙaddamar da samfuri mai ƙunci ([Kyriba](https://www.kyriba.com/blog/iso-20022-corporate-treasury-2026/ "ISO 20022 migration")).
> - **Ka'idar ƙira balagagge ta bayanai ce.** Bankuna suna buƙatar gine-gine wanda ke haɗa manufa, samfuri, bayanai, zaɓin hanya, kulawar haɗari, da tattalin arziƙi mai aunawa ([Montran](https://www.montran.com/resources/44-of-banks-will-miss-the-next-iso-20022-deadline-and-compliance-isnt-the-real-risk/ "44% of Banks Will Miss the Next ISO 20022 Deadline")).
> - **Tsarin kulawa dole ne ya zama na lokaci na ainihi.** Yanke shawarar zamba, kuɗi, bin doka, daidaitawa, da haɗarin aiki dole ne suyi gudu da saurin aikin, ba bayan abin ya faru ba.
> - **Ingancin bayanai ya zama fa'idar kasuwanci.** Bayanai masu tsari, mahallin ma'amala, ƙwafin duba, da alamomin shaida sun zama tushen sarrafa kai tsaye da samfuran abokin ciniki.
> - **Rarrabuwa ita ce abokin gaba.** Bankin da ya gina gwaje-gwajen ware game da kowane hanya, alama, samfuri, ko buƙatar bin doka yana ƙirƙirar haɗarin aiki na nan gaba.
> - **Tsarin nasara shine tsari.** Cibiyar da za ta iya jagoranta, sarrafa, ƙididdige farashi, baiwa shaida, da bayyana kowane aiki za ta yi fice fiye da wadda ta dauki wani kayan aiki kawai ([J.P. ## Me ya sa 2026 ita ce Shekarar da Wannan ya zama Dabarun

Masana'antar ta wuce matakin karɓa. Ba ya isa kawai a shiga hanya, a yi ƙaurar saƙo, a gudanar da shaidar ra'ayi na AI, ko a sanar da gwajin tokenisation. A 2026, gefen dabaru yana fitowa daga tsara wadannan iyawar kan ainihin aikin, sannan a tabbatar cewa aikin ya fi aminci, sauri, arha, juriya, ko amfani ga abokan ciniki.

Wannan shine dalilin da ya sa kayayyakin bayanan biyan kuɗi yanzu batu ne na hukumar gudanarwa. Matsi guda yana ci gaba: bayanan biyan kuɗi masu wadata, daidaitawa na lokaci na ainihi, kuɗin tokenised, yanke shawara na AI, Buɗewar Banki, juriyar aiki, taro a cloud, da shaidar tsari mafi ƙarfi. Idan an mu'amalantasu daban, waɗannan matsalolin suna haifar da yaɗuwar shirye-shirye. Idan an mu'amalantasu a matsayin gine-gine guda, suna haifar da ƙarfin aiki ([Kyriba](https://www.kyriba.com/blog/iso-20022-corporate-treasury-2026/ "ISO 20022 migration"), [Montran](https://www.montran.com/resources/44-of-banks-will-miss-the-next-iso-20022-deadline-and-compliance-isnt-the-real-risk/ "44% of Banks Will Miss the Next ISO 20022 Deadline")).

## Tushen Gine-ginen 2026

### 1. Aiki Tukunna, Fasaha Daga Baya

Banki ya kamata ya fara daga matsala: kuɗin da aka makale, jinkirin daidaitawa, farashin daidaitawa, biyan kuɗin da ya faɗi, fallasa zamba, ƙarancin duba, ko mummunan kwarewar abokin ciniki. An tabbatar da fasaha ne kawai inda ta kawar da wannan matsala ([Kyriba](https://www.kyriba.com/blog/iso-20022-corporate-treasury-2026/ "ISO 20022 migration")).

### 2. Bayanai a matsayin Tsarin Kulawa

Bayanai masu tsari, da aka kula da su, da za a iya bibiyarsu sune tushen. Ba tare da bayanai masu amfani ba, sarrafa kai tsaye yana zama mai rauni kuma bin doka yana zama na hannu. Tare da bayanai masu amfani, bankuna za su iya ƙirƙirar hankalin jagora, kulawa na lokaci na ainihi, da nazari ga abokin ciniki ([Montran](https://www.montran.com/resources/44-of-banks-will-miss-the-next-iso-20022-deadline-and-compliance-isnt-the-real-risk/ "44% of Banks Will Miss the Next ISO 20022 Deadline")).

### 3. Tsari A Tsakanin Hanyoyi da Dandamali

Gine-ginen dole ne ya goyi bayan hanyoyi da yawa, masu samarwa, tsare-tsaren shaida, alamomin haɗari, da kayan daidaitawa. Ya kamata yanke shawara na jagora ya yi ta hanyar farashi, sauri, ƙarewa, ikon, fifikon abokin ciniki, juriya, da wadatar bayanai.

### 4. Bin Doka da Shaida da aka Sanya

Tsarin bin doka dole ne ya zama na asali ga aikin. Manufar a matsayin lambar, ƙwafin duba kai tsaye, shaidar juriyar aiki, bayanan yarjejeniya, da gudanarwar abin koyi suna buƙatar samuwa a matsayin ɓangare na zartarwa, ba a sake gina su ga masu binciken daga baya ba.

### 5. Tattalin Arziƙin Unit da Darajar Abokin Ciniki

Kowane shiri yana buƙatar shaidar darajar kasuwanci. Farashi-kowane-biyan kuɗi, farashi-kowane-yanke shawara, farashi-kowane-bincike, kuɗin da aka adana, gyaran hannu da aka guji, asarar zamba da aka rage, da karɓar abokin ciniki ya kamata su tantance yanke shawarar girma.

## Me Wannan ke Nufi ta Nau'in Banki

### Bankunan Duniya

Bankunan duniya ya kamata su ƙirƙiri tsari a matakin dandalin domin kowace kasuwa, hanya, alama, da iyawar AI ba su zama tsarin aiki daban ba.

### Bankunan Yanki

Bankunan yanki ya kamata su mai da hankali kan al'amura inda amana, ilimin kasuwa na gida, da haɗawa mai sauƙi suna doke girma: gani na baitulmali, hana zamba, biyan kuɗin Buɗewar Banki, da sabis na kuɗi na dijital da aka tsara.

### Fintechs da PSPs

Fintechs ya kamata su rage rikitarwa ga bankuna maimakon ƙara wata hanya da aka ware. Mafi kyawun shawarwari za su kawo tsari, shaidar bin doka, ko hankalin bayanai.

### Masu Baitulmali na Kamfanoni

Masu baitulmali ya kamata su buƙaci ingantawa masu aunawa: rage gyaran biyan kuɗi, ingantaccen gani na kuɗi, bayanan daidaitawa masu wadata, daidaitawa mafi sauri, da iko mai ƙarfi a kan yanke shawara da aka sarrafa.

## Ƙarshe

ISO 20022 Bayan Ƙaura a ƙarshe tambayar gine-gine ce. Cibiyoyi da za su yi nasara ba za su zama waɗanda ke da mafi yawan gwaje-gwaje ko ƙawancen harshen sabuntawa mafi ƙarfi ba. Za su zama cibiyoyi da ke haɗa ayyukan abokin ciniki, ingancin bayanai, tsarin hanya, bin doka da aka sanya, da tattalin arziƙin naúrar zuwa cikin tsarin aiki mai daidaitawa.

## Tambayoyin da ake yawan yi

**Me ya sa wannan batu ya kasance gaggawa a 2026?**

Domin abubuwan more rayuwa, ka'idodi, da alamomin buƙatar abokin ciniki sun haɗa. Abin da ya kasance gwaji na zaɓi yanzu yana zama ɓangare na tsarin aiki na banki.

**Menene babbar haɗari na aiwatarwa?**

Babban haɗari shine rarrabuwa: ƙungiyoyi daban suna gina gwaje-gwaje daban, kowane da bayanai, kulawa, gudanarwa, da tattalin arziƙi daban.

**Me banki ya kamata ya gina farko?**

Banki ya kamata ya fara da aikin inda akwai darajar aunawa, kamar daidaitawa mafi sauri, ƙananan farashin daidaitawa, ƙarancin bincike, ingantaccen hana zamba, ko ingantaccen gani na kuɗi.

**Yaya za a auna nasara?**

Ya kamata a auna nasara ta hanyar tattalin arziƙin naúrar, shaidar juriya, ingancin bayanai, karɓar abokin ciniki, ragewar haɗarin aiki, da ingantaccen kuɗi ko jari na aiki.
