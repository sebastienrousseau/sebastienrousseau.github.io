## Me ya sa hybrid signature tun farko

Takardar ita ce RFC 10073, *Composite Module-Lattice-Based Digital Signature Algorithm (ML-DSA) for Use in X.509 Public Key Infrastructure*, wadda IETF ta wallafa a watan Nuwamba 2026, kuma M. Ounsworth, J. Gray, M. Pala, J. Klaußner da S. Fluhrer suka rubuta.

RFC ɗin ya buɗe da ginshiƙin da ya faɗa ba tare da sharaɗi ba: traditional signature algorithms kamar RSA, DSA da nau'ikansu na elliptic curve za su zama masu rauni ga hare-haren quantum. Ya tsara yanayin a matsayin batun lokaci kaɗai. Traditional algorithms, a cewarsa, za a karya su a nan gaba amma za su ci gaba da ƙarfi a halin yanzu, kuma rashin tabbas ɗaya kawai shi ne yaushe.

Wannan tsari yana da muhimmanci domin yana kawar da karatun kasala na matsalar. Idan karyewa tabbatacciya ce kuma kwanan wata kaɗai ba a sani ba, to jira ba zaɓi ne na tsaka-tsaki ba. Amma takardar ta fito fili daidai kan kuɗin motsawa da wuri. Jadawalin ƙaura mai sauri na iya buƙatar tura post-quantum cryptography (PQC) algorithms kafin a gama ƙarfafa ko tabbatar da implementations ɗinsu. RFC ɗin ya yi taka-tsantsan a nan, haka ma ya kamata mu yi: ya ce "na iya", ba "zai" ba.

Ajiye waɗannan matsi biyu gefe da gefe, kuma hujjar hybrid za ta bayyana. Kuna son kariya daga makomar quantum, amma sababbin algorithms da implementations ɗinsu ƙanana ne. RFC ɗin ya ce kariyar bayanai da algorithms biyu (dual-algorithm) na iya zama abin so na tsawon lokaci, don kariya daga raunin tsaro da sauran kurakuran aiwatarwa a sababbin implementations. Har ila yau harshen na sharaɗi ne. Takardar tana ba da dalili; ba ta iƙirarin ta tabbatar cewa hybrid koyaushe shi ne zaɓi daidai ba.

Ya dace a fayyace abin da composite ke saya muku. Composite algorithms suna riƙe wani tsaro ko da ɗaya daga cikin component algorithms ɗinsu ya karye. Kalmar ita ce "wani tsaro", ba cikakken tsaro ba, kuma RFC ɗin bai wuce gona da iri ba. Karatun aiki shi ne cewa gazawar ɓangare ɗaya ba ta bar ku da komai ta atomatik ba.

## Takardar da marubutanta

Kafin a juya zuwa yadda take aiki, asalin takardar ya cancanci sakin layi, domin waɗanda suka kafa kamfani (founders) da injiniyoyi da ke auna wani ma'auni ya kamata su san irin ma'aunin da yake. RFC ɗin yana cikin rafin Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF) kuma rukuninsa Standards Track ne. An wallafa shi a watan Nuwamba 2026 kuma yana ɗauke da ISSN 2070-1721. Rubutun tsarin ya bayyana shi a matsayin takardar Internet Standards Track: samfurin IETF wanda ke wakiltar yarjejeniyar al'ummar IETF kuma IESG ta amince da wallafa shi.

Jerin marubutan ya ratsa ƙungiyoyi huɗu. An lissafa M. Ounsworth da J. Gray daga Entrust, M. Pala daga OpenCA Labs, J. Klaußner daga Bundesdruckerei GmbH da S. Fluhrer daga Cisco Systems. Ba zan karanta fiye da abin da takardar ta goyi baya a wannan tsari ba. Abin da jerin ya nuna shi ne cewa wannan ba aikin mai sayarwa ɗaya ba ne.

Takardar ta kuma gaya wa masu karatu yadda za su fassara harshen umarninta. Kalmomin mahimmanci kamar "MUST" da "SHOULD" ana fassara su kamar yadda aka bayyana a BCP 14 (RFC 2119 da RFC 8174) idan, kuma kawai idan, sun bayyana da manyan haruffa duka. Wannan al'ada ce, amma shi ne dalilin da ya sa "should" da ƙananan haruffa a wannan ƙayyadaddun bayani ba shi da wani ƙarfi na musamman. Ƙayyadaddun bayanin ya kuma yi daidai da kalmomin da aka ayyana a RFC 9794.

## Public key ɗaya, signature value ɗaya

Babban shawarar ƙira tana da sauƙin faɗi. Composite algorithm yana gabatar da public key da signature value guda ɗaya, don haka ana iya ɗaukar shi a matsayin algorithm guda mara rabuwa a matakin protocol.

Wannan zaɓi ne mafi jarumtaka fiye da yadda yake bayyana da farko, kuma ina ganin shi ne daidai. RFC ɗin ya bayyana bambancin kai tsaye: PQ/T hybrids da yawa suna buƙatar gyaran protocol don ya zama "hybrid aware", yayin da wannan ƙayyadaddun bayani ke gabatar da hybrid a matsayin algorithm mai zaman kansa don haka zai iya amfana da hanyoyin cryptographic agility da ake da su.

Yi tunanin abin da hakan ke nufi ga duk wanda dole ne ya isar da samfur. Hybrid wanda dole ne protocol ya fahimta, hybrid ne da ke taɓa kowane protocol da ke son amfani da shi. Hybrid wanda ke kama da kowane algorithm, shi ne wanda ke shiga duk wani tsarin tattaunawa ko gano algorithm da ake da shi. RFC ɗin ya ayyana "algorithm" a wannan mahallin a matsayin wanda gabaɗaya ke nufin kowane aiki da ke da registered Object Identifier (OID) don amfani a cikin ASN.1 AlgorithmIdentifier. Composite yana samun shaidarsa a wannan tsari, kuma tsarin da ke kewaye yana ɗaukar shi kamar kowane.

Akwai batun kalmomi mai alaƙa da ke fayyace abin da RFC ɗin ke iƙirari da wanda ba ya yi. Ya ayyana Protocol Backwards Compatibility a matsayin siffar da za a iya ƙara sabon fasali ga protocol ba tare da buƙatar wani canji ga ƙayyadaddun bayanin protocol ɗin ba, da ƙananan canje-canje kawai ga implementations ɗinsa. Hanyar atomic-algorithm tana nufin irin wannan ƙari mara wahala kai tsaye. RFC ɗin ya tsara wannan a matsayin manufar ƙira: protocols kamar PKCS #10, CMP, X.509, CMS da Trust Anchor Format ya kamata su iya ɗaukar composite algorithms kamar kowane algorithm, ba tare da matakin protocol ya buƙaci wani "hybrid awareness" ba.

## Abin da ke shiga cikin composite

Rabin post-quantum shi ne ML-DSA, Module-Lattice-Based Digital Signature Algorithm da aka ayyana a FIPS 204. Ƙayyadaddun bayanin yana amfani da shi a matsayin ɓangaren Post-Quantum na PQ/T hybrids ɗinsa. Rabin traditional ana zaɓar shi daga tsayayyen rukuni. Ana samar da instantiations na tsarin Composite ML-DSA bisa ML-DSA, RSASSA-PSS, RSASSA-PKCS1-v1_5, ECDSA, Ed25519 da Ed448.

Sashen kalmomi ya ƙayyade kowane ɓangaren traditional. RSA ya bayyana a matsayin Probabilistic Signature Scheme, RSASSA-PSS, da aka ayyana a RFC 8017. ECDSA shi ne Elliptic Curve Digital Signature Algorithm da aka ayyana a FIPS 186-5. EdDSA shi ne Edwards-curve Digital Signature Algorithm da aka ayyana a RFC 8410, da rukunin parameters biyu: Ed25519 da Ed448. Ana amfani da Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) kamar yadda aka ayyana a RFC 5280, kuma DER na nufin Distinguished Encoding Rules kamar yadda aka ayyana a X.690.

Takardar ta ba da ƙaramin misali na abin da ake ɗauka a matsayin composite cryptographic element. Misalanta asymmetric algorithms ne kamar "ML-DSA-65" ko "RSASSA-PSS". Kuma ana amfani da "signature" a faɗaɗɗen ma'ana: digital cryptographic signature, ba tare da zato kan wane algorithm ba.

Wannan labarin bai shiga cikin ginin composite signature na ciki ba, wanda RFC ɗin ya ƙayyade a sassansa na key generation, signing, verification da serialisation. Abin da ke da muhimmanci a nan shi ne cewa siffar da ake gani daga waje, key ɗaya da signature ɗaya, ita ce siffar da sauran ƙirar ta dogara da ita.

## Me ya sa haɗe-haɗe suke da yawa haka

Idan kun duba jerin component algorithms kuma kun yi mamakin dalilin da ya sa ƙayyadaddun bayani zai ayyana haɗe-haɗe da yawa haka, RFC ɗin ya amsa a fili. Hidimar use case na regulatory re-certification, fiye da kowace buƙatar ƙira, shi ne ke haifar da yawan haɗe-haɗen algorithm a wannan ƙayyadaddun bayani. Ya ƙara da cewa an tsara haɗe-haɗen ne don cika jagororin ƙa'ida a wasu yankuna.

Dalilin da ke bayan use case ɗin yana da taka-tsantsan amma a fili yake. A wasu yanayi, zai iya zama mafi sauƙi a sake tabbatar da samfurin da ake da shi ta hanyar ƙara Post-Quantum ta PQ/T hybrid ga mafita da ta riga ta bi ƙa'ida ba tare da canza ɓangaren traditional ba, fiye da maye gurbin ɓangaren traditional. A wasu kalmomi, mai sayarwa da ke da traditional algorithm da aka tabbatar zai iya fifita barin shi yadda yake ya maƙala rabin post-quantum a gefensa. Wannan fifiko yana aiki ne kawai idan ƙayyadaddun bayanin ya ba da haɗi ga duk traditional algorithm da samfurin ke amfani da shi. Shi ya sa faɗin.

Wannan matsayin ƙira ne da na ga yana gamsarwa, kuma gyara ne mai amfani ga ɗabi'ar cewa ma'auni ya kamata ya zama ƙarami. Gajeren jeri zai fi tsafta, amma zai tilasta wa wasu samfura su musanya ɓangaren da aka tabbatar, wanda shi ne ainihin kuɗin da haɗe-haɗen ke son kauce masa. Dogon jerin shi ne farashin haɗuwa da samfuran da ke ƙarƙashin ƙa'ida a inda suke.

RFC ɗin ya kuma yi hankali don hana wannan faɗin zama nauyi ga masu karatu. Ana sa ran ƙungiyoyi su zaɓi tura composite algorithm ɗaya, ko ƙananan adadi, kuma masu aiwatarwa kaɗan ne za su buƙaci damuwa da dukkan jerin. Lura da taka-tsantsan: "ana sa ran". Hasashe ne kan halin turawa, ba buƙata ba. Amma yana gaya muku yadda za ku karanta takardar. Ba a nufin ku aiwatar da komai ba; ana nufin ku nemo haɗin ku.

## Abin da ƙayyadaddun bayanin ya bari

Ma'auni nagari yana fayyace iyakokinsa, kuma wannan ya yi. Iyakoki huɗu sun fito fili.

Na farko, ƙayyadaddun bayanin bai ayyana kowane mandatory-to-implement algorithm ba. Idan aka haɗa da tsammanin cewa ƙungiyoyi za su zaɓi composite ɗaya ko kaɗan, wannan na nufin interoperability tsakanin ɓangarori biyu ya dogara ne kan sun zaɓi haɗe-haɗe masu jituwa. RFC ɗin bai ayyana tsoho da kowa dole ne ya tallafa ba, kuma ya yi gargaɗin cewa yawan haɗe-haɗe na iya raba tsarin zuwa ƙananan rukunoni marasa interoperability idan al'ummomi daban-daban suka zaɓi rukunoni marasa haɗuwa. Yana ƙarfafa ƙungiyoyi a takamaiman fannonin aikace-aikace su ayyana profiles da ke zaɓar ƙananan adadin composites. Zan nuna wannan a matsayin sakamako mafi amfani guda ɗaya ga ƙungiyar da ke tsara turawa: za ku buƙaci yarda kan haɗin ku da abokan hulɗarku maimakon ku ɗauka.

Na biyu, ba a samar da backwards compatibility a ma'anar tsarin da aka haɓaka su ci gaba da interoperability da tsofaffin tsarin ba. Ya dace a raba wannan da batun matakin protocol na sama. RFC ɗin ya ayyana Application Backwards Compatibility a matsayin siffar da ke nuna ko aikace-aikace da aka haɓaka da wanda ba a haɓaka ba za su iya kafa sadarwa cikin nasara. Section 10.3 ya fito fili: hanyoyin da ke cikin takardar ba sa samar da application backwards compatibility, tsarin da aka haɓaka kaɗai ne za su fahimci OIDs da ta ayyana, kuma za a buƙaci ƙarin hanyoyi inda ake buƙatar wannan siffar. Ƙirar atomic-algorithm tana taimakawa da canje-canjen protocol, amma ba ta iƙirarin warware ko aikace-aikace da aka haɓaka zai iya magana da wanda ba a haɓaka ba.

Na uku, akwai iyakar siffar tsaro. Composite ML-DSA ya dace inda aka yarda a sami signature primitive mai rauninta siffar EUF-CMA maimakon ƙaƙƙarfan siffar Strong Unforgeability under Chosen Message Attack (SUF-CMA), wadda Composite ML-DSA ba ya bayarwa. Idan aikace-aikacenku ya dogara da SUF-CMA, wannan tsari bai dace ba kamar yadda aka ƙayyade. Wannan iyaka ce mai tsafta da za a iya bincikawa, kuma founder da ke kimanta tsarin ya kamata ya gwada buƙatunsa da ita kafin ya ci gaba.

Na huɗu, kuma cikin tawali'u, takardar ta yarda cewa matsin jadawalin da kansa yana ɗauke da haɗari, kamar yadda aka tattauna a baya: ana iya tura PQC implementations kafin a gama ƙarfafa ko tabbatar da su. Composite hanyar rage wannan haɗari ce, ba kawar da shi ba.

## Karanta alamomin rubutu

Masu aiwatarwa za su ɓata lokaci da bayanan algorithm, don haka bayani kan al'adunsu yana da amfani. Bayanan algorithm suna amfani da tsarin rubutu irin na Python. Alamar || tana wakiltar haɗa byte arrays biyu, kuma alamar [:] tana wakiltar yanka byte array.

Ga ayyukan da ke dawo da ƙima da yawa, takardar tana amfani da (a, b) don wakiltar ƙima biyu, galibi tana nuna cewa aiki yana dawo da ƙima da yawa, inda ainihin hanyar isarwa (tuple, struct, output parameters, da sauransu) aka bar wa mai aiwatarwa. Wannan sashe na ƙarshe 'yanci ne da gangan: ƙayyadaddun bayanin yana kula da abin da aka dawo, ba yadda harshenku ke naɗa shi ba. Alamar (a, _) tana wakiltar haɗi inda aka yi watsi da ƙima ɗaya, ta biyu a wannan yanayin.

Signatures na ayyuka suna bin irin wannan tsari. Alamar func(a) -> b tana wakiltar aiki mai suna func wanda ke karɓar a a matsayin shigarwa kuma ya samar da b. Kuma Func<TYPE>() tana wakiltar aiki da aka daidaita ta <TYPE>, ma'ana implementation ɗinsa zai sami ƙananan bambance-bambance dangane da TYPE na ƙasa. Wannan al'ada ta ƙarshe ita ce ke barin bayani ɗaya ya rufe algorithms na ƙasa da yawa ba tare da maimaita shi ga kowannensu ba, wanda ya dace da ƙayyadaddun bayani da ya ratsa nau'ikan RSA, ECDSA da EdDSA tare da ML-DSA.

Babu ɗaya daga cikin waɗannan da ke da ƙyalli, amma shi ne ɓangaren takardar da za ku buɗe a zahiri yayin rubuta code, kuma shawarar barin hanyar dawo da ƙima da yawa ga mai aiwatarwa sassauci ne mai ma'ana ga bambancin harsunan da ake amfani da su.

## Inda wannan ya bar ƙungiyar da ke tsara ƙaura

Idan aka tattara waɗannan guntaye cikin tsarin yanke shawara, RFC ɗin yana ba ku abubuwa uku masu amfani kuma yana roƙon ku ku samar da biyu.

Abin da yake ba ku shi ne dalili, siffa da iyaka. Dalilin shi ne matsi biyun da aka riga aka bayyana: traditional algorithms za su faɗi ga hare-haren quantum a lokacin da ba a sani ba, yayin da sababbin implementations ba za su iya zama an gama ƙarfafa ko tabbatar da su ba tukuna. Siffar ita ce atomic composite, da public key ɗaya da signature value ɗaya, wadda ke riƙe wani tsaro idan ɓangare ya karye. Iyakar ita ce jerin iyakokin da ke sama: babu mandatory-to-implement algorithms, babu rufe backwards compatibility kai tsaye, kuma EUF-CMA maimakon SUF-CMA.

Abin da yake roƙon ku ku samar shi ne zaɓin haɗi da yarjejeniyar interoperability. Saboda ƙayyadaddun bayanin bai ambaci mandatory-to-implement algorithms ba, zaɓin haɗi alhakinku ne, ko da yake Section 10.4 ya ba da gajeren jerin shawarwari don use cases na gama-gari. Idan samfurinku ya riga ya samu tabbaci da wani takamaiman traditional algorithm, dabarar re-certification tana nuna haɗin da ke barin wannan ɓangaren yadda yake. Idan kuna gina sabon abu ba tare da buƙatun ƙa'ida ko legacy implementations da za a yi la'akari da su ba, RFC ɗin ya ba da shawarar mai da hankalin aiwatarwa kan id-MLDSA65-ECDSA-P256-SHA512, wanda ya ce yana ba da mafi kyawun daidaito gaba ɗaya na aiki da tsaro.

Akwai hujja mai ma'ana cewa wannan rabon aiki shi ne daidai ga ma'auni a wannan mataki. Wajabta haɗi ɗaya zai yi wa wasu turawa hidima ya bar wasu a baya, musamman waɗanda jagororin ƙa'ida na yanki suka ɗaure. Ƙin wajabtawa yana sa ƙayyadaddun bayanin ya zama mai amfani a waɗannan yankuna, a farashin tura matakin haɗin kai ga masu karɓa. RFC ɗin ya zaɓi sassauci kuma ya gaya muku haka.

Zan kuma ɗan yi turjiya kan wani kuskuren karatu mai jan hankali. Bayanin cewa masu aiwatarwa kaɗan ne za su buƙaci damuwa da dukkan jerin na iya yin kama da izinin watsi da faɗin ƙayyadaddun bayanin. Ba haka ba ne. Bayani ne kan wa ke ɗaukar kuɗin wannan faɗi: ƙayyadaddun bayanin ya sha shi don yawancin turawa kada su sha. Idan kai marubucin library ne ko mai sayarwa da ke yi wa abokan ciniki masu ƙa'ida hidima, faɗin na iya zama matsalarka; idan kai ƙungiya ɗaya ce da ke zaɓar composite ɗaya, galibi ba matsalarka ba ce. RFC ɗin ya yi taka-tsantsan kan tsammanin, kuma zan ɗauke shi a matsayin hasashe ba garanti ba.

## Tambayoyin da ƙayyadaddun bayanin ya bari a buɗe

Zaruruwa biyu a takardar sun cancanci a kalle su domin ƙayyadaddun bayanin da kansa ya nuna su ba tare da warware su ba.

Na farko shi ne tsawon sauyin. RFC ɗin ya ce kariyar bayanai da algorithms biyu na iya zama abin so na tsawon lokaci, kuma jadawalin ƙaura na iya zama mai sauri. Waɗannan bayanai biyu suna jan juna ta hanya mai ban sha'awa: motsawa da sauri zuwa PQC yana goyon bayan turawa kafin a gama ƙarfafawa, yayin da dogon lokacin kariya ke goyon bayan riƙe rabin traditional a wurin har bayan haka. Tsawon lokacin da ƙungiyoyi za su riƙe ɓangarorin biyu a zahiri tambaya ce da takardar ta tayar a kaikaice kuma ba ta amsa ba.

Na biyu shi ne backwards compatibility. RFC ɗin ya faɗi cewa hanyoyinsa ba sa samar da application backwards compatibility kai tsaye kuma za a buƙaci ƙarin hanyoyi inda ake buƙatar ta. Ya lissafa protocols da ake buƙatar tunani kan ƙaura da interoperability a cikinsu, daga TLS 1.3 da IKEv2 zuwa S/MIME, eIDAS document signing da code signing, amma ya bar ƙirar waɗannan ƙarin hanyoyi ga protocols da aikace-aikacen da kansu.

Ga ƙungiyoyin da ke yanke shawarar inda za su fara kashe ƙoƙari, wannan tambaya ta biyu ita ce ta fi dacewa a fara da ita: ba wane haɗi za a karɓa ba, sai yadda aikace-aikace da aka haɓaka zai yi idan ɓangaren da ke ɗaya gefen bai motsa ba tukuna.

## Tambayoyin da Ake Yawan Yi

**Menene Composite ML-DSA?**
Composite ML-DSA tsarin PQ/T hybrid signature ne da aka ayyana a RFC 10073. Yana haɗa ML-DSA, da aka ayyana a FIPS 204, da traditional algorithm kamar RSASSA-PSS, RSASSA-PKCS1-v1_5, ECDSA, Ed25519 ko Ed448. Haɗin yana gabatar da public key da signature value guda ɗaya, don haka protocols za su iya ɗaukar shi a matsayin algorithm guda mara rabuwa.
**Me ya sa RFC ɗin ya ayyana haɗe-haɗen algorithm masu yawa haka?**
RFC ɗin ya ce hidimar use case na regulatory re-certification, fiye da kowace buƙatar ƙira, shi ne ke haifar da yawan haɗe-haɗe. Mai sayarwa zai iya ganin ya fi sauƙi a sake tabbatar da samfur ta hanyar ƙara ɓangaren post-quantum a gefen traditional ɗin da ya riga ya bi ƙa'ida fiye da maye gurbinsa. Ana sa ran ƙungiyoyi su tura composite algorithm ɗaya ko ƙananan adadi.
**Wane composite algorithm ya kamata sabuwar turawa ta fara da shi?**
RFC ɗin bai ambaci mandatory-to-implement algorithm ba. Ga aikace-aikacen da ba su da buƙatun ƙa'ida ko legacy implementations da za a yi la'akari da su, Section 10.4 ya ba da shawarar mai da hankalin aiwatarwa kan id-MLDSA65-ECDSA-P256-SHA512. Ya kuma ba da shawarwari ga aikace-aikacen da ke buƙatar RSA, masu damuwa da aiki da bandwidth, ko waɗanda ke yarda da NIST PQC Level 5 kaɗai.
**Shin Composite ML-DSA yana sa tsarin da aka haɓaka su ci gaba da aiki da tsofaffi?**
A'a. RFC ɗin ya faɗi cewa hanyoyinsa ba sa samar da application backwards compatibility kai tsaye kuma tsarin da aka haɓaka kaɗai ne za su fahimci OIDs da ya ayyana. Inda ake buƙatar wannan siffar, za a buƙaci ƙarin hanyoyi.
**Wace siffar tsaro Composite ML-DSA ba ya bayarwa?**
Ba ya bayar da Strong Unforgeability under Chosen Message Attack (SUF-CMA), sai siffar EUF-CMA mai rauni kawai. RFC ɗin ya ce aikace-aikacen da tsaron SUF-CMA ke da muhimmanci a gare su kada su yi amfani da shi, kuma NOT RECOMMENDED ne inda ba a nuna cewa EUF-CMA abin karɓa ne ba.
