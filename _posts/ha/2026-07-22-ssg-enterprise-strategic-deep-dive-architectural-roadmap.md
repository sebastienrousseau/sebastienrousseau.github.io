# Static Site Generator (SSG): Zurfin Nazarin Dabaru na Matakin Kamfani da Tsarin Gine-gine

*Ranar bincike: 2026-06-22. Bisa dubawar codebase na `static-site-generator` a v0.0.41 da binciken yanar gizo na yanayin SSG na 2026.*

**Ga mai bugawa da ke ƙarƙashin ka'ida, static site generator ba kayan aikin ƙira ba ne kuma; wani ɓangare ne na iyakar haɗarin aiki.** Rust [static-site-generator](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/static-site-generator) mai buɗaɗɗen tushe an gina shi bisa wannan tunani, yana matsar da tsaro, samun dama, harhaɗa harsuna da AI content pipelines zuwa lokacin compile domin dubawa da ta gaza ta dakatar da build maimakon ta kai ga production. Wannan nazari ya raba abin da sigar 0.0.41 ke bayarwa da gaske daga abin da takardun ta har yanzu ke yi alkawari kawai, ya gabatar da iyawa biyar na kamfani da ba ta da su tukuna, kuma ya ba da shawarar hanya mai matakai zuwa fitowar 1.0 da ta dace da DORA, European Accessibility Act da ƙa'idodin supply-chain na zamani.

> **Taƙaitawar Zartarwa**
>
> - **Bugawa yanzu iyakar haɗarin aiki ce.** A ƙarƙashin DORA, European Accessibility Act da GDPR, kowace kadara da ke fuskantar jama'a wuri ne mai yiwuwar shiga na lalata supply-chain, ɓata fuska da fallasa ga masu kula da ka'ida. Tsarin lokacin compile yana rage wannan iyaka ta hanyar ƙin fitowar da ba ta bin ka'ida kafin a tura ta.
> - **Abubuwan da suka bambanta injin an tilasta su ta compiler, ba burin da aka rubuta ba.** `forbid(unsafe_code)` a fadin workspace, SHA-256/384 SRI na gaske, ciran CSP ta atomatik, da WCAG 2.2 AA gate na lokacin build suna mayar da tsaro da samun dama daga bincike na baya-bayan zuwa gazawar build mai tsanani.
> - **Sigar 0.0.41 tana da gibi tsakanin takardu da code.** An kwatanta native minification, incremental rebuilds ta hanyar dependency graph, da tallafin AVIF amma ba sa aiki; labarin ya ambaci kowane gibi tare da ainihin wurin tushe.
> - **Hanya zuwa 1.0 jerin abubuwa ne, ba jerin buri ba.** Ƙarfi da farko (0.0.42), sannan daidaiton incremental (0.1.0), sannan iyawa na kamfani, WASM sandboxing, bincike na ma'ana na gida da SLSA provenance mai tabbaci, wanda mai siye da ke ƙarƙashin ka'ida ke buƙata (1.0.0).

## Ƙarfin da Ake da shi Yanzu

Codebase na `static-site-generator` yana nuna shawarwarin injiniya masu bambanta da dama da suka raba shi da tsofaffin injin JavaScript da Go:

- **Matsayin tsaro na lokacin compile:** `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` a fadin workspace yana ba da tabbacin tsaron ƙwaƙwalwa a lokacin compile. Build pipeline yana samar da hashes na Subresource Integrity (SRI) na SHA-256/SHA-384 na gaske (`src/plugins/assets.rs`) kuma yana yin ciran Content Security Policy (CSP) ta atomatik da ke cire unsafe-inline scripts da styles. Ana sa hannu kan releases, suna ɗauke da Sigstore attestation, kuma suna samar da CycloneDX 1.5 SBOM a kowane build.  
- **Ƙofar samun dama da compiler ke tilastawa:** Ana gudanar da dubawar Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA a cikin compilation pipeline ta hanyar axe-core parser na lokacin build wanda Playwright ke kora. Samun dama ya zama build gate mai tsanani maimakon bincike na bayan bugawa: idan shafi ya gaza, compilation ya dakata tare da kurakuran lambar layi daidai.  
- **AI pipeline mai mallakar bayanai:** Local-LLM translation da metadata-extraction pipeline (ta hanyar local Ollama ko llama.cpp endpoints) yana ba wa cibiya damar sarrafa taƙaita abun ciki, samar da JSON-LD schema da fassara harsuna daban-daban ta atomatik ba tare da aika bayyanawar kudin shiga na gaba ko mallakar ilimi mai mahimmanci zuwa public cloud AI APIs ba.  
- **Compilation mai daidaici:** Tabbacin tsaron ƙwaƙwalwa na Rust ya goyi bayan HTML da asset pipeline mai daidaici da Rayon ke kora (`src/core/pipeline.rs`). Plugin pipeline yana aiwatar da fused transforms, tare da `SearchPlugin`, `SeoPlugin`, `CanonicalPlugin` da `JsonLdPlugin` da ke aiki bisa `par_iter()`, don haka ana karanta kowane shafi kuma a rubuta shi zuwa disk sau ɗaya.  
- **Tsabtar supply-chain da dependency:** Ƙaura da template engine daga Tera zuwa MiniJinja (`v0.0.37`) ya rage girman binary, ya cire transitive dependencies kamar `rand` a lokacin compile, kuma ya samar da ƙaramin sawun dependency da ke rage fallasa ga software supply-chain.

## Gibi da Gaskiyar Duniya ta Zahiri

Duk da waɗannan ƙarfi na musamman, dubawar codebase mai tsauri na v0.0.41 ta bayyana gibi da dama na gine-gine, na aiki, da na kwarewar mai haɓakawa tsakanin da'awar takardun ta da ainihin rust code:

### Gibin Gine-gine

- **Tattara Farin Fili vs. Native Minification:** Yayin da README ke yi alkawarin "native JS/CSS minification," `MinifyPlugin` (`src/plugins/plugins.rs:96-116`) yana aiki ne kawai a matsayin mai tattara farin fili mara wayo. Yana gajarta kan `<pre>` elements kuma yana tattara jerin farin fili a cikin HTML, amma ba ya yin native CSS ko JS minification da ke fahimtar tsari. Bugu da ƙari, yana sarrafa shafuka na sama kawai kuma ba ya bin subdirectories cikin zurfi (kamar `/blog/` ko `/tags/`), yana barin shafuka masu zurfi ba tare da an minify su ba.  
- **Matattun Ababen More Rayuwa na Incremental:** An compile dependency tracking graph (`DepGraph` a cikin `src/core/depgraph.rs`) kuma an loda shi cikin `PluginContext.dep_graph` amma ba a taɓa cika shi da gaske a production code ba. Ana kiran hanyar `add_dep()` a cikin unit tests kawai, wanda ke sa da'awar README na "incremental rebuilds via dependency graphs" ta zama buri kawai a yanzu.  
- **Compilation na Rukuni vs. Streaming Compilation:** Module na `streaming::compile_batch` (`src/core/streaming.rs`) ba ya yin stream da gaske. Maimakon haka, yana compile shafuka a rukuni zuwa wani temporary directory, yana aiwatar da `staticdatagen::compile` daga farko ga kowane rukuni, kuma yana haɗa fitattun. Wannan yana haifar da babban nauyin disk I/O da parsing mai maimaituwa, yana kauce daga ainihin gine-ginen streaming.  
- **Keta Matakan Rayuwar Plugin:** Plugins da ke samar da sabbin shafukan HTML yayin build process, kamar `TaxonomyPlugin`, `PaginationPlugin` da `I18nPlugin`, suna rubutawa kai tsaye zuwa disk a cikin `after_compile` maimakon amfani da lifecycle na `transform_html`. Sakamakon haka, shafukan da waɗannan plugins ke samarwa suna ƙetare muhimman post-processing plugins (kamar `CanonicalPlugin`, `JsonLdPlugin`, `RobotsPlugin`, da `AccessibilityPlugin`) idan an yi rijistar waɗannan plugins da wuri. Wannan yana barin shafukan tag, category, da paginated ba tare da ingantattun canonical links, JSON-LD schemas, ko accessibility validations ba.  
- **Fita Zuwa `curl` a cikin `LlmPlugin`:** Local LLM content pipeline (`src/plugins/llm.rs`) yana fita kai tsaye zuwa `curl` binary na host domin tambayar local endpoints. Wannan yana haifar da manyan bugs na cross-platform (misali, a kan Windows hosts ba tare da curl a cikin PATH ba), yana haifar da haɗarin tsaro (shell injection vectors), kuma yana gaza a cikin CI environments da aka rufe ko aka ware daga hanyar sadarwa.  
- **Sarrafa String Mara Wayo a cikin Sake Rubuta HTML:** Extractors na `image_plugin.rs` da `search.rs` suna sake rubuta HTML strings ta amfani da ayyukan `str::find` da `str::rfind` marasa ƙarfi. Wannan hanya tana da rauni sosai ga karyayyun HTML tags, `<img>` tags a cikin comments, character entities a cikin alt text, ko `srcset` properties da suka riga suka kasance, wanda zai iya haifar da fitowar da ta lalace.  
- **Tallafin AVIF da ba a Aiwatar ba:** Ko da yake an rubuta AVIF image encoding sosai, aiwatarwa a cikin `image_plugin.rs` stub ne inda `avif_variants` kawai ke mayar da `Vec::new()`, yana barin fasalin ba ya aiki.  
- **Watcher da ke Bisa Polling:** Watcher na local development server (`src/server/watch.rs`) yana amfani da polling maimakon filesystem event APIs, wanda ke haifar da yawan amfani da CPU a lokacin zaman banza da latency na gyare-gyare ƙasa da daƙiƙa ɗaya.

### Gibin Aiki da DX

- **Babu Bin Diddigin Transitive Dependency:** Dependency graph ba zai iya bin diddigin nested dependencies ba (misali, canje-canje ga wani sub-template da ke shafar layout da ke shafar shafi), kamar yadda unit test `transitive_not_tracked` ya tabbatar.  
- **Babu CLI Flag na Incremental Compilation:** Babu `--incremental` CLI flag da aka haɗa da execution compiler, wanda ke hana masu haɓakawa amfani da cached builds.  
- **HMR ya Iyakance ga CSS:** Hot Module Replacement (HMR) yana goyon bayan CSS kawai; kowane gyara ga HTML, layouts, ko markdown files yana haifar da cikakken sake loda shafi, yana rage saurin mai haɓakawa.  
- **Rashin Subcommand:** Dole ne masu haɓakawa su ba da flags masu tsawo da hannu (`ssg -s public -w`) saboda daidaitattun subcommands kamar `ssg dev`, `ssg build`, `ssg check`, da `ssg lint` ba su wanzu.

## Gibin Gine-gine da Muke Rasa (Sabbin Ganowa)

Bayan gibin da ke v0.0.41, tantance aikin bisa bayanan haɗari na matakin kuɗi ya bayyana iyawa da dama da ba ta bayarwa tukuna amma mai siye na kamfani zai buƙata:

### 1. WebAssembly Plugin Sandboxing (Ƙarin Zero-Trust)

Yayin da compiler binary kanta an rubuta ta a cikin safe Rust, barin plugins na wasu ɓangarori na uku su yi aiki kai tsaye a kan host systems yana haifar da rauni mai tsanani na supply-chain. Wani plugin na ɓangare na uku da aka lalata zai iya samun damar shiga filesystem na host cikin sauƙi, karanta Markdown files masu mallaka, ko fitar da credentials na sirri.

* **Iyawar da ta Ɓace:** Wani sandbox execution environment. Domin cimma zero-trust compilation, compiler ya kamata ya aiwatar da plugins na ɓangare na uku a cikin WebAssembly runtime da aka haɗa (kamar `wasmtime`). Plugins ya kamata su yi mu'amala da host ta hanyar WebAssembly System Interface (WASI) da aka takaita kawai, suna iyakance damar su kawai ga shafin da ake canzawa.

### 2. Zero-Copy HTML Parsing ta hanyar Streaming AST (`lol_html`)

Ƙaura da HTML parsing layer zuwa cikakken in-memory DOM library (kamar Kuchiki ko html5ever) yana haifar da babban nauyin ƙwaƙwalwa da tsayawar sarrafawa yayin da ake sarrafa shafukan yanar gizo masu shafuka sama da 100,000.

* **Iyawar da ta Ɓace:** Wani streaming, zero-copy HTML rewriter. Amfani da `lol_html` na Cloudflare (Low-Output-Latency HTML rewriter) yana ba compiler damar parse, dubawa, da gyara HTML elements a cikin wucewa ɗaya na streaming tare da kusan sifili allocation na ƙwaƙwalwa, yana daidaita da manufar parallel streaming compiler na builds ƙasa da daƙiƙa ɗaya.

### 3. Bincike na Vector na Ma'ana na Gida (Local RAG)

search index na yanzu (`SearchPlugin`) yana samar da JSON index mai nauyi, mai lebur da ke yin daidaituwar string na client-side masu sauƙi, ba tare da tallafin fuzzy search, stemming, ko semantic queries ba. Pagefind ci gaba ne, amma har yanzu yana dogara ga sauke babban index.

* **Iyawar da ta Ɓace:** Embedded semantic search. Compiler ya kamata ya yi amfani da local, mai sauƙi Rust-native vector embedding model (kamar MiniLM-L6 model da aka aiwatar ta `candle` ko `ort` / ONNX Runtime) a lokacin build. Ya kamata ya samar da dense vector embeddings ga kowane sakin layi na shafi kuma ya fitar da ƙaramin vector index. Client-side search widget, da aka compile zuwa WASM, sannan zai iya yin ainihin offline semantic search kai tsaye a cikin browser.

### 4. Deterministic Translation da Inference Caching

Saboda local LLM inference (misali, ta Ollama ko Llama.cpp) yana amfani da CPU/GPU sosai, fassara ko samar da metadata ga dubban shafuka a kowane build yana da tsada ta fuskar ƙididdiga.

* **Iyawar da ta Ɓace:** Inference caching da ke bisa content-hash. Compiler dole ne ya kula da deterministic cache na dukkan ayyukan LLM. Idan SHA-256 hash na abun ciki na markdown file da parameters na fassararsa ya dace da wani cache entry, compiler ya kamata ya sake amfani da cached translation da metadata, yana ƙetare local inference mai maimaituwa.

### 5. Asynchronous File I/O don Faɗaɗa Daidaici

Yayin da plugin pipeline yake daidaici ta Rayon, daidaitattun synchronous disk writes suna toshe OS threads na Rayon, suna haifar da I/O bottleneck yayin rubuta dubun-dubatar shafuka.

* **Iyawar da ta Ɓace:** Asynchronous, non-blocking disk I/O. Compiler ya kamata ya raba ayyukan da ke amfani da CPU sosai (Markdown parsing, minification) daga disk-bound writes, ta amfani da asynchronous I/O thread pools ko Linux `io_uring` bindings (ta `rio` ko `tokio`) domin rubuta shafukan da aka compile a daidaici ba tare da toshe parallel CPU executors ba.

## Tsarin Aiki na Dabaru na 1.0

Tsarin aiki mai zuwa yana haɗa gibin da aka warware da sabbin iyawa na matakin kamfani da aka gano cikin tsari mai tsari, na jerin fitowa bisa lokaci.

### Mataki na 1: 0.0.42 (Patch na Ƙarfi da Daidaito, mako 1 zuwa 2)

1. **Sake Gina `MinifyPlugin`:** Haɗa `minify-html`, `oxc_minifier`, da `lightningcss` don native, HTML, JS, da CSS minification da ke fahimtar tsari. Tabbatar cewa plugin yana bin dukkan nested directories a ƙarƙashin `site_dir` cikin zurfi.  
2. **Tabbatar da Tsaron AI Pipeline:** Ƙaura da `LlmPlugin` daga native `curl` shellouts zuwa `ureq` (wani mai sauƙi, synchronous, safe Rust HTTP client) domin tabbatar da daidaituwa a duk dandamali da kawar da raunin shell injection.  
3. **Kammala Aiwatar da AVIF:** Haɗa `ravif` kai tsaye cikin image asset pipeline, don ba da damar AVIF encoding mai inganci tare da WebP da PNG.  
4. **Sarrafa HrefLang da Multi-Locale Mapping ta Atomatik:** Gano shafukan da aka fassara masu daidaici a cikin multilingual builds ta atomatik kuma a saka daidaitattun `<link rel="alternate" hreflang="..." />` tags da suka bi ka'idar Google cikin head na kowane HTML file da aka compile.  
5. **Tallafin JSON Feed 1.1:** Fitar da wani JSON Feed 1.1 emitter na musamman tare da daidaitattun RSS 2.0 da Atom 1.0 syndication channels.

### Mataki na 2: 0.1.0 (Ƙaramar Sabuntawa ta Sahihanci da Incremental, wata 2 zuwa 3)

1. **Cika `DepGraph` da Kunna `--incremental`:** Haɗa `DepGraph` sosai domin bin diddigin template-to-page da markdown-to-page dependencies. Aiwatar da cache invalidation layer kuma a haɗa `--incremental` CLI flag, tare da nufin rebuilds ƙasa da 200ms ga warm-cache environments.  
2. **Sake Rubuta Streaming AST ta `lol_html`:** Maye gurbin sake rubuta string mara ƙarfi a cikin `image_plugin.rs`, `search.rs`, da CSP injections da streaming, zero-copy HTML rewriter da `lol_html` ke ƙarfafawa.  
3. **Watcher da ke Bisa Event da Component HMR:** Ƙaura da watch module daga polling zuwa event-driven `notify` crate, kuma a aiwatar da CSS-only da partial-HTML hot reloading domin sabuntawar browser ƙasa da 100ms.  
4. **CLI na Umarni Guda:** Sake gina compiler interface domin goyon bayan daidaitattun subcommands: `ssg dev`, `ssg build`, `ssg check` (bincike na accessibility/SEO), da `ssg deploy`.  
5. **Deterministic Inference Cache:** Aiwatar da content-hash caching layer ga dukkan ayyukan local LLM translation, taƙaitawa, da metadata extraction.

### Mataki na 3: 1.0.0 (Babban Fitowa na Kamfani da Production, wata 6 zuwa 12)

1. **Zero-Trust WASM Plugin Sandboxing:** Haɗa WebAssembly runtime (`wasmtime` ko `wasmer`) domin aiwatar da plugins na ɓangare na uku a cikin cikakken sandbox environment tare da damar filesystem da network da ke bisa iyawa.

## Haɗa Ka'ida da Bin Doka

A cikin manyan sassa na kamfani da kuɗi, ana tantance software ta ruwan tabarau na bin doka da jarin haɗari. Tsarin gine-gine na `static-site-generator` ya dace kai tsaye da manyan umarnin ka'ida:

- **DORA Article 6 (Sarrafa Haɗarin ICT):** Ƙididdiga da injection na SHA-384 SRI hashes da tsauraran Content Security Policies a lokacin compile suna cika buƙatar kare tashoshin bugawa na dijital daga supply-chain injection, ɓata yanar gizo, da cross-site scripting (XSS) vectors.  
- **DORA Article 7 (Juriyar Tsarin ICT):** Ta hanyar matsawa zuwa static assets marasa canzawa da aka tabbatar a lokacin compile, cibiyoyin kuɗi suna kawar da raunin database da runtime server, suna rage yawaitar haɗarin aiki da rage ajiyar jarin haɗari da ake buƙata a ƙarƙashin Basel III.  
- **European Accessibility Act (EAA) Directive (EU) 2019/882:** Matsar da binciken accessibility zuwa cikin compilation pipeline a matsayin compiler gate mai tsanani yana tabbatar da bin doka 100% kafin deployment, yana kawar da haɗarin lalata brand da ƙarar farar hula a ƙarƙashin EAA da ADA Title III.  
- **GDPR Article 25 (Privacy-by-Design):** Gudanar da translation da metadata pipeline a kan local hardware da aka ware daga hanyar sadarwa yana kiyaye daftarin mallaka, ma'aunin kuɗi da bayanan sirri daga public third-party cloud LLM providers, yana tallafawa bin ka'idodin mallakar bayanai.

## Tambayoyin da Ake Yawan Yi

**Menene sigar 0.0.41 ke bayarwa da gaske a yau, sabanin abin da README ke da'awa?**
Tsarin tsaro da samun dama na gaske ne kuma an tilasta shi a cikin code: `forbid(unsafe_code)` a fadin workspace, samar da SHA-256/384 SRI, ciran CSP, releases masu sa hannu tare da Sigstore attestation da CycloneDX SBOM, da WCAG 2.2 AA gate mai dakatar da build. Fasaloli uku da aka rubuta ba sa aiki a v0.0.41. `MinifyPlugin` mai tattara farin fili ne maimakon minifier da ke fahimtar syntax; `DepGraph` da zai kori incremental rebuilds an compile shi amma ba a taɓa cika shi a production code ba; kuma AVIF encoding stub ne wanda `avif_variants` ke mayar da vector maras komai.

**Shin accessibility gate na gaske compiler gate ne ko post-build linter?**
build gate ne. Ana gudanar da dubawar WCAG 2.2 AA a cikin compilation pipeline ta hanyar axe-core parser na lokacin build wanda Playwright ke kora, kuma shafi da ya gaza yana dakatar da compilation tare da kurakuran lambar layi daidai maimakon fitar da gargaɗi bayan an gama. Wannan shi ne halin da wajibcin European Accessibility Act ke buƙata: fitowar da ba ta bin ka'ida ba za ta iya kai ga deployment ba.

**Me ya sa fita zuwa `curl` a cikin LLM plugin ke da muhimmanci?**
Local LLM pipeline (`src/plugins/llm.rs`) yana kiran `curl` binary na host domin isa ga local endpoints. Wannan yana ɗaure build da wani host executable, yana gaza a kan systems ba tare da `curl` a PATH ba, yana haifar da fuskar shell-injection, kuma yana karyewa a cikin CI da aka ware daga hanyar sadarwa. Ƙaura da kiran zuwa Rust HTTP client kamar `ureq` yana cire external dependency da injection vector, shi ya sa yake abu na biyu a cikin patch na 0.0.42.

**Menene abu ɗaya mafi muhimmanci a kan hanya zuwa 1.0?**
Cika `DepGraph` da haɗa `--incremental` flag. Incremental rebuilds shi ne gibin sahihanci tsakanin injin da aka rubuta da na hakika, kuma kowace da'awa ta gaba game da builds ƙasa da daƙiƙa ɗaya a shafuka sama da 100,000 tana dogara ga dependency graph mai bin diddigin template-to-page da markdown-to-page edges maimakon ya kasance ababen more rayuwa na gwaji kawai.
