# Ababen Biyan Kuɗi Bayan-Quantum: Me ya sa Bankuna za su Maye Gurbin Tsoffin Hanyoyi Maimakon Inganta Su

<!-- lead-start -->
<aside class="post-lead" aria-label="Taƙaitaccen labari">
<p class="post-lead-tldr"><strong>Taƙaitawa.</strong> Cryptography bayan-quantum yana tilasta tambayar dabaru da bankuna suka jinkirta: a inganta tsoffin hanyoyin biyan kuɗi ta hanyar haɗa algorithm na PQC (ML-KEM, ML-DSA), ko a maye gurbin waɗannan hanyoyin da ababen kintsattse na crypto da aka gina ta yadda juyawar algorithm ta gaba za ta ɗauki kwanaki ba shekaru ba. Amsar 2026 ga galibin bankunan Tier-1 ita ce ta hade: a inganta inda ya dace, a maye gurbin inda tushen tsohon ke yaƙar kintsattse na crypto. Barazanar tara yanzu-warware daga baya tana nufin yanke shawara ba "yaushe" ba ne — "wane ayyuka ne na farko, wane gine-gine zai tsira daga sauyin algorithm na gaba, da kuma wace sarkar dubawa ce mai aminci-quantum kafin maƙiya su warware abubuwan da aka kama yau a 2030."</p>
<p class="post-lead-heading"><strong>Manyan abubuwan da za a kula</strong></p>
<ul class="post-lead-takeaways">
  <li><strong>Inganta haraji ne, ba dabaru ba.</strong> Haɗa PQC akan tsoffin tarin COBOL/HSM/SWIFT MT yana aiki sau ɗaya; yin sa kuma don juyawar algorithm ta gaba ba zai yiwu ba a aikace.</li>
  <li><strong>Kintsattse na crypto shi ne ainihin kayan.</strong> Tambayar gine-gine ita ce "shin za mu iya juyawar algorithm cikin kwanaki?", ba "shin mun shigar da ML-KEM?" ba — bankunan da ke saka kintsattse na crypto cikin tushe suna doke juyawar gaba; waɗanda ba sa yi suna biyan kuɗin ƙaura sau biyu.</li>
  <li><strong>Tara yanzu-warware daga baya ita ce agogo.</strong> Saƙonnin biyan kuɗi na ƙetare iyaka da aka kama yau aka warware a 2030 suna fallasa bayanan abokin ciniki, bayanan zaɓin hanya, alamomin AML — sarkar dubawa dole ta zama mai aminci-quantum kafin wannan ya zama matsalar 2030.</li>
  <li><strong>HSMs da takaddun shaida sune ƙuncin.</strong> Tallafin PQC na HSM na masu kawo, bututun bayar da takaddun shaida, da kayan aikin juyawar maɓalli duk suna buƙatar daidaita da zaɓin algorithm.</li>
  <li><strong>Rarrabuwa ita ce maƙiyi.</strong> Gwaje-gwajen PQC na kowace hanya / kowane yanki / kowane mai kawo suna ƙirƙirar haɗarin aiki na shekaru goma masu zuwa — ƙaura ɗaya da aka tsara tare da shaida mai aunawa ce ke yin nasara.</li>
</ul>

Cryptography bayan-quantum yana haifar wa bankuna tambaya mai wuya: a inganta tsoffin hanyoyin biyan kuɗi ko a maye gurbin su da ababen kintsattse na crypto. Alamar 2026 ita ce hanyoyin aminci-quantum sun ƙaura daga wasan kwaikwayo na ƙirƙira zuwa cikin tsarin aiki na banki, inda tambayar yanke shawara ita ce horon zane: wane bayanai, hanyoyi, sarrafawa, alhakai, da kwararar aikin abokin ciniki ke da alaƙa ([The Finanser](https://thefinanser.com/2026/03/programmable-liquidity-five-foundations-reshaping-modern-treasury "Programmable liquidity")).

> **Taƙaitawar Hukumar Gudanarwa / Manyan Abubuwan da za a Kula**
>
> - **Hanyoyin Aminci-Quantum yanzu suna da dabaru.** Maganar tana da alaƙa da tsarin aiki, juriya, ƙimar abokin ciniki, da shaidar tsari maimakon ƙaramin ƙaddamar da kaya ([The Finanser](https://thefinanser.com/2026/03/programmable-liquidity-five-foundations-reshaping-modern-treasury "Programmable liquidity")).
> - **Ka'idar zane ita ce kintsattse na crypto.** Bankuna suna buƙatar gine-gine da ke haɗa manufofi, kaya, bayanai, zaɓin hanya, sarrafa haɗari, da tattalin arziki masu aunawa ([J.P. Morgan](https://www.jpmorgan.com/insights/payments/trends-innovation/payments-outlook-trends-2026 "Payments Outlook: Five Trends Powering Payments in 2026")).
> - **Tsarin sarrafawa dole ya zama na lokaci-zahiri.** Yanke shawara akan zamba, kuɗi, biyayya, sasantawa, da haɗarin aiki dole su gudana cikin saurin kwararar aiki, ba bayan abin ya faru ba.
> - **Ingancin bayanai ya zama fa'idar kasuwanci.** Bayanai masu tsari, mahallin ma'amala, bayanan duba, da alamomin asali sun zama ginshiƙi don aikin atomatik da kayayyakin da abokan ciniki ke fuskanta.
> - **Rarrabuwa ita ce maƙiyi.** Bankin da ke gina gwaje-gwaje ware a kewayen kowace hanya, token, samfuri, ko buƙatar biyayya yana ƙirƙirar haɗarin aiki na gaba.
> - **Tsarin nasara shi ne tsari.** Cibiyar da za ta iya ja-gora, sarrafawa, kafa farashi, ba da shaida, da bayyana kowace kwararar aiki za ta zarce wadda ta ɗauki wani kayan aiki kawai ([Kyriba](https://www.kyriba.com/blog/iso-20022-corporate-treasury-2026/ "ISO 20022 migration")).

## Me ya sa 2026 ita ce Shekarar da Wannan ya Zama Mai Dabaru

Masana'antar ta wuce matakin karɓuwa. Ba ya isa a haɗa hanya, a ƙaura saƙo, a gudanar da hujjar ra'ayi na AI, ko a sanar da gwajin tokenisation. A 2026, fa'idar dabaru tana fitowa daga tsara waɗannan iyawa akan ainihin kwararar aiki, sannan a tabbatar da cewa kwararar aikin ta fi aminci, sauri, arha, juriya, ko amfani ga abokan ciniki.

Wannan shi ne dalilin da ya sa hanyoyin aminci-quantum yanzu maganar matakin kwamiti ne. Matsi guda suna sake komawa: bayanan biya masu wadata, sasantawa ta lokaci-zahiri, kuɗi tokenised, yanke shawara ta AI, Open Banking, juriyar aiki, taro na cloud, da shaidar tsari mafi ƙarfi. Idan aka kula da su daban-daban, waɗannan matsi suna ƙirƙirar yaduwar shirye-shirye. Idan aka kula da su a matsayin gine-gine ɗaya, suna ƙirƙirar ƙarfin aiki ([The Finanser](https://thefinanser.com/2026/03/programmable-liquidity-five-foundations-reshaping-modern-treasury "Programmable liquidity"), [J.P. Morgan](https://www.jpmorgan.com/insights/payments/trends-innovation/payments-outlook-trends-2026 "Payments Outlook: Five Trends Powering Payments in 2026")).

## Tushen Gine-gine na 2026

### 1. Kwararar Aiki Tukuna, Fasaha ta Biyu

Banki ya kamata ya fara da gogayya: kuɗi a tarko, jinkirin sasantawa, kuɗin daidaitawa, biya da suka kasa, fuskantar zamba, rauni na iya dubawa, ko ƙwarewar abokin ciniki marar kyau. Fasahar ana ba ta hujja ne kawai inda take cire wannan gogayya ([The Finanser](https://thefinanser.com/2026/03/programmable-liquidity-five-foundations-reshaping-modern-treasury "Programmable liquidity")).

### 2. Bayanai a matsayin Filin Sarrafawa

Bayanai masu tsari, masu mulki, kuma masu yiwuwar bi sune tushe. Ba tare da bayanai masu amfani ba, aikin atomatik yana zama mai rauni kuma biyayya tana zama ta hannu. Tare da bayanai masu amfani, bankuna za su iya ƙirƙirar hankalin zaɓin hanya, sarrafawa ta lokaci-zahiri, da nazari ga abokan ciniki ([J.P. Morgan](https://www.jpmorgan.com/insights/payments/trends-innovation/payments-outlook-trends-2026 "Payments Outlook: Five Trends Powering Payments in 2026")).

### 3. Tsari A Kan Hanyoyi da Dandalai

Gine-ginen dole ya tallafa wa hanyoyi da yawa, masu kawo, tsarin asali, alamomin haɗari, da kadarorin sasantawa. Yanke shawarar zaɓin hanya yakamata a yi shi ta hanyar kuɗi, sauri, ƙarewa, hurumi, zaɓin abokin ciniki, juriya, da wadatar bayanai.

### 4. Biyayya da Shaida da aka Saka

Tsarin biyayya dole ya zama na asali ga kwararar aiki. Manufa-a-matsayin-code, bayanan dubawa ta atomatik, shaidar juriyar aiki, bayanan yarda, da mulkin samfuri suna buƙatar samun su a matsayin wani ɓangare na zartarwa, ba a sake ƙirƙira su ga masu duba daga baya ba.

### 5. Tattalin Arzikin Naúrar da Ƙimar Abokin Ciniki

Kowace shirin yana buƙatar shaidar ƙimar kasuwanci. Kuɗin-ga-biyan-kuɗi, kuɗin-ga-yanke-shawara, kuɗin-ga-bincike, kuɗi da aka adana, gyare-gyaren da aka ƙi yin su da hannu, asarar zamba da aka rage, da karɓuwar abokin ciniki yakamata su yanke shawara akan girma.

| **Layer na bayanai** | Bayanan ma'amala da sarrafawa masu tsari, masu mulki | Aiki atomatik, nazari, da iya dubawa | An ƙaura mummunan bayanai cikin sauri |

| **Layer na sarrafawa** | Manufa ta lokaci-zahiri, zamba, takunkumi, juriya, asali, da yarda | Haɗarin da aka sarrafa cikin saurin zartarwa | Biyayya ta hannu bayan abin ya faru |
| **Layer na tattalin arziki** | Kuɗin naúrar da aka auna da ƙimar abokin ciniki | Girma da aka jagoranci ta shaida | Kashe ƙirƙira ba tare da dawowar dorewa ba |

## Me Wannan ke Nufi ta nau'in Banki

### Bankuna na Duniya

Bankunan duniya yakamata su ƙirƙira tsari na matakin dandali ta yadda kowace kasuwa, hanya, token, da iyawar AI ba zai zama tsarin aiki daban ba.

### Bankunan Yanki

Bankunan yanki yakamata su mai da hankali kan amfani inda amincewa, ilimin kasuwan gida, da haɗawa mafi sauƙi ke doke girma: ganin baitulmali, hana zamba, biyan kuɗi na Open Banking, da sabis na kuɗi na dijital da aka tsara.

### Fintechs da PSPs

Fintechs yakamata su rage rikitarwa ga bankuna maimakon ƙara wata hanya keɓe. Mafi kyawun shawarwari za su kawo tsari, shaidar biyayya, ko hankalin bayanai.

### Masu Kula da Baitulmalin Kamfanoni

Masu kula da baitulmali yakamata su nemi ingantawa masu aunawa: ƙarancin gyare-gyaren biya, ganin kuɗi mafi kyau, bayanan daidaitawa masu wadata, sasantawa mai sauri, da iko mafi ƙarfi akan yanke shawara ta atomatik.

## Kammalawa

Ababen Biyan Kuɗi Bayan-Quantum a ƙarshe tambayar gine-gine ce. Cibiyoyin da za su yi nasara ba za su zama waɗanda ke da gwaje-gwaje mafi yawa ko harshen ƙirƙira mafi ƙarfi ba. Za su zama cibiyoyin da ke haɗa kwararar aikin abokin ciniki, ingancin bayanai, tsarin hanyoyi, biyayya da aka saka, da tattalin arzikin naúrar zuwa cikin tsarin aiki mai daidaituwa.

## Tambayoyin da Akan Yi Sosai

**Me ya sa wannan maganar ke da gaggawa a 2026?**

Saboda ababen more rayuwa masu dacewa, tsari, da alamomin buƙatar abokin ciniki sun haɗu. Abin da ya kasance gwajin zaɓi yanzu yana zama wani ɓangare na tsarin aiki na banki.

**Mene ne mafi girman haɗarin aiwatarwa?**

Mafi girman haɗari shi ne rarrabuwa: ƙungiyoyi daban-daban suna gina gwaje-gwaje daban-daban, kowane da bayanai, sarrafawa, mulki, da tattalin arziki daban-daban.

**Mene ne banki ya kamata ya gina farko?**

Banki ya kamata ya fara da kwararar aiki inda akwai ƙima mai aunawa, kamar sasantawa mai sauri, ƙarancin kuɗin daidaitawa, ƙarancin bincike, ingantacciyar hana zamba, ko ganin kuɗi mafi kyau.

**Yaya ya kamata a auna nasara?**

Nasara yakamata a auna ta da tattalin arzikin naúrar, shaidar juriya, ingancin bayanai, karɓuwar abokin ciniki, raguwar haɗarin aiki, da ingantawar kuɗi ko jari na aiki.
