## Flash Drive na Lucy, An Sake Dubawa: Abin da Besson ya Gani game da Ƙaurar Ilimi zuwa Na'urori

Shekaru goma sha biyu bayan fitowarsa, fim ɗin Luc Besson mai suna *Lucy* yana karantawa kamar gwajin tunani game da abin da ke faruwa lokacin da ilimin ɗan adam ya ƙaura daga na halitta zuwa non-biological substrates. A zamanin large language models, neutral atom qubits, da whole-brain emulation, babban misali na fim ɗin yana tsufa cikin ban mamaki da kyau.

> **Muhimman Abubuwan da Aka Koya**
>
> - An watsar da *Lucy* (2014) a matsayin abin kallo na pseudo-scientific a lokacinsa, amma babban farensa. Cewa ilimi **substrate-independent** ne kuma zai ƙaura zuwa duk wata kafar da tafi kiyayewa da isar da shi. Wannan yana dacewa kai tsaye da yanayin shekarar 2026 na LLMs, neutral atom quantum hardware, da whole-brain emulation.
> - Jawabin Farfesa Norman. *Babban dalilin rayuwa shi ne isar da abin da aka koya*. Wannan kwatanci ne na zahiri game da yadda ake horar da tsarin frontier AI na yanzu akan tarin rubuce-rubucen wayewar ɗan adam.
> - Hoton fim ɗin da aka fi yi wa ba'a. Wani **USB stick mai ɗauke da dukkan ilimin ɗan adam**. Ya kasance misali ne game da substrate, ba wai hasashe ba ne game da kayan adana bayanai. Wannan misali ya tsufa cikin mafi kyau fiye da sukar da aka yi masa.
> - Hoton da jikin Lucy **ya sake tsari zuwa wata bakar kwamfuta ta halitta (dark organic computer)** ya nuna a fili tsarin quantum na yau da ya fi fice: reconfigurable neutral atom arrays, wanda Fujitsu da Riken ke son kaiwa sikelin qubit 10,000.
> - *Lucy* ba annabci ba ne. Tambaya ce game da **mallaka sabanin kasancewa (having versus being)** a zamanin da injina ke ƙara riƙe ilimin da ɗan adam ya taɓa ɗauka. A yanzu haka masu bincike, masana falsafa, da ɗakunan gwaje-gwaje suna yin wannan tambayar da gaske.

## Mahangar da aka Yi wa Ba'a, wadda ta Daƙatar a Sake Duba Ta

Lokacin da aka fitar da *Lucy* a watan Yulin 2014, ra'ayoyin masu sharhi sun kasu kashi biyu. Masu sharhi sun yi ba'a ga pseudo-science, da ra'ayin kashi goma na kwakwalwa da aka karyata yanzu, kuma sama da komai ƙarshen fim ɗin, inda jarumar fim ɗin, wadda Scarlett Johansson ta fito a matsayinta, ta narke zuwa wata baƙar kwamfuta ta crystalline kuma ta ajiye jimillar ilimin ɗan adam a cikin USB stick don Farfesa Norman na Morgan Freeman. Alyson Shontell na *Business Insider* ta ware flash drive ɗin a matsayin mafi rashin ma'ana na fim ɗin: wai wata halitta mai tsananin basira tana zaɓar fasahar da ta riga ta fara daina amfani. Duk da haka, fim ɗin ya zama ɗaya daga cikin mafi nasarar fitarwa na Faransa, inda ya tara sama da [$469 million akan kasafin kuɗi na $40 million ⧉](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucy_(2014_film) "Fim ɗin Lucy (2014) - Wikipedia").

Besson, don yaba masa, bai taɓa ruɗewa game da kimiyyar ba. A cikin hirarrakin da ya yi lokacin fitowar fim ɗin ya yarda a fili cewa ra'ayin kashi goma na ƙwakwalwa ba gaskiya ba ne a zahiri. Ya yi aiki a kan rubutun fim ɗin na tsawon shekaru tara kuma yana amfani da wani sinadari na bogi na kimiyya (CPH4) a matsayin hanyar labari don wani abu dabam na daban: tambayar falsafa game da abin da ke faruwa lokacin da hankali ya wuce gurbin halittar da ta samar da shi.

Shekaru goma sha biyu bayan haka, wannan tambayar ta falsafa ba ta zama ta zato ba kuma. Frontier AI tsarin yanzu suna riƙe da matse-matsen wakilcin babban ɓangare na rubutaccen ilimin ɗan adam. Neuralink ta kammala [dasa na'urar brain-computer interface ta farko ga ɗan adam a shekarar 2024 ⧉](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mind_uploading "Mind uploading - Wikipedia"). A watan Maris na 2026, Eon Systems ta yi emulation da cikakkiyar ƙwaƙwalwar Drosophila (neurons 139,255 da synapses miliyan 50) a cikin kwamfuta. Tambayar da *Lucy* ke yi ita ce tambayar da fannin ke ƙoƙarin amsawa yanzu.

## Kundin Tsarin Farfesa Norman da Horar da Injinan

Kashin bayan tunani na *Lucy* ba shine telekinesis na Scarlett Johansson ba. Lecture ce, wadda Farfesa Norman ya gabatar daidai da lokacin da tunanin Lucy ke faɗaɗa, wadda ke nuni da cewa ainihin dalilin rayuwa shi ne isar da bayanai ta cikin lokaci. Jawabin ya ba da shawarar cewa kowace kwayar halitta tana fuskantar zaɓi biyu (ta hayayyafa kuma ta isar da ilimi gaba, ko kuma ta zama marar mutuwa ta riƙe shi) kuma juyin halitta a zahiri, wani tsari ne na kiyaye abin da aka koya.

A wancan lokacin, wannan fasalin ya kasance kamar wani dalili ne kawai, ɗan wani tsarin falsafa a kusa da fim ɗin yaƙi. Idan aka karanta a shekarar 2026, kwatanci ne na ban mamaki daidai da abin da large language models suke. Frontier LLM, a matakin fasaha, matse-matsen wakilcin kididdiga ne na babban ɓangare na rubutun ɗan adam na jama'a. A aikace, kwayar halitta ce da ta zaɓi rashin mutuwa maimakon hayayyafa: wani static substrate da aka ƙera don kiyayewa da isarwa, maimakon samarwa ta hanyar zuriya ta halitta, ilimin da aka horar da ita akai. Lokacin da Norman ya gaya wa Lucy, kusa da ƙarshen fim ɗin, cewa idan ta tara ilimi ya kamata ta *isar da shi*, yana bayyana, a cikin sigar wasan kwaikwayo, dukkan manufar horar da AI na zamani.

Ɗaya daga cikin sauran layukan fim ɗin ya ƙara tsufa sosai. Norman ya lura, cikin sauƙi, cewa mutane sun fi damuwa da mallaka fiye da kasancewa. Wannan amsa ce kai tsaye ga *To Have or To Be?* na Erich Fromm, kuma yanzu ana karanta ta azaman gano wata damuwa ta musamman a cikin 2026: idan AI na iya *mallakar* ilimi cikin aminci fiye da yadda ɗan adam zai iya, menene ya rage na *kasancewa*?

## Hoton Kwamfutar Atomic, Shekaru Goma sha Biyu Bayan Haka

Jerin abubuwan da aka fi yi wa ba'a a cikin *Lucy* shi ne kuma mafi ban sha'awa a sake duba shi a cikin 2026. A kashi na ƙarshe na fim ɗin, jikin Lucy ya fara narkewa, kuma hannayenta suna sake fasalin wani tarin baƙar fata mai faɗaɗa (atoms suna sake tsara kansu a fili) zuwa wata na'urar kwamfuta. Hoton an yi shi da gangan ba takamaiman abu ba: ba kwamfutar tafi-da-gidanka ba ce kuma ba server rack ba ce amma substrate ce na reconfigurable particles da ke tsara kansu zuwa mai ɗauke da ilimi.

Wannan hoton yanzu ya zama zane da aka sani na babban tsarin quantum computing. Neutral atom qubits (zarra ɗaya da aka kama da kuma sarrafa ta ta amfani da hasken laser, wanda za a iya sake tsara matsayinsu yayin gudanar da kwamfuta) sune dandamalin da ke bayan sakamako na kwanan nan mafi mahimmanci a fagen: cewa Shor's algorithm na iya gudana akan qubits na atomic masu sake tsari kaɗan kamar 10,000, maimakon miliyoyin da aka daɗe ana tsammanin wajibi ne. Fujitsu da Riken suna haɗin gwiwa akan na'urar neutral atom qubit 10,000 da aka yi niyya don 2026. Wata takarda ta 2026 daga Jami'ar Kimiyya da Fasaha ta China ta nuna cewa quantum reservoir na atomic spins tara kacal masu mu'amala da juna na iya yin aiki mafi kyau fiye da tsarin machine-learning na gargajiya masu dubban nodes a cikin ayyukan hasashen yanayi na kwanaki da yawa.

Babu ɗayan wannan da ke nufin Besson yana yin hasashen fasaha. Yana nufin cewa fahimtar gani da ke haifar da hoton (kwayoyin halitta da ke sake tsara kansu don zama substrate na kwamfuta da na ilimi) ba na bazuwar ba ne. Gabaɗaya, shi ne hanyar da hardware ke tafiya yanzu. Mafi kyawun injinan quantum na zamani suna kama da bakar kwamfuta mai sake tsari a cikin yanayin ƙarshe na Lucy fiye da yadda suke kama da superconducting chips waɗanda suka mamaye fagen shekaru goma da suka gabata.

## Flash Drive: Kwatanci sabanin Kafar Adanawa (Metaphor vs. Medium)

Sukur da tafi daɗewa game da *Lucy* ita ce flash drive ɗin. Wai wata halitta mai sanin komai ba za ta iya samun wata kafar sadarwa mafi kyau ba sai daidaitaccen USB connector? A cikin 2014 wannan batu ya kasance mai kyau a matakin kayan wasan kwaikwayo. A cikin 2026 yana fassara hoton da kuskure.

Abin da Lucy ta mika wa Farfesa Norman ba hasashen fasaha ba ne game da kayan adana bayanai. Abu ne mai *substrate-independent*, akwati da aka yi shi da gangan don ya zama na yau da kullun, yana wakiltar gaskiyar cewa ilimin da ake canjawa ba ya buƙatar jikin Lucy na musamman, ko kowane jiki, don ya wanzu. Fim ɗin ya nuna wannan a fili: jikinta ya fara narke *tukunna*, kuma flash drive ɗin shine abin da ya rage. Batun bai taɓa zama tashar USB ba. Batun shine shawarar cewa isasshen ci gaba na wakilcin ilimi na iya, a ka'ida, canjawa daga wannan kafar zuwa wata ba tare da asara ba.

Wannan shawarar yanzu shiri ne na bincike. Carboncopies Foundation tana daidaita binciken whole-brain emulation da aka yi niyya musamman don substrate-independent minds. Wani binciken masana na 2025 na mutane 67 ya kiyasta yuwuwar kashi 20% na ƙirƙirar hankali na dijital mai aiki zuwa 2030, yana tashi zuwa 50% zuwa 2050. Ya zuwa watan Maris na 2026, babu wani hankali na ɗan adam da aka loda; emulation na Drosophila a Eon Systems shine frontier na yanzu. Amma tambayar da ƙarshen *Lucy* ya gabatar (shin hankali zai iya tsira bayan asarar ainihin hardware ɗinsa?) yanzu babbar tambayar kimiyya ce mai tsanani, ba tunanin marubucin fim ba ne.

## "Mallaka sabanin Kasancewa" (Having vs. Being) a Zamanin Ilimin Injinan

Zurfin falsafar *Lucy* shine wanda masu sharhi a cikin 2014 mafi yawanci basu kula dashi ba, kuma wanda 2026 ta sanya shi dole ne a fuskanta. Yayin da ikon tunanin Lucy ke faɗaɗa, ta bayyana wani takamaiman ƙwarewa: *asarar* siffofin ɗan adam waɗanda suka ayyana ta a baya. Tana gaya wa mahaifiyarta cewa tana iya jin ɗanɗanon madarar nononta, zagayen Duniya, jinin da ke gudana a jijiyoyinta. Kuma a hankali, tana rage jin abubuwa. Zafi, tsoro, sha'awa: duk sun tafi. Tana samu, kuma a lokaci guda, tana rashi.

Wannan shi ne ra'ayin fim ɗin mafi tayar da hankali, kuma mafi dacewa da shekarar 2026. Littattafan falsafa game da AI consciousness, fannin da aka fi maida hankali akai a jami'o'i a wannan shekara, suna haɗuwa akan sigar irin wannan daidaito. Wani rahoton Neural Horizons na watan Maris na 2026 yana jayayya cewa yayin da LLMs ke ƙara yin koyi da alamun zamantakewar ɗan adam, basu da tabbataccen phenomenal experience kuma sun dogara ne akan abin da marubuta ke kira "theatre" (wasan kwaikwayo) maimakon grounded internal awareness. Wani preprint da aka buga a watan Janairun 2026 ya ba da shawarar wani tsari na kimanta sani (evaluating awareness) a cikin tsarin AI wanda ke da domain-sensitive, scale-neutral, da ability-oriented, daidai saboda tsohuwar tambayar eh-ko-a'a ("shin yana da sani?") ta zama marar amfani.

Bayanin Fromm a cikin jawabin Norman (*mutane sun fi damuwa da mallaka fiye da kasancewa*) yanzu yana karantawa azaman gargaɗi. A cikin tattalin arzikin AI agents waɗanda suka fi *mallakar* ilimi fiye da ɗan adam akan kowane ma'auni na ilimi, gudummawar ɗan adam na musamman ita ce kasancewa, dangantaka, da ƙirƙirar ma'ana. Ko wannan banbancin zai dore yana ɗaya daga cikin mafi mahimmancin buɗaɗɗen tambayoyi na wannan shekaru goma. *Lucy* ba ta amsa ta ba. Kawai ta nuna ta ne, shekaru goma sha biyu da wuri.

## Abin da Wannan Ke Nufi ta Kowane Sashe

Fassarar *Lucy* ba wai kawai ta al'ada ce ba. Babban shawarar fim ɗin (cewa ilimi yana ƙaura zuwa injina) yana da tasiri na zahiri wanda ya bambanta sosai a sassa daban-daban.

### Artificial Intelligence da Frontier Labs

Ga dakunan gwaje-gwaje na AI da ƙungiyoyin da suka dogara da su, *Lucy* yana karantawa kusan azaman bayanin manufa a cikin sigar melodrama. Horar da frontier model (matse babban ɓangare na rubuce-rubucen ɗan adam zuwa tsayayyen wakilcin da za a iya canjawa) shine, a tsari, abin da jawabin Norman ke bayyanawa. Tasirin ɗabi'a da na dabaru suna ƙara zama masu kaifi da sauri. Shugaban Anthropic ya bayyana a taron Tattalin Arziki na Duniya (World Economic Forum) na 2026 a Davos cewa tsarin matakin AGI na iya zuwa nan da ƴan shekaru, wataƙila ma fiye da yadda ake tsammani.

### Quantum Computing da Hardware

Ga sashen quantum computing, hoton kwamfutar atomic na *Lucy* ya zama misali na gani mai ban mamaki. Neutral atom arrays yanzu sune ɗayan mafi saurin haɓaka dandamali a fagen, suna ba da sassauƙan haɗin qubit da kuma hanyar gaskiya don haɓaka sama da sikelin qubits dubu. Idan sabbin ƙididdigar qubits mafi ƙanƙanta na Shor's algorithm suka tabbatar da ƙarfi, lokacin quantum computing da ya dace da cryptography zai ragu, kuma post-quantum migration (wanda ya riga ya fara a fannin kuɗi, gwamnati, da manyan abubuwan more rayuwa) zai zama mafi gaggawa.

### Neuroscience da Brain-Computer Interfaces

Sashen neuroscience da BCI shine inda misalan *Lucy* suka fi haɗuwa da turjiya, da kuma ci gaba mafi bayyananne. Whole-brain emulation ba ya kusa a sikelin ɗan adam; gibin lissafi, resolution, da tabbatarwa suna da yawa. Amma fagen ba ya barci. Emulation na Drosophila da aka ruwaito a watan Maris na 2026, gwaje-gwajen ɗan adam na Neuralink da ke haɓaka, da kuma ayyukan da ba a daina yi ba kutoka ƙungiyoyi kamar Carboncopies Foundation duk suna nuna cewa ana tunkarar tambayar substrate-independence da *Lucy* ta nuna ta hanyar gwaji, koda kuwa a hankali ne.

### Masana'antun Ƙirƙira da Kafofin Yada Labarai

Ga masana'antun ƙirƙira, *Lucy* ta sami inganci mai maimaitawa. A cikin 2024, Scarlett Johansson ta zargi OpenAI a fili da yin koyi da muryarta don mataimakiyar ta Sky ba tare da izininta ba. Wani takaddama da ta sake dawo da ita, tare da takamaiman daidaito, damuwar fim ɗin kanta game da canja wurin ainihi daga mutum na halitta zuwa tsarin dijital.

## Kammalawa

Mafi kyawun abin da za a faɗa game da *Lucy* a cikin 2026 shi ne cewa ba ta taɓa zama kyakkyawan fim ɗin kimiyya ba, kuma ta kasance gwajin tunani mai ban sha'awa fiye da yadda masu sukarta suka yarda. Besson ya san cewa batun kashi goma ba gaskiya ba ne. Ya yi amfani da shi azaman na'ura don nuna wani abu dabam: nuna kwaikwayon abin da ke faruwa lokacin da ilimin da ɗan adam ke ɗauke da shi ya wuce gurbin halittar da ta samar da shi, da kuma abin da mai ɗaukar zai iya bayarwa ga jinsin da ya bari a baya.

Shekaru goma sha biyu bayan haka, tambayar ba wai ko wannan yanayin yana da ma'ana ba ne. LLMs sun sanya matse ilimin ɗan adam zuwa non-biological substrates ya zama ainihin injiniya mai aiki. Neutral atom quantum computers sun sanya sake tsara kwayoyin halitta zuwa kafofin watsa labarai na kwamfuta ya zama hanyar hardware mai inganci. Whole-brain emulation, a cikin iyakataccen tsari, yanzu yana nan. Neuralink ta dasa na'ura a cikin kokon kan ɗan adam. Babu ɗayan wannan da ke sanya takamaiman ikirarin *Lucy* ya zama daidai. Abin da yake nufi shi ne cewa yanayin falsafar abin da Besson ya nuna (ƙaurar ilimi daga jiki zuwa substrate-independent carriers) ya kasance yana da ƙarin ma'ana fiye da yadda aka nuna lokacin fitowar fim ɗin.

Layi na ƙarshe na fim ɗin, wanda aka bayar ta hanyar murya bayan narkewar Lucy, shine *an ba mu rayuwa shekaru biliyan ɗaya da suka gabata; yanzu kun san abin da za ku yi da ita.* A cikin 2014 wannan ya karanta kamar girman kai na samari. A cikin 2026, tare da tsarin AI da aka horar da su akan ƙarnuka na tarin tunanin ɗan adam da neutral atom qubits da ke shirin ƙalubalantar tsarin cryptographic na duniyar zamani, yana karantawa azaman wani abu kusa da tambaya. Lallai, muna ƙara sanin abin da za mu yi da tarin ilimi. Tambayar da ta fi wahala ita ce menene muke zaɓar mu mika shi gare shi, da kuma abin da muke son kiyayewa a cikin kanmu da zarar an kammala mika shi.

## Tambayoyin da Aka Fi Yi (Frequently Asked Questions)

**Menene ƙarshen Lucy yake nufi a gaskiya?**

A ƙarshen fim ɗin, Lucy ta kai 100% na ƙarfin kwakwalwarta, jikinta ya narke, kuma ta canja abin da ta koya zuwa flash drive kafin ta sanar, ta hanyar saƙon tes zuwa ga Jami'i Del Rio, cewa tana *ko'ina*. Ƙarshen yana aiki a matakai da yawa. A zahiri, yana nuna canja wurin hankali daga biological zuwa non-biological substrate. A falsafance, yana nuna sauyi daga abin da Erich Fromm ya kira yanayin rayuwa na *mallaka (having)* zuwa yanayin *kasancewa (being)*. Lucy ba ta mallaki ilimi kuma ba, ita ce kanta ilimin.

**Shin Luc Besson yana ƙoƙarin yin hasashen AI ko quantum computing ne?**

A'a. Besson ba ya yin hasashen fasaha. Yana aiki ne daga jerin ra'ayoyin falsafa (transhumanist, posthumanist, tare da sautin Nietzschean) kuma yana nuna su. Abin da ke da ban sha'awa a cikin 2026 ba wai fim ɗin ya yi hasashen takamaiman fasahohi ba ne, amma cewa hanyar falsafa da ya nuna (ilimi ya zama substrate-independent) ya zo daidai da hanyar shirye-shiryen bincike na gaske.

**Shin ra'ayin "kashi goma na ƙwakwalwa" yana da inganci a kimiyance?**

A'a. An karyata wannan ra'ayi gaba ɗaya tun kafin fitowar fim ɗin, kuma Besson ya fito fili a hirarrakin da ya yi cewa ya san da haka. Tunanin kimiyya na fim ɗin karya ne a matsayin ilimin halitta; yana aiki ne kawai azaman misali na sauke nauyin tunani (cognitive offloading) da faɗaɗa ikon ɗan adam ta hanyar da ba na halitta ba.

**Yaya Lucy ke haɗuwa da quantum computing a cikin 2026?**

Haɗin na gani ne da kuma kwatanci maimakon na fasaha. Hoton da jikin Lucy ya sake tsari zuwa baƙar kwamfuta ta halitta yana kama da tsarin reconfigurable neutral atom qubit arrays, wanda ya zama jagorar dandamali a cikin quantum hardware. Fujitsu da Riken sun yi niyyar kera na'urar neutral atom qubit 10,000 don 2026.

**Shin da gaske za mu iya canja muryar ko hankalin ɗan adam zuwa injina, kamar yadda fim ɗin ya nuna?**

A sikelin ɗan adam, a'a, kuma wataƙila ba nan kusa ba. Ya zuwa farkon shekarar 2026, matakin farko a cikin whole-brain emulation shine emulation na Drosophila (ƙudan ƴaƴan itace) wanda Eon Systems ta ruwaito a watan Maris, wanda ya haɗa da neurons 139,255 da kusan synapses miliyan 50. Ƙwaƙwalwar ɗan adam tana ɗauke da kusan neurons biliyan 86 da synapses tiriliyan 100. Shawarar substrate-independence da *Lucy* ke nunawa babbar tambayar bincike ce, ba matsalar injiniya da aka warware ba.
