# Buɗaɗɗen Tushe, FINOS da Tarin CIB na Cloud-Native

A Yuni 2026, ajandar fasaha ta bankin saka hannun jari na kamfanoni (CIB) ta daina yin riya. Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan da Citi suna zaune a kan kwamitin gudanarwa na FINOS kuma yanzu suna ɗaukar buɗaɗɗen tushe a matsayin ainihin tushen aiki, ba aikin gefe ba — sauyi da Banking Dive ya kama a cikin rahotonsa na baya-bayan nan game da bankuna ukun da ke ƙara tsananta dogaro akan lamba ta gama gari ta hanyar [FINOS](https://www.finos.org/) da [Linux Foundation](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/) ([Banking Dive, 2026](https://www.bankingdive.com/news/bank-technology-open-source-finos-morgan-stanley-jpmorgan-citi/743937/ "Bank tech leaders double down on open source")). Dalilin ba shi da daɗi ga masu sayarwa: tarin CIB yanzu yana buƙatar za a iya bincika shi daga ƙarshe zuwa ƙarshe, kuma akwatunan mallaka ba sa rayuwa daga binciken DORA Article 5.

Wannan labarin yana haɗa wancan sauyi zuwa gefen injiniya. Ɗakunan karatu na Rust da nake bugawa — [noyalib](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/noyalib), [http-handle](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/http-handle), [hsh](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/hsh), [KyberLib](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/kyberlib), [html-generator](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/html-generator), [Shokunin SSG](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/shokunin) — ba su ne batun ba kawai. Misalai ne na zahiri na yadda tarin CIB na cloud-native yake yanzu lokacin da ka ɗauki ka'idar FINOS da gaske: lasisi masu izini, sifili `unsafe`, kayan aikin da aka sa hannu, da tushen jerin samar da kayayyaki da aka gina a lokacin tarawa.

## 01. Me Yasa CIBs Ke Komawa Buɗaɗɗe

Matsi uku suna tura CIBs zuwa buɗaɗɗen tushe, kuma babu ɗayansu da ke da akida.

**Basira.** Mafi ƙarfin injiniyoyin tushen ababen more rayuwa a 2026 suna ginawa a fili. Rahoton FINOS na 2025 game da Halin Buɗaɗɗen Tushe a cikin Sabis na Kuɗi ya sanya tushen masu ba da gudummawa a kan ƙarshen babba na haɓaka, tare da masu kula da bankin yanzu suna ganuwa a fadin ayyukan runtime na CNCF da workstreams na FINOS ([Linux Foundation, 2025](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/hubfs/Research%20Reports/05_FINOS_2025_Report.pdf?hsLang=en "State of Open Source in Financial Services 2025")). Lokacin da CTO na Tier-1 ke buƙatar babban injiniyan Rust ko Kotlin wanda zai iya jigilar sake rubuta tsarin tsabtacewa, wannan injiniyan yana sa ran zai ba da gudummawa a sama. Shaguna na mallaka kawai suna rasa tattaunawar daukar ma'aikata da wuri.

**Bin doka.** DORA Article 5 yana sanya alhakin haɗarin ICT wanda ba za a iya wakilta ba akan hukumar. Basel III yana danganta babban birnin haɗarin aiki zuwa tsayawa. Tsare-tsaren biyu suna ɗauka cewa cibiyar za ta iya bincikar kowane sashi a cikin hanyar samarwa — kuma hakan yana da sauƙi ta tsarin lamba ta buɗaɗɗen tushe mai izini a ƙarƙashin MIT, Apache 2.0 ko BSD-3-Clause fiye da na fitar da ISV na akwatin baƙar fata inda SBOM yake "amince da mu". CycloneDX da SPDX bills na kayan masarufi, tabbatattun tushen SLSA da sa hannu na sigstore yanzu sune mafi ƙanƙantan ma'auni da mai kula da doka yake tsammanin gani an makala da bututun fitarwa.

**Saurin isarwa.** Ƙungiyar dandali na CIB da ke jigilar canjin injin biya cikin kwanaki maimakon kwata-kwata ba ta yin nasara akan jaruntaka. Tana yin nasara akan tushe na gama gari — Kubernetes, OpenTelemetry, ɗakunan karatu na schema na ISO 20022, FINOS Common Domain Model — wanda babu wanda ke biya don sake aiwatarwa. Tattalin arziki bai sake fifita hanyoyi na musamman ba.

Matsi uku, ƙaddara ɗaya. Komawa buɗaɗɗe shawarar isarwa ce, ba ta sayayya ba.

## 02. Tarin Rust Mara Dogaro

Tarin CIB na cloud-native a 2026 ba shi da hoton zamanin LAMP na "buɗaɗɗen tushe = Linux + nginx + Postgres". Saiti ne mai laushi na abubuwan da aka lasisa cikin izini, masu aminci na ƙwaƙwalwa — kowannensu yana da SBOM nasa, tushensa, da ƙarancin saman barazana. Ɗakunan karatu na Rust da nake kulawa suna taswira da kyau zuwa wannan laushi.

- **Shigarwar gefe.** [http-handle](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/http-handle) sabar HTTP/1.1 ce mara dogaro, mai bin RFC 7230 / 9112 da aka rubuta a safe Rust — an gina ta don lokacin da ƙungiyar dandali ta CIB ta gane cewa layin shigarwa bai kamata ya ja crates 200 masu wucewa ba. An tsara hujjarsa a cikin [http-handle: Shigarwar Gefe Mara Dogaro don Banki a Rust](https://sebastienrousseau.com/2026-06-20-http-handle-zero-dependency-edge-ingress-banking-rust-2026).
- **Tsarin tsari.** [noyalib](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/noyalib) yana parse YAML 1.2 tare da 406/406 spec compliance, JSON-Schema validation da lossless concrete syntax tree — don haka manifests na Kubernetes, rajistun servers na MCP da workflows na CI sun daina zama saman hari na shiru. Duba [Me Yasa YAML Ke Buƙatar Tarin Rust Mai Aminci don AI, MCP, da Tsarin Kuɗi a 2026](https://sebastienrousseau.com/2026-06-18-noyalib-safe-yaml-rust-ai-mcp-financial-infrastructure-2026).
- **Tushen cryptography.** [hsh](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/hsh) yana ba da Argon2id, bcrypt da scrypt hashing na kalmar sirri tare da API na tabbatarwa mai daidaiton lokaci. [KyberLib](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/kyberlib) yana aiwatar da ML-KEM-512/768/1024 a ƙarƙashin FIPS 203 don ƙaurar post-quantum da aka bincika a cikin [KyberLib da Ƙaurar Banki ta Post-Quantum a 2026](https://sebastienrousseau.com/2026-06-12-kyberlib-post-quantum-banking-migration-standards-code-2026).
- **Abun ciki da isarwar gefe.** [html-generator](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/html-generator) yana tara Markdown mai isa zuwa HTML mai tsari; [Shokunin SSG](https://github.com/sebastienrousseau/shokunin) yana gina littafin da kake karantawa; [CloudCDN](https://sebastienrousseau.com/2026-06-11-cloudcdn-open-source-blueprint-ai-native-edge-2026) yana zaune a gabansa a matsayin gefe na buɗaɗɗen tushe, na AI-native.

Babu ɗayan waɗannan da yake "framework" a ma'anar bankin gado. Ƙanana ne, lasisi cikin izini, sassan da aka sa hannu tare da samfuran barazana bayyananne. Wannan shi ne sifar aiki da ka'idar FINOS ke ƙarfafawa — kuma sifar da ƙungiyar dandali na CIB za ta iya kare ta gaban mai kula da doka ba tare da bayanin nunin sliding ba.

Wani ƙaramin gargaɗi mai gaskiya: manufar ba "sake rubuta bankin a Rust ba". Shine a ba ƙungiyoyin dandali na CIB zaɓi na tarin mai aminci na ƙwaƙwalwa, mara dogaro a laushin masu ɗauke da nauyi — shigarwa, parsing, cryptography, ginawa, jerin samar da kayayyaki — ba tare da tilasta shawarar addini a wani wuri ba.

## 03. Buɗaɗɗen Tushe Shine Tushen Ajandar ISO, AI da Quantum

Ajandojin CIB guda uku na tsari na 2026 — sauyi na ISO 20022, agentic AI a aiki, da ƙaurar cryptography post-quantum — duk suna gudana akan lambar da za a iya bincika. Babu ɗayansu da ke aiki a matsayin tarin mallaka.

**ISO 20022.** Iyalin schema na pacs.008 / pacs.009 / camt yanzu shine tsohuwa na biyan kuɗi na babba. FINOS yana karbar bakuncin Common Domain Model tare da ɗakunan karatu na Java da Kotlin masu buɗewa waɗanda ke parse, tabbatarwa da hanyar tafiyar da waɗannan saƙonnin. Aikin a cikin [Atomatik na pacs.008 da Biyan Kuɗi Tsakanin Bankuna na ISO 20022](https://sebastienrousseau.com/2026-06-15-pacs008-automation-iso-20022-interbank-payments-2026) yana nuna yadda bututun matakin tsabtacewa ke haɗuwa daga waɗannan abubuwan buɗewa — schema validation, remittance mai tsari, ganowa daga ƙarshe zuwa ƙarshe — ba tare da sake gina parser a kowane banki ba.

**Agentic AI.** Model Context Protocol (MCP) shi ne harshen gama gari don bari wakilan AI su kira kayan aikin banki na ciki — kuma servers na MCP suna gudana akan rajistun YAML, asusun sabis da OAuth ya iyakance, da bututun rajistar audit. Tsarin sarrafawa buɗaɗɗen tushe ne saboda dole ne ya kasance: kowane wakili da ke taɓa ledger na samarwa yana buƙatar tafiyar da aiki mai iyaka da za a iya bincika. Hujjar yin maganin hakan a matsayin matsalar injiniya maimakon zaɓin mai sayarwa tana gudana ta [Me Yasa YAML Ke Buƙatar Tarin Rust Mai Aminci](https://sebastienrousseau.com/2026-06-18-noyalib-safe-yaml-rust-ai-mcp-financial-infrastructure-2026) da aikin tashar aiki na dotfiles a [Dotfiles Masu Sane da AI a 2026](https://sebastienrousseau.com/2026-06-16-ai-aware-dotfiles-secure-reproducible-workstation-2026).

**Cryptography post-quantum.** FIPS 203 (ML-KEM) da FIPS 204 (ML-DSA) yanzu sune manufofin ƙaura. Musayar maɓallin haɗaka X25519MLKEM768 ita ce tsohuwa mai amfani a cikin TLS 1.3. Babu ɗayan wannan da ke aiki ba tare da aiwatarwa masu buɗewa waɗanda masu binciken doka da ƙungiyoyin cryptography na banki za su iya karantawa layi zuwa layi — KyberLib na zama misali ɗaya, kuma faɗin ƙirar ƙaura ita ce batun [KyberLib da Ƙaurar Banki ta Post-Quantum a 2026](https://sebastienrousseau.com/2026-06-12-kyberlib-post-quantum-banking-migration-standards-code-2026).

Ajandoji uku. Dogaron raba ɗaya: lambar buɗewa, sigstore ya sa hannu, SLSA ya tabbatar, an lissafa a cikin CycloneDX ko SPDX SBOM, OSSF scorecards suka gudanar. Wancan shi ne tarin CIB na cloud-native a 2026.

## 04. Yin Dandali a Ƙarƙashin PSD3 da FiDA

Ajandar yin dandali na Turai — PSD3, Dokar Sabis na Biyan Kuɗi, da tsarin Samun Damar Bayanan Kuɗi (FiDA) — jajircewa ce ta tsari ga buɗaɗɗen kuɗi. Yana ɗauka cewa bankuna za su iya bayyana, gudanarwa da bincika kwararar bayanai a sikeli. Buɗaɗɗun ka'idoji sune sharaɗin farko, ba sakamako na gefe ba.

Hangen nesa na Consultancy.uk na 2026 game da daidaita buɗaɗɗen banki don haɓakar dandali yana yin lura iri ɗaya daga gefen kasuwanci: cibiyoyin da ke yin nasara a ƙarƙashin PSD3 sune waɗanda ke ɗaukar gadon API a matsayin samfuri, ba kamar tunani na bin doka bayan-lamarin ba ([Consultancy.uk, 2026](https://www.consultancy.uk/news/42202/orchestrating-open-banking-for-platform-growth-2026-outlook "Orchestrating open banking for platform growth — 2026 outlook")). Wannan tsayuwa ba ta yiwuwa akan tarin rufaffe. Yin samfuri na APIs yana buƙatar ƙayyadaddun OpenAPI tare da sigogi, gwajin kwangila ta atomatik, lura a fadin kowane mai amfani, da layi na gudanarwa da mai bincike zai iya tafiya ta ciki. Kowane ɗaya daga cikin waɗancan tushe buɗaɗɗen tushe ne a 2026, kuma mafi yawansu suna zaune a ayyukan CNCF ko FINOS.

Hankali iri ɗaya ya kai zuwa fadin perimeter na samun damar bayanai na FiDA — fansho, mortgage, samfuran saka hannun jari. Bankin da ke sarrafa parsing nasa, shigarwa, tsari da cryptography tare da lambar da za a iya bincika zai iya faɗaɗa perimeter ba tare da sake gina gine-gine ba. Bankin da ya ba da waɗancan laushi ga masu sayar da rufaffe zai biya masu ba da shawara na haɗawa na shekaru uku masu zuwa. Ka'idar FINOS ita ce, a zuciyarta, ka'idar yin dandali: mallaki ka'idoji, raba tushen, gasa akan saman.

## Kammalawa

Tarin CIB a 2026 buɗaɗɗe ne ta tsohuwa. Ba saboda akida ba, amma saboda matsi uku — basira, bin doka, saurin isarwa — suna ja a hanya ɗaya, kuma masu kula da doka (DORA, Basel III, PSD3, FiDA) sun tabbatar da shi. Rahoton Banking Dive akan Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan da Citi sigar jama'a ce ta tattaunawar sirri da manyan ƙungiyoyin dandali ke yi shekaru biyu.

Ga hukumomi, abin da ke nufi a sarari ne. Tambayar ba "shin za mu yi amfani da buɗaɗɗen tushe" ba ne. Ita ce: shin muna da SBOMs, tushen SLSA, sa hannu na sigstore, OSSF scorecards, da manufar gudummawa da aka daidaita da FINOS don amfani da shi cikin aminci. Idan amsar ba ce, amsar ga mai kula da doka kuma ba za ta zama ba.

Ga shugabannin injiniya, abin da ke nufi ya fi kaifi. Zaɓi laushi masu ɗauke da nauyi — shigarwa, parsing, cryptography, ginawa, jerin samar da kayayyaki — kuma ka daidaita akan abubuwan da aka lasisa cikin izini, masu aminci na ƙwaƙwalwa tare da samfuran barazana bayyananne. Misalai na tarin Rust mara dogaro a cikin wannan labarin saiti ɗaya ne mai inganci. Batun shine sifar, ba alamar ba. Gina tushen don saman zai iya motsawa da sauri.
