DORA, EU AI Act, GDPR, haɗarin tarawar cloud, da ikon mallakar bayanai suna haɗuwa zuwa tarin bin doka guda na bankuna a 2026. Alamar 2026 ita ce gine-ginen bin doka ya kau daga gidan wasan kwaikwayo na kirkire-kirkire zuwa cikin samfurin aikin bankuna, inda tambaya mai mahimmanci ita ce horon ƙira: wace bayanai, hanyoyi, sarrafawa, alhakuna, da tafiyar ayyukan abokan ciniki suka dace tare ([European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai "AI Act")).

> - **Samfurin nasara shine tsari.** Cibiyar da za ta iya tafiyarwa, gudanarwa, farashi, shaida, da bayyana kowane aiki za ta wuce wadda kawai ta ɗauki wani kayan aiki ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-fintech-backed-to-embrace-future-payments-technology "UK fintech backed to embrace future payments technology")).

## Me Yasa 2026 Ita ce Shekarar da Wannan Ya Zama Dabara

Masana'antar ta wuce matakin ɗauka. Ba ya isa kawai a haɗu da hanya, a yi ƙaura da saƙo, a gudanar da tabbacin ra'ayi na AI, ko a sanar da gwajin tokenisation. A 2026, gefen dabara yana zuwa daga tsara waɗannan damar a kan ainihin aiki, sannan a tabbatar da cewa aikin ya fi aminci, sauri, arha, juriya, ko amfani ga abokan ciniki.

Wannan shi ya sa gine-ginen bin doka yanzu batu ne na matakin allo. Matsalolin guda suna ci gaba: bayanan biya masu wadata, sasantawa a ainihin lokaci, kuɗi mai tokenised, yanke shawara na AI, Open Banking, juriya na aiki, tarawar cloud, da shaidar hukuma mai ƙarfi. Idan aka kula da su daban-daban, waɗannan matsi suna haifar da yaɗuwar shirye-shirye. Idan aka kula da su a matsayin gine-gine guda, suna haifar da ɗagawa na aiki ([European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai "AI Act"), [IOMETE](https://iomete.com/resources/blog/data-sovereignty-compliance-2026-dora-ai-act "Data Sovereignty Compliance in 2026")).

## Tushen Gine-gine na 2026

### 1. Aiki Tukuna, Fasaha Bayan

Bankin ya kamata ya fara da rikici: ruwa da aka kama, jinkirin sasantawa, kuɗin sasantawa, biyan kuɗi da ya gaza, fallasa zamba, rashin ƙarfi na bincike, ko rashin kyawun kwarewa ta abokin ciniki. Fasahar tana da hujja kawai inda take cire wannan rikici ([European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai "AI Act")).

### 2. Bayanai a Matsayin Saman Sarrafawa

Bayanai masu tsari, gudanarwa, da bin diddigi su ne tushe. Ba tare da bayanai da za a iya amfani da su ba, sarrafa kai yana zama mai rauni kuma bin doka yana zama na hannu. Tare da bayanai da za a iya amfani da su, bankuna za su iya ƙirƙirar hankali na hanya, sarrafa ainihin lokaci, da nazarce-nazarcen abokin ciniki ([IOMETE](https://iomete.com/resources/blog/data-sovereignty-compliance-2026-dora-ai-act "Data Sovereignty Compliance in 2026")).

### 3. Tsari a Kan Hanyoyi da Dandamali

Gine-ginen dole ya goyi bayan hanyoyi da yawa, masu samar da kayayyaki, tsare-tsaren ainihi, siginar haɗari, da kadarorin sasantawa. Yanke shawarar tafiyarwa ya kamata a yi shi ta kuɗi, sauri, gama-gari, ikon yanki, fifikon abokin ciniki, juriya, da wadatar bayanai.

### 4. Bin Doka da Shaida Da Aka Saka

Samfurin bin doka dole ya zama na asali ga aikin. Manufa-a-matsayin-code, log na bincike ta atomatik, shaidar juriya na aiki, bayanan yarda, da gudanar da samfuri suna buƙatar samar da su a matsayin ɓangare na aiwatarwa, ba a sake gina su don masu binciken daga baya ba.

### 5. Tattalin Arzikin Sashe da Ƙimar Abokin Ciniki

Kowane shiri yana buƙatar shaida na ƙimar kasuwanci. Kuɗi-kowane-biya, kuɗi-kowane-yanke shawara, kuɗi-kowane-bincike, ruwa da aka adana, gyaran hannu da aka guje wa, asarar zamba da aka rage, da ɗauka ta abokin ciniki ya kamata su ƙayyade yanke shawara na faɗaɗawa.

## Abin da Wannan Ke Nufi Bisa Nau'in Banki

### Bankunan Duniya

Bankunan duniya ya kamata su ƙirƙira tsari a matakin dandamali ta yadda kowace kasuwa, hanya, alama, da damar AI ba za ta zama samfurin aiki dabam ba.

### Bankunan Yanki

Bankunan yanki ya kamata su mai da hankali kan amfani inda amincewa, ilimin kasuwa na gida, da haɗawa mai sauƙi suka fi sikeli: ganin baitulmali, hana zamba, biyan kuɗi na Open Banking, da sabis na kuɗi na dijital da aka tsara.

### Fintechs da PSPs

Fintechs ya kamata su rage rikitarwa ga bankuna maimakon ƙara wata hanya keɓaɓɓe. Mafi kyawun shawarwari za su kawo tsari, shaidar bin doka, ko hankalin bayanai.

### Masu Kula da Baitulmali na Kamfani

Masu kula da baitulmali su buƙaci ingantawa masu aunawa: ƙananan gyare-gyare na biya, ingantacciyar ganin ruwa, bayanan sasantawa masu wadata, sasantawa mai sauri, da iko mai ƙarfi kan yanke shawara ta atomatik.

## Kammalawa

DORA, EU AI Act, da Ikon Mallakar Bayanai a ƙarshe tambayar gine-gine ce. Cibiyoyin da za su yi nasara ba za su zama waɗanda ke da mafi yawan gwaje-gwaje ko harshe mafi ƙarfi na kirkire-kirkire ba. Za su zama cibiyoyin da suka haɗa tafiyar ayyukan abokin ciniki, ingancin bayanai, tsara hanyoyi, bin doka da aka saka, da tattalin arzikin sashe zuwa samfurin aiki mai daidaituwa.

## Tambayoyin da Akan Yi Akai-akai

**Me yasa wannan batu yake da gaggawa a 2026?**

Saboda ababen more rayuwa, dokoki, da siginar buƙatun abokin ciniki sun haɗu. Abin da ya kasance gwaji na zaɓi yanzu ya zama ɓangare na samfurin aikin banki.

**Mene ne mafi girman haɗarin aiwatarwa?**

Mafi girman haɗari shi ne wargajewa: ƙungiyoyi daban suna gina gwaje-gwaje daban, kowannensu da bayanai, sarrafawa, gudanarwa, da tattalin arziki dabam.

**Mene ne banki ya kamata ya fara ginawa?**

Banki ya kamata ya fara da aikin da ke da ƙimar aunawa, kamar sasantawa mai sauri, raguwar kuɗin sasantawa, ƙananan bincike, ingantacciyar hana zamba, ko ingantacciyar ganin ruwa.

**Yaya ya kamata a auna nasara?**

Ya kamata a auna nasara ta tattalin arzikin sashe, shaidar juriya, ingancin bayanai, ɗaukar abokin ciniki, raguwar haɗarin aiki, da ingantawar ruwa ko jari na aiki.
