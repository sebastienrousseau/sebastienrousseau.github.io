# CIB Mai Aiki Koyaushe: Cyber Recovery, Fallback Rails da Treasury Mai Aminci ga Quantum

A 03:14 UTC ranar Talata, wani CIB na matsayi na ɗaya ya ga haɗin RTGS na farko ya ɓace. Abin da ya jawo shi ba yanke fibre ba ne. Fashewar ransomware ne a cikin ɓangare na uku na ICT wanda ke gudanar da ƙofar musayar kasuwarsa. A cikin mintina shida, hasumiyar kula da biyan kuɗi ta banki tana aiwatar da triage protocol: ƙarar commercial mai ƙasa da USD 500k tana kwararowa zuwa FedNow, SEPA Instant da RTP; kwararar retail na GBP tana riƙe a rufin Faster Payments; kwararar wholesale mai darajar gaske tana jira don ƙofar RTGS na sakandare — CHIPS don USD, adaftan contingency na CHAPS don GBP, mai shiga na backup na T2 don EUR. Failover ɗin ba madubi ne na cikakke ba. Tsananin sake fifikon liquidity ne na algorithmic wanda ke girmama rufin ƙimar hanyar sadarwa mai tsananin yayin da yake ci gaba da buɗe banki. Treasury yana tabbatar da cewa an rufe vault na cyber recovery, batches na pacs.008 na ranar suna sake kunna su cikin sauƙi zuwa fallback rail, kuma dashboard na hukumar yana motsawa daga kore zuwa rawaya — ba ja ba ko kaɗan. Ƙirar tana zuwa kai tsaye daga [littafin contingency-rails na 2026](https://tradetreasurypayments.com/articles/automation-contingency-rails-iso-20022-and-stablecoins-the-2026-trends-reshaping-corporate-finance-and-b2b-payments "Automation, contingency rails, ISO 20022 and stablecoins — the 2026 trends reshaping corporate finance and B2B payments") wanda bankunan wholesale yanzu suna ɗauka a matsayin tushe maimakon manufa.

Batun yana da sauƙi. CIB mai aiki koyaushe ba magana ta tallace-tallace ba ce. Samfuri ne na aiki da aka tsara, mai aunawa, kuma an tilasta shi ta cryptography.

## 01. Daga DR zuwa "aiki koyaushe" — ƙirar DORA Article 5/6

An ƙare disaster recovery a matsayin ra'ayin tsarawa. [DORA Article 5](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32022R2554 "Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 — Digital Operational Resilience Act") yana sanya mulkin haɗarin ICT a kan jikin gudanarwa a matsayin aiki da ba za a iya wakilta ba. [DORA Article 6](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32022R2554 "Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 — Article 6 ICT risk-management framework") sannan yana buƙatar tsarin sarrafa haɗarin ICT da aka tattara wanda ya rufe ganowa, amsawa, dawowa da koyo. Karatu tare da babban birnin haɗarin aiki na [Basel III](https://www.bis.org/bcbs/publ/d424.htm "Basel III: finalising post-crisis reforms") da tsarin [SM&CR](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/prudential-regulation/authorisations/senior-managers-regime-approvals "Senior Managers and Certification Regime") na Burtaniya, saƙon ga hukumomin CIB kai tsaye ne. Manufofin lokacin maidowa da manufofin maidowar bayanai dole ne a bayyana su a cikin minutoci, a tabbatar da su a ƙarƙashin gwajin rayuwa, kuma a haɗa su da manyan manajoji da aka ambata.

Canjin harshe yana da mahimmanci. "Maido da sabis" yana ɗauka cewa sabis ya tsaya. Aiki koyaushe yana ɗauka cewa an gano lalacewa, an tsaftace ta kuma an zaga ta ba tare da tsayar da kwararar mai ciniki ba. Wannan shine ma'aunin da [tsammanin UK PRA SS1/21 "Operational Resilience"](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/prudential-regulation/publication/2021/march/operational-resilience-impact-tolerances-for-important-business-services-ss "PRA SS1/21 Operational resilience") da DORA tare suke tilastawa, kuma shine kawai ma'aunin da treasury na CIB na 2026 zai iya yiwa abokin cinikin Fortune 100 corporate tallace-tallace.

## 02. FHE, QKD da PQC a matsayin primitives na juriya — ba kawai sarrafa sirri ba

Cryptography yanzu wani ɓangare ne na tarin juriya, ba aikin tsaro daban ba. Primitives uku ne ke da mahimmanci.

**FHE** yana ba banki damar ƙididdige akan matsayin treasury da aka ɓoye a cikin vault na cyber recovery ba tare da fallasa plaintext ba. Lokacin da yanayin samarwa ke da shakku, nazari, sasantawa da bincike kafin ciniki na iya ci gaba a kan kwafin ɓoye. [Takaddar BIS "Project Leap: quantum-proofing the financial system"](https://www.bis.org/publ/bppdf/bispap158.htm "BIS Papers No 158 — Project Leap") yana yin shari'ar aiki kai tsaye — sarrafa sirri da sarrafa juriya suna haɗuwa a kan primitives iri ɗaya.

**QKD** yana ba da rarraba maɓallin information-theoretic tsakanin cibiyoyin bayanai masu ɗauke da nauyin aiki koyaushe. Ba maye gurbin PQC ba ne. Sashi ne mai dacewa don ƴan haɗin da inda tabbacin musayar maɓalli na zahiri ya cancanci kuɗin. Takaddar marubucin ta baya — [QKD a cikin wholesale banking: inda maɓallai na physics-grade ke biyan kuɗi a zahiri](https://sebastienrousseau.com/2026-04-18-qkd-wholesale-banking-physics-grade-keys-2026 "QKD a cikin wholesale banking") — yana saita iyaka.

**PQC**, musamman [FIPS 203](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/FIPS/NIST.FIPS.203.pdf "FIPS 203 — Module-Lattice-Based Key-Encapsulation Mechanism") da [FIPS 204](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/FIPS/NIST.FIPS.204.pdf "FIPS 204 — Module-Lattice-Based Digital Signature Standard"), yanzu yana sa hannu a manifests na fallback-rail, snapshots na vault na cyber recovery da sarƙoƙin amincin inter-domain tsakanin masu farko da masu shiga na contingency. CIB na 2026 wanda ya sa hannu a artefacts na failover tare da classical RSA yana ba da rahoton bincike ga mai sarrafawa. [Takarda na FHE a nazarin banki](https://sebastienrousseau.com/2026-05-29-fhe-banking-analytics-confidential-compute-2026 "FHE a nazarin banki") ya yi jayayya iri ɗaya a yankin nazari — hujjar juriya tana faɗaɗa shi cikin sauƙi zuwa dawowa.

## 03. Ƙirar fallback rail design patterns — ISO 20022 a fadin RTGS, sauri, tokenised da hanyoyin sadarwa na waje

Fallback rail ba spreadsheet na lambobin tuntuɓar ba ne. Madadin hanya ce mai zagaye, an gwada ta, ta asalin ISO 20022 tare da nasa liquidity, nasa masu shiga da nasa dress-rehearsed cutover.

Hannan huɗu yanzu suna mamaye zanen CIB.

- **Sa hannu RTGS biyu.** Kwararar USD wacce ta saba tafiya akan Fedwire tana riƙe da haɗin sakandare mai dumi — CHIPS don commercial mai darajar gaske, ko correspondent bank mai haɗin Fedwire mai zaman kansa. GBP yana riƙe da CHAPS tare da tsarin mai shiga-na contingency na Bank of England. EUR yana riƙe da T2 tare da yarjejeniyar mai shiga kai tsaye ta backup ta hanyar ɓangare na uku na ICT daban.
- **Maye gurbin instant-rail.** Inda kwararar corporate ke jurewa ƙarancin rufin kowane ma'amala, SEPA Instant, FedNow da RTP suna ɗaukar biyan kuɗi yayin da aka rufe rail mai darajar gaske. Hasumiyar kula da treasury tana amfani da tafiya mai ƙarfi na value-band — duk abin da ke cikin rufin instant-rail yana tafiya nan take; duk sauran yana jira don RTGS resumption. Mafi mahimmanci, injin manufofin yana ƙin gwaji na raba biyan kuɗi mai darajar gaske zuwa ƙananan ɓangarori na instant don kaucewa rufin ƙima: tsara canja wurin wholesale na USD 2 m zuwa biyan kuɗi na instant goma na USD 200 k yana tafiyar da kowane ƙararrawar smurfing a cikin bututun AML na bankin mai karɓa kuma yana mai da abin da ya faru na aiki zuwa na laifin kuɗi. Amsa daidai ita ce a riƙe biyan kuɗi mai darajar gaske lafiya don RTGS resumption, ba a ba shi ta hanyar da za a yi masa alama yayin da ya zo ba.
- **Hanyoyin sadarwa na sasantawa na tokenised.** Tsare-tsaren wholesale CBDC, hanyoyin sadarwa na stablecoin masu sarrafawa da dandamali na tokenised-deposit yanzu suna cikin iyaka a matsayin fallback na uku don wajibai na interbank. Ba daidai suke ba da finality na sasantawar RTGS, amma suna sayen sa'o'i, kuma sa'o'i shi ne abin da cyber recovery ke buƙata.
- **Bypass na hanyar sadarwa na waje.** Lokacin da nasa ɓangare na uku na ICT na banki shi ne mahaɗin gazawa, saƙonni na ISO 20022 pacs.008 da pacs.009 suna tafiya ta correspondent da aka yarda kafin lokaci wanda ke da haɗin kai mai zaman kansa. Haɗarin tarawa a cikin mai siyar ICT guda shi ne mai kashe shiru; wannan hanyar tana cire shi.

Kuɗin shiru na wannan gine-gine shi ne fragmentation na liquidity. Kowane ma'auni da aka riga aka biya a mai shiga na RTGS na sakandare, kowane asusun backup na correspondent mai dumi, da kowane matsayin sasantawa na tokenised da aka riga aka shirya — duk babbar jari ne wadda ba ta haifar da riba. Ƙalubalen injiniya na 2026 ba kawai rubuta logic na tafiya na ISO 20022 ba ne; haɗa sweeps na liquidity na intraday ne wanda ke ba da kuɗin fallback rail just-in-time — yana janyowa daga kayan aikin repo na intraday na babban banki, tafkin liquidity na rukunin iyaye, ko layin kuɗi na contingent da aka yi alkawari ta hanyar kwangila — don kar banki ya biya kuɗin damar lamba tara-lamba don bala'in da bai faru ba tukuna. Juriya ba tare da daidaita kuɗin intraday ba shi ne babbar jari da aka kama tare da lakabin bin doka.

ISO 20022 shine abin da ke sa wannan aiki a matsayin gine-gine maimakon improvisation. Payload iri ɗaya na `pacs.008`, block iri ɗaya na `<RmtInf><Strd>`, `EndToEndId` iri ɗaya, a kan rail daban. Dandalin treasury yana tabbatarwa akan schema guda kuma yana barin layin tafiya ya zaɓi rail.

## 04. SLA na treasury da bayar da rahoton hukumar — ma'auni masu ƙididdiga na juriya

Hukumomi yanzu suna tambayar tambayoyi biyar kuma suna sa ran amsoshin lambobi.

1. **Menene RTO kowace kuɗi?** USD, GBP, EUR, JPY mai darajar gaske: minutoci, ba sa'o'i ba. Hanyoyin sauri: dakika.
2. **Menene RPO kowace kuɗi?** Yawan snapshot na vault na cyber recovery, ana bayyana shi a cikin minutoci na asarar darajar tattalin arziki a mafi munin lokacin fashewa.
3. **Menene rufin liquidity na fallback-rail?** Ma'aunin da aka riga aka biya a masu shiga na sakandare, an ƙididdige su don ɗaukar fitar da sa'o'i 24 na farko a kan ƙarar ranar mafi girma.
4. **Menene rufin sa hannu na PQC akan artefacts na dawowa?** Kashi na snapshots na vault, manifests da inter-domain trust anchors da aka sa hannu a ƙarƙashin FIPS 203 / FIPS 204.
5. **Menene Cost of Contingency Capital (CoCC)?** Kuɗin damar yau da kullum na liquidity na intraday da aka kama a cikin asusun share na sakandare, ma'aunin correspondent masu dumi da matsayin tokenised da aka riga aka shirya, ana auna shi da rate na dare. Hukumar dole ne ta ga ainihin farashin inshorar juriya na banki, kuma kwamitin aiki dole ne ya kare cinikin tsakanin babbar jari da aka kama da haƙurin fitar da hidima — ana sabunta shi aƙalla kowace kwata.

Waɗannan su ne ma'aunai da suka taswira cikin sauƙi zuwa shaidar DORA Article 6, zuwa maganganun nauyi na manyan manajoji na SM&CR, da kuma mulkin haɗarin model na [SR 11-7](https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/sr1107.htm "SR 11-7 — Guidance on Model Risk Management") akan logic na tafiya wanda ke yanke shawarar wane rail ya yi nasara. Hukumar ba ta buƙatar ba da labari ba; tana buƙatar zane na kwata-kwata tare da ƙasa mai tsayi.

## Kammalawa

Juriya na CIB a 2026 tsarin aiki ne, ba shirin dawowa ba. Vaults na cyber recovery suna rufe bayanai. FHE, QKD da PQC suna tilasta amincin a kan hanyar failover. ISO 20022 fallback rails suna ɗaukar kwararar a fadin RTGS, sauri, tokenised da hanyoyin sadarwa na waje. SLA na treasury suna ba da rahoton sakamako a cikin minutoci waɗanda hukumar za ta iya kare wa mai sarrafawa safiyar Litinin.

Aikin yana da kankare. Lissafi ɓangarorin ICT na uku akan kowane rail na biya. Saita vault na cyber recovery tare da snapshots na PQC-signed. Tattauna sa hannun RTGS na sakandare da maye gurbin instant-rail. Haɗa shawarwarin tafiya ta schema guda na ISO 20022. Gwada cutover a ƙarƙashin nauyin rayuwa, kowace kwata, tare da hukumar tana kallo.

Aiki koyaushe ba taken ba ne. Lamba ce a kan dashboard, mai sa hannu daga babban manaja, tabbatacce daga mai sarrafawa, kuma an gina shi a kan cryptography wanda ya tsira a ranar da mai gaba mai iya quantum ya bayyana.
