What this estimator does not tell you
  • Performance. ML-KEM keygen / encaps / decaps are faster than RSA on most CPUs and roughly comparable to ECDH; ML-DSA verify is fast, sign is slower. Use a benchmark on your target hardware, not size as a proxy.
  • Migration risk. Hybrid (classical + PQC) is what most operators are deploying first; this tool models the PQC half only. The hybrid wire-format adds the classical key/signature alongside, doubling the bytes during the transition window.
  • Side-channel posture. Both ML-KEM and ML-DSA require constant-time implementations to be safe; check the library you intend to use.
