# sebastienrousseau.github.io 0.0.44

## Highlights ⭐️

* **`/api/pdf/*` answers 410 Gone**: Search Console reported 679 pages as "Server error (5xx)", all under `/api/pdf/`. The lang-router Worker now answers the retired route with 410 and `noindex`, so crawlers drop the URLs instead of retrying.
* **Three new articles**: treasury automation (17 November), JevAct agent-action verification (18 November) and NIST IR 8547 quantum-vulnerable signatures (19 November), each in 34 locales.
* **Survey posts move up**: the three Bank of England and FCA AI survey posts now run on 20, 21 and 22 November, and the drafts that held those dates move to 28 November, 30 November and 1 December.
