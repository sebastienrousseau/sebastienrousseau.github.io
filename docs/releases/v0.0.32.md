# sebastienrousseau.github.io 0.0.32

## Highlights ⭐️

* **Every article links its pillar hub**: the cluster block at the end of each article now names the article's editorial pillar and links that pillar's hub in the reader's own locale. Indexed articles linking their pillar hub: 0 to 1,320.
* **No internal link lands on a redirect stub**: links to retired URLs now point at their final target, translated into the page's locale where the article exists. Indexed articles linking a stub: 39 to 0; linking another locale's article: 10 to 0.
* **Complete Article structured data**: the `TechArticle` / `ScholarlyArticle` node on every article now carries the same `datePublished`, `dateModified` and `image` as its `BlogPosting`. Articles with an incomplete Article node: 1,320 to 0.
* **About links its evidence**: the repositories and the EPAA paper it names now link to the repositories and the PDF.
