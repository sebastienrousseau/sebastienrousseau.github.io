# sebastienrousseau.github.io 0.0.22

## Highlights ⭐️

* **Homepage redesign ([#496])**: A credential band replaces the hero lead, the six institution logos return, three production repositories become proof cards, the three domain pillars become cards, and the new structure is translated into all 34 non-English locales.
* **Homepage meets the theme gallery's gates ([#500])**: Tap targets of at least 44 px, credential links that no longer overlap when they wrap, focused buttons the next section no longer covers, AAA contrast on the closing eyebrow, and a homepage of 13.8 KB gzipped that fits the first TCP window.
* **English marked as English on translated pages ([#500])**: ssg 0.0.69 dropped the `<div lang="en">` wrapper that declared every body on 646 locale pages English. A postbuild pass now marks only the text that is still English with `lang="en"` (WCAG 3.1.2).
* **Patched CI tooling ([#501])**: zod 4.6.7 in the visual, pa11y and Lighthouse tools, undici 6.29.1 and body-parser 1.20.9, clearing Dependabot alerts #44 to #49.
