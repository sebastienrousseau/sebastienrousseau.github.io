# sebastienrousseau.github.io 0.0.26

## Highlights ⭐️

* **Ruff 0.17.2 locked ([#507])**: Dependabot's `ruff` 0.17.2 update is paired with a regenerated `requirements-dev.lock` so CI installs the version `requirements-dev.txt` declares and `test_requirements_locks_agree` passes.
* **GitHub Actions updated ([#508])**: `actions/setup-python` moves from 7.0.0 to 7.1.0 across seven workflows and `github/codeql-action` moves from 4.39.0 to 4.39.1 in the CodeQL and Scorecard workflows.
* **CI tooling advisories patched**: `msgpack` moves from 1.2.1 to 1.2.3 in `requirements-dev.lock` (CVE-2026-114613) and `qs` moves from 6.16.0 to 6.17.0 in `.github/ci-tools/lighthouse/` (GHSA-6pq8-jhm2-2vcw), keeping `pip-audit` and `npm audit` at zero vulnerabilities.
