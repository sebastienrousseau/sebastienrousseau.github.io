# sebastienrousseau.github.io 0.0.25

## Highlights ⭐️

* **A steady first view on desktop**: the home page no longer moves when its "Read as" control appears; the control's space is held from the first paint, which takes the desktop layout shift from 0.0032 to 0.
* **An accessibility check that passes at any hour**: the hero clock's digit drums now hold two labels each instead of every value, so the minute hand's layout box stays at its badge and no longer covers the headline, where axe could not measure its contrast at some times of day.
* **Renamed locale URLs keep working**: `translate_post.py --rederive-slugs` now redirects a live locale URL it renames to follow a translated title, as ADR-0012 requires, so translating the published backlog keeps every inbound link.
* **Two translation defects now fail the build**: a numbered list whose markers were localised into native digits (it renders as one paragraph, as 10 bn, mr and fa posts did) and a link to an English article that never existed (384 links in 169 posts served a 404). Both are fixed, and the pipeline-artefacts and URL-integrity gates now reject them.
