# sebastienrousseau.github.io 0.0.30

## Highlights ⭐️

* **SEO recovery after the 11 Sep 2026 demotion**: English plus 10 locales stay indexable and the other 24 go `noindex, follow`, so `sitemap.xml` drops from 7,679 to 2,423 URLs. Renamed locale URLs answer one 301 from the Worker instead of a meta-refresh page, HTML is cached at the Cloudflare edge for 10 minutes, every sitemap URL carries its real `<lastmod>`, and titles and descriptions are unique across indexable pages.
